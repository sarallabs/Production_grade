# Multiple Select Questions

### Question 1

**Question:** In evaluating rock permeability and lithological weathering rates, which statements are correct? (Select all that apply)

- **A)** Permeability is the most critical single lithological factor governing weathering rates.
- **B)** Porous coarse-textured Sandstone weathers more rapidly than dense Basalt.
- **C)** Polymineralic Basalt weathers more rapidly than massive crystalline Granite.
- **D)** Coarse gravel deposits weather faster than fine volcanic ash because gravels have larger pores.
- **E)** Fine volcanic ash weathers rapidly due to massive internal porosity and surface area.

**Correct Answers:** A, B, C, E

**Explanation / Rationale:** Permeability controls weathering; Sandstone > Basalt > Granite in decay rate; volcanic ash weathers faster than gravels. Statement D is false because water flows between rather than through gravels. (Key Takeaway: Lithological permeability and grain porosity govern weathering velocity.)

### Question 2

**Question:** What quantitative thermal expansion relationships exist among rock-forming minerals and rock types? (Select all that apply)

- **A)** Cubical expansion of Quartz is precisely twice (2.0x) that of Feldspars.
- **B)** Linear expansion of Granite is half (0.5x) that of Sandstone.
- **C)** Dark-colored ferromagnesian minerals absorb heat faster than light-colored minerals.
- **D)** Feldspar cubical expansion is four times greater than quartz.
- **E)** Differential mineral expansion produces internal microfractures along crystal boundaries.

**Correct Answers:** A, B, C, E

**Explanation / Rationale:** Quartz cubical expansion = 2x Feldspar; Granite linear = 0.5x Sandstone; dark rocks heat faster; thermal differential causes microfractures. Statement D is inverted. (Key Takeaway: Differential mineral expansion generates internal shear stresses along grain boundaries.)

### Question 3

- **B)** 30 cm/s current transports gravel.

- **D)** 9.0 m/s current transports boulders weighing several tonnes.

**Explanation / Rationale:** All 4 fluvial velocity thresholds (15 cm/s, 30 cm/s, 1.2 m/s, 9.0 m/s) and the headwater disintegration gradient are verified physical principles. (Key Takeaway: Fluvial sediment carrying power scales precisely with current velocity.)

### Question 4

**Question:** What physical and quantitative facts characterize Frost Wedging (Cryofracturing)? (Select all that apply)

- **A)** Liquid water expands by precisely 9 percent (9%) upon freezing into ice.
- **B)** Confined freezing water generates a lateral bursting pressure of 1465 Mg m⁻² (1465 tonnes/m²).
- **C)** It is most effective in alpine and temperate regions with frequent diurnal freeze-thaw cycles.
- **D)** Ice contraction upon freezing pulls rock joint walls together.
- **E)** Frost wedging generates extensive scree and talus deposits at cliff bases.

**Correct Answers:** A, B, C, E

**Explanation / Rationale:** Water expands 9% upon freezing, exerting 1465 Mg m⁻² bursting pressure in alpine freeze-thaw zones, creating talus slopes. Ice expands rather than contracts. (Key Takeaway: Confined 9% ice expansion generates 1465 Mg m⁻² bursting pressure in freeze-thaw environments.)

### Question 6

**Question:** How do living organisms drive chemical decomposition of minerals in Biological Weathering? (Select all that apply)

- **A)** Plant roots secrete low-molecular-weight organic acids (citric, oxalic, malic).
- **B)** Root-secreted organic chelating ligands bind and extract metal cations (Fe³⁺, Al³⁺, Ca²⁺) from silicate lattices.
- **C)** Microbial respiration elevates soil pore CO₂ partial pressure, forming carbonic acid (H₂CO₃).
- **D)** Subterranean termites and ants secrete biochemical formic acid (HCOOH).
- **E)** Living plant roots absorb pure solid granite crystals directly into leaf tissue.

**Correct Answers:** A, B, C, D

**Explanation / Rationale:** Root acids, chelating ligands, microbial carbonic acid, and termite/ant formic acid drive chemical mineral decomposition. Plants cannot absorb solid rock crystals. (Key Takeaway: Biological chemical decomposition is driven by root exudates, microbial carbonic acid, and insect formic acid.)

### Question 8

**Question:** In the context of soil genesis, how does the weathering feedback loop operate? (Select all that apply)

- **A)** Physical disintegration fragments rock, expanding total specific surface area.
- **B)** Expanded surface area accelerates chemical hydrolysis and dissolution kinetics.
- **C)** Released nutrient cations support biological colonization by pioneer plants and microbes.
- **D)** Biological acid secretion further accelerates chemical and mechanical breakdown.
- **E)** The feedback loop causes all soil to vanish into deep space.

**Correct Answers:** A, B, C, D

**Explanation / Rationale:** Physical fragmentation -> surface area expansion -> chemical hydrolysis -> nutrient release -> biological colonization -> further breakdown forms the self-reinforcing feedback loop. (Key Takeaway: Weathering processes form a self-reinforcing feedback cycle driving soil genesis.)

### Question 9

**Question:** Which of the following are essential soil plant nutrients released through primary mineral weathering? (Select all that apply)

- **A)** Potassium (K⁺) from orthoclase feldspar and micas
- **B)** Calcium (Ca²⁺) and Magnesium (Mg²⁺) from plagioclase, pyroxenes, and amphiboles
- **C)** Phosphorus (as orthophosphate, H₂PO₄⁻) from apatite
- **D)** Pure radioactive plutonium (Pu²³⁹)

**Correct Answers:** A, B, C

**Explanation / Rationale:** Weathering of primary silicates and apatite supplies essential plant nutrients (K, Ca, Mg, P). Plutonium is a synthetic transuranic element. (Key Takeaway: Primary mineral weathering is the original natural source of essential plant nutrients.)

### Question 10

**Question:** What geomorphic and lithological outcomes result from Exfoliation on crystalline rock outcrops? (Select all that apply)

- **A)** Formation of rounded, dome-shaped inselbergs and exfoliation domes
- **B)** Concentric peeling of curved rock sheets parallel to the surface
- **C)** Accumulation of curved spalled rock fragments at the base of slopes
- **D)** Instantaneous transmutation of basalt into pure limestone

**Correct Answers:** A, B, C

**Explanation / Rationale:** Exfoliation produces rounded domes, curved spalling sheets, and basal talus debris in massive granite and basalt. Transmutation into limestone is impossible. (Key Takeaway: Exfoliation shapes rounded granite/basalt domes and spalls curved rock sheets.)
