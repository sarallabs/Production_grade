# Multiple Select Questions

### Question 1

**Question:** Which of the following are recognized as the three fundamental processes of rock weathering in nature? (Select all that apply)

- **A)** Physical (Mechanical) Weathering
- **B)** Chemical Weathering (Decomposition)
- **C)** Biological Weathering (Living Agents)
- **D)** Tectonic Plate Subduction

**Correct Answers:** A, B, C

**Explanation / Rationale:** Weathering operates via physical, chemical, and biological processes. Plate subduction is a deep crustal tectonic process. (Key Takeaway: The three fundamental weathering processes are physical, chemical, and biological.)

### Question 2

**Question:** Which of the following are primary agents of Physical (Mechanical) Weathering? (Select all that apply)

- **A)** Diurnal temperature fluctuations
- **B)** Freezing water (Frost wedging)
- **C)** Flowing water hydraulic forces
- **D)** Wind-driven sandblasting
- **E)** Atmospheric lightning discharges

**Correct Answers:** A, B, C, D, E (All options correct)

**Explanation / Rationale:** Temperature cycling, frost wedging, hydraulic forces, wind abrasion, and lightning are all recognized physical weathering agents. (Key Takeaway: Physical weathering agents encompass temperature, frost, water, wind, and lightning.)

### Question 3

**Question:** What secondary minerals are synthesized during the weathering of primary rock minerals? (Select all that apply)

- **A)** Silicate clay minerals (Kaolinite, Smectite)
- **B)** Hydrous iron and aluminum oxides (Gibbsite, Goethite)
- **C)** Secondary carbonates (Calcite)
- **D)** High-temperature unweathered magmatic olivine

**Correct Answers:** A, B, C

**Explanation / Rationale:** Clays, hydrous oxides, and secondary carbonates are secondary minerals synthesized during weathering. Magmatic olivine is an unstable primary mineral. (Key Takeaway: Secondary minerals include clays, sesquioxides, and carbonates.)

### Question 4

**Question:** Which organisms act as primary biological agents of rock weathering? (Select all that apply)

- **A)** Higher plants and trees
- **B)** Burrowing rodents and earthworms
- **C)** Subterranean termites and ants
- **D)** Rhizosphere microorganisms (bacteria, fungi)
- **E)** Deep-sea hydrothermal vent tube worms living on ocean ridges

**Correct Answers:** A, B, C, D

**Explanation / Rationale:** Plants, burrowers, termites/ants, and soil microbes are active terrestrial weathering agents. Hydrothermal vent worms do not participate in terrestrial soil genesis. (Key Takeaway: Terrestrial biological weathering is driven by flora, fauna, insects, and soil microbes.)

### Question 5

**Question:** What mechanical changes are brought about by Physical Weathering? (Select all that apply)

- **A)** Cracking and fracturing of rock boulders
- **B)** Crumbling and crushing into gravel and sand
- **C)** Reduction in clast dimensions
- **D)** Alteration of silicate molecular chemical formulas

**Correct Answers:** A, B, C

**Explanation / Rationale:** Physical weathering causes mechanical fracturing and size reduction without altering mineral molecular chemical formulas. (Key Takeaway: Physical weathering causes mechanical size reduction with zero chemical formula alteration.)

### Question 6

**Question:** Which of the following characteristics accurately describe Regolith? (Select all that apply)

- **A)** It is an unconsolidated mantle of weathered rock debris
- **B)** It rests above solid unaltered parent bedrock
- **C)** It is formed through the combined physical and chemical breakdown of rocks
- **D)** It consists of pure refined synthetic metal

**Correct Answers:** A, B, C

**Explanation / Rationale:** Regolith is the unconsolidated mantle of weathered rock residue resting above solid bedrock. It contains natural minerals, not synthetic metal. (Key Takeaway: Regolith is the unconsolidated weathered debris layer above parent bedrock.)

### Question 7

**Question:** Which physical forces enable flowing river water to disintegrate bedrock? (Select all that apply)

- **A)** Hydraulic impact and collision of moving water
- **B)** Abrasive scouring by suspended gravel and sand bedload
- **C)** Grinding of tumbling boulders along the riverbed
- **D)** Complete dissolution of pure quartz crystals in seconds

**Correct Answers:** A, B, C

**Explanation / Rationale:** Hydraulic collision, suspended sand abrasion, and tumbling boulder grinding disintegrate river channels. Quartz does not dissolve instantly. (Key Takeaway: Hydraulic impact and sediment abrasion are the primary fluvial disintegration mechanisms.)

### Question 8

**Question:** Which of the following statements about Exfoliation (Onion Weathering) are scientifically accurate? (Select all that apply)

- **A)** Outer rock sheets peel away from the parent monolith
- **B)** It is driven by differential thermal expansion between heated outer surfaces and cooler inner rock layers
- **C)** It is common in massive Basalt and Granite rock outcrops
- **D)** It occurs exclusively under permanent glacial ice sheets

**Correct Answers:** A, B, C

**Explanation / Rationale:** Exfoliation is thermal spalling of outer curved rock sheets in massive granite and basalt outcrops. It is not a subglacial process. (Key Takeaway: Thermal stress drives concentric exfoliation in massive granite and basalt.)

### Question 9

**Question:** What roles do plant roots play in transforming rock into soil? (Select all that apply)

- **A)** Mechanical root wedging into microscopic rock joints
- **B)** Creating deep macropore channels for water percolation
- **C)** Secreting biochemical organic acids that dissolve minerals
- **D)** Preventing all chemical weathering from occurring

**Correct Answers:** A, B, C

**Explanation / Rationale:** Plant roots wedge joints open, create percolation macropores, and secrete corrosive organic acids. (Key Takeaway: Plant roots execute mechanical wedging, create percolation channels, and exude organic acids.)

### Question 10

**Question:** Which living organisms are responsible for Pedoturbation (biological soil mixing)? (Select all that apply)

- **A)** Subterranean termites
- **B)** Burrowing ants
- **C)** Earthworms
- **D)** Burrowing rodents

**Correct Answers:** A, B, C, D (All options correct)
