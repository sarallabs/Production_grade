# Multiple Choice Questions

### Question 1

**Question:** What geological term describes the unconsolidated mantle of weathered rock debris resting above unaltered parent bedrock?

- **A)** Pedon
- **B)** Regolith
- **C)** Solum
- **D)** Humus

**Correct Answer:** B) Regolith

**Explanation / Rationale:** Regolith is the unconsolidated residue of weathered rock resting on the Earth's surface above solid bedrock. (Key Takeaway: Regolith is the unconsolidated mantle of weathered rock material.)

---

### Question 2

**Question:** Which fundamental process of weathering brings about mechanical breakdown into smaller particles without changing chemical composition?

- **A)** Chemical Weathering
- **B)** Physical Weathering
- **C)** Biological Decomposition
- **D)** Hydrolysis

**Correct Answer:** B) Physical Weathering

**Explanation / Rationale:** Physical weathering causes mechanical disintegration such as cracking and crushing with zero chemical composition change. (Key Takeaway: Physical weathering alters physical geometry only without changing mineral chemistry.)

---

### Question 3

**Question:** Among all physical weathering agents, which agent exerts the most pronounced and widespread disintegrating effect on Earth?

- **A)** Atmospheric Electricity
- **B)** Action of Water
- **C)** Glacial Abrasion
- **D)** Wind Sandblasting

**Correct Answer:** B) Action of Water

**Explanation / Rationale:** Water is the pre-eminent physical weathering agent, acting continuously as a disintegrating, transporting, and abrasive medium. (Key Takeaway: Water is the most active and widespread physical weathering agent.)

### Question 4

**Question:** By what percentage does liquid water expand in volume upon freezing into crystalline ice?

- **A)** 3 percent
- **B)** 6 percent
- **C)** 9 percent
- **D)** 15 percent

**Correct Answer:** C) 9 percent

**Explanation / Rationale:** Water expands by precisely 9 percent in volume when freezing into crystalline ice. (Key Takeaway: Phase transition to ice increases water volume by 9 percent.)

### Question 5

**Question:** What geomorphic term describes the concentric peeling away of outer curved rock shells from parent bedrock due to thermal stresses?

- **A)** Carbonation
- **B)** Exfoliation
- **C)** Hydration
- **D)** Pedoturbation

**Correct Answer:** B) Exfoliation

**Explanation / Rationale:** Exfoliation (onion-skin weathering) is the peeling of outer curved rock sheets caused by differential expansion between heated surfaces and cool inner rock layers. (Key Takeaway: Exfoliation peels concentric outer shells from parent rock.)

### Question 6

**Question:** In which Indian geographic regions are classic wind-sculpted Mushroom Rocks prominently observed?

- **A)** Western Ghats of Kerala
- **B)** Aravalis and Thar Desert of Rajasthan
- **C)** Sundarbans Delta of Bengal
- **D)** Himalayan Foothills of Uttarakhand

**Correct Answer:** B) Aravalis and Thar Desert of Rajasthan

**Explanation / Rationale:** Mushroom Rocks sculpted by differential eolian sandblasting are prominent in the Aravalis and Thar Desert of Rajasthan. (Key Takeaway: Eolian sandblasting creates mushroom rocks in arid desert regions.)

### Question 7

**Question:** What mechanical action is exerted by growing plant roots penetrating microscopic rock joints?

- **A)** Chemical Hydrolysis
- **B)** Mechanical Root Wedging
- **C)** Differential Thermal Shock
- **D)** Eolian Saltation

**Correct Answer:** B) Mechanical Root Wedging

**Explanation / Rationale:** Expanding plant roots exert powerful radial turgor pressure that mechanically wedges rock joints apart. (Key Takeaway: Expanding root biomass acts as a mechanical wedge splitting rock joints.)

### Question 8

**Question:** What chemical acid is secreted by subterranean termites and ants that corrodes rock minerals?

- **A)** Sulfuric Acid
- **B)** Formic Acid
- **C)** Nitric Acid
- **D)** Hydrochloric Acid

**Correct Answer:** B) Formic Acid

**Explanation / Rationale:** Termites and ants secrete biochemical formic acid (HCOOH), which chemically corrodes rock-forming minerals. (Key Takeaway: Termites and ants secrete formic acid that chemically corrodes rock minerals.)

### Question 9

**Question:** What is the sediment-transporting capacity of a river current moving at a velocity of 15 cm/s?

- **A)** Multi-tonne boulders
- **B)** Heavy stones
- **C)** Gravel
- **D)** Fine sand

**Correct Answer:** D) Fine sand

**Explanation / Rationale:** A stream current moving at 15 cm/s (0.15 m/s) has the kinetic capacity to carry fine sand. (Key Takeaway: A stream current of 15 cm/s carries fine sand.)

### Question 10

**Question:** Why is weathering defined as a combination of destruction and synthesis?

- **A)** It destroys parent rocks and synthesizes artificial chemical fertilizers.
- **B)** It destroys primary rock minerals and synthesizes new secondary minerals like clays and hydrous oxides.
- **C)** It eliminates soil organisms and creates atmospheric nitrogen.
- **D)** It dissolves riverbeds and forms mountain glaciers.

**Correct Answer:** B) It destroys primary rock minerals and synthesizes new secondary minerals like clays and hydrous oxides.

**Explanation / Rationale:** Weathering destructively breaks down primary minerals and synthetically recombines released ions into secondary clays and sesquioxides. (Key Takeaway: Weathering unites destructive breakdown with secondary mineral synthesis.)
