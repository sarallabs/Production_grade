# Mindmap: Chapter 11 Metamorphosis & Diapause

```mermaid
mindmap
root("Chapter 11: Metamorphosis & Diapause")
  "1. Morphogenesis Framework [Bloom: Remember]"
    "1.1 Post-Embryonic Growth"
      "Growth, moulting, differentiation from egg to adult"
    "1.2 Four Developmental Stages"
      "Egg Stage (Embryogenesis)"
      "Immature Stage (Nymph or Larva)"
      "Transitory Stage (Pupa in Holometabola)"
      "Adult Stage (Imago - Reproduction & Dispersal)"
  "2. Types of Metamorphosis [Bloom: Understand & Apply]"
    "2.1 Ametabolous (No Metamorphosis)"
      "Primitive direct development"
      "Adults differ only in size & sexual maturity"
      "Taxa: Apterygota (Silverfish, Springtails)"
    "2.2 Hemimetabolous (Incomplete Metamorphosis)"
      "Gradual metamorphosis: Egg -> Nymph -> Adult"
      "Exopterygota (External wing pads)"
      "Nymphs share adult habitat (except aquatic naiads)"
      "Taxa: Orthoptera, Hemiptera, Odonata"
    "2.3 Holometabolous (Complete Metamorphosis)"
      "Complete metamorphosis: Egg -> Larva -> Pupa -> Adult"
      "Endopterygota (Internal wing discs)"
      "Larvae differ radically in anatomy & food from adults"
      "Accounts for ~88% of all insect species"
      "Taxa: Lepidoptera, Coleoptera, Diptera, Hymenoptera"
  "3. Immature & Pupal Classifications [Bloom: Apply & Analyze]"
    "3.1 Larval Types"
      "Eruciform: Caterpillar-like, thoracic & prolegs (Lepidoptera)"
      "Scarabaeiform: C-shaped grub, well-developed head & thoracic legs (Scarabaeidae)"
      "Campodeiform: Flattened, active predator with long legs (Neuroptera, Carabidae)"
      "Elateriform: Wireworm-like, hard cylindrical body (Elateridae)"
      "Vermiform: Maggot-like, legless, reduced head (Diptera)"
    "3.2 Pupal Types"
      "Exarate: Appendages free and unattached (Coleoptera, Hymenoptera)"
      "Obtect: Appendages glued tightly to body (Lepidoptera chrysalis)"
      "Coarctate: Enclosed inside hardened 4th/5th larval puparium (Diptera)"
      "Decticous vs Adecticous: Functional vs non-functional mandibles"
  "4. Hypermetamorphosis [Bloom: Analyze & Evaluate]"
    "4.1 Definition"
      "Complex holometabolous development with two or more distinct larval forms"
    "4.2 Specialized Instars"
      "Triungulin: Active, campodeiform 1st instar for host location"
      "Coarctate Stage: Legless, grub-like resting instar before pupation"
    "4.3 Representative Taxa"
      "Meloidae (Blister beetles)"
      "Stylopidae (Twisted-wing parasites)"
  "5. Endocrine Regulation [Bloom: Analyze & Evaluate]"
    "5.1 PTTH (Brain Hormone)"
      "Secreted by neurosecretory cells of protocerebrum"
      "Stored in corpora cardiaca; stimulates prothoracic glands"
    "5.2 Ecdysone (Moulting Hormone)"
      "Secreted by prothoracic glands"
      "Triggers apolysis, epidermal cell division, & cuticle synthesis"
    "5.3 Juvenile Hormone (JH)"
      "Secreted by corpora allata"
      "High titer maintains larval status; low titer permits pupal/adult differentiation"
  "6. Diapause & Dormancy [Bloom: Evaluate & Create]"
    "6.1 Core Concepts"
      "State of arrested development & low metabolic rate"
      "Quiescence (immediate stress response) vs Diapause (programmed arrest)"
    "6.2 Diapause Types"
      "Obligatory: Genetically fixed in every generation (Univoltine)"
      "Facultative: Triggered by environmental token stimuli (Multivoltine)"
    "6.3 Seasonal Variations"
      "Hibernation: Winter diapause (Cold response)"
      "Aestivation: Summer diapause (Heat & drought response)"
    "6.4 Stage Specificity & Examples"
      "Embryonic Diapause: Bombyx mori (mid-embryonic)"
      "Larval Diapause: Omphisa fuscidentalis (Bamboo borer)"
      "Pupal Diapause: Pieris brassicae (Cabbage white)"
      "Imaginal Diapause: Leptinotarsa decemlineata (Colorado potato beetle)"
```
