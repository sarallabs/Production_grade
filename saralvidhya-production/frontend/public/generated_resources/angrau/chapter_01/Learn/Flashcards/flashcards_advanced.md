**Depth Level:** L-4

**Q:** What electrogenic membrane mechanism enables caterpillar goblet cells to elevate midgut luminal pH up to 11?  
**A:** Apical V-type H+-ATPase pumps generate a protonmotive force that drives K+/H+ antiporters to transport K+ into the lumen, driving pH upward to solubilize leaf tannins.

**Depth Level:** L-4

**Difficulty:** Hard  

**Q:** Why does an alkaline midgut pH prevent foliar tannin toxicity in phytophagous Lepidoptera caterpillars?  
**A:** Alkaline pH dissociates hydrogen bonds between plant tannins and dietary proteins, preventing protein-tannin precipitation and enabling enzymatic digestion.

**Q:** Detail the 4-step molecular mode of action of Bacillus thuringiensis (Bt) Cry protoxins in target insect midguts.  
**A:** 1. Solubilization in alkaline lumen → 2. Proteolytic cleavage into active toxin → 3. Binding to Cadherin & APN microvillar receptors → 4. Oligomeric pore formation causing enterocyte lysis.
