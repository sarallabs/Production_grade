**Question:** Which of the following statements regarding the Peritrophic Membrane (PM) are accurate? (Select ALL that apply)
- [ ] A) Type I PM is delaminated from midgut epithelial cells throughout the midgut length.
- [ ] B) Type II PM is synthesized exclusively by an anterior ring organ called the cardia.
- [ ] C) PM acts as a protective barrier preventing pathogen entrance and microvillar damage.
- [ ] D) PM is shed along with the outer body cuticle during ecdysis.

<details><summary>Click to view Answer</summary>Correct Options: A, B, C<br>Rationale: Type I and Type II PM secretion mechanisms are distinct and protect microvilli. PM is non-cuticular and is not shed during ecdysis.</details>
