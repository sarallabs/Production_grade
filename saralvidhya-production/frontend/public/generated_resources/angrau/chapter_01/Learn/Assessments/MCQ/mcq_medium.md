**Difficulty:** Medium  

**Question:** What primary physiological advantage does a filter chamber provide to sap-sucking insects (Hemiptera)?
- A) It secretes cellulase to break down plant cell walls.
- B) It shunts excess water directly to the hindgut to prevent digestive enzyme dilution.
- C) It stores excess blood during rapid feeding pulses.
