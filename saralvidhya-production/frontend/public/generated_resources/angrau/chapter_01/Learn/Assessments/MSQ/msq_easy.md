# Question 1
**Mindmap Location:** Chapter 13 Digestive System > 1. Three Primary Regions  
**Depth Level:** L-1  
**Bloom's Taxonomy:** Remembering  
**Difficulty:** Easy  

**Question:** Which of the following regions of the insect alimentary canal are ectodermal in embryonic origin? (Select ALL that apply)
- [ ] A) Foregut (Stomodaeum)
- [ ] B) Midgut (Mesenteron)
- [ ] C) Hindgut (Proctodaeum)
- [ ] D) Gastric Caeca

<details><summary>Click to view Answer</summary>Correct Options: A, C<br>Rationale: Both the foregut and hindgut develop from ectodermal invaginations (stomodaeum and proctodaeum) and possess a cuticular intima. Midgut and gastric caeca are endodermal.</details>
