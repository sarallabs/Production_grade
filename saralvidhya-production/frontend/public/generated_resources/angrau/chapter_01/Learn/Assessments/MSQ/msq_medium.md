# Question 1
**Mindmap Location:** Chapter 13 Digestive System > 1. Three Primary Regions > 1.2 Midgut (Mesenteron)  
**Depth Level:** L-4  
**Bloom's Taxonomy:** Analyzing  
**Difficulty:** Medium  

**Question:** Which cell types are present in the midgut epithelium of insects? (Select ALL that apply)
- [ ] A) Columnar cells (Enterocytes)
- [ ] B) Regenerative cells (Stem cells)
- [ ] C) Calciform cells (Goblet cells)
- [ ] D) Rectal papillae cells

<details><summary>Click to view Answer</summary>Correct Options: A, B, C<br>Rationale: Midgut epithelium consists of columnar cells (secretion/absorption), regenerative cells (tissue replacement), and calciform goblet cells (ion regulation). Rectal papillae are in the hindgut.</details>

**Depth Level:** L-3  
**Bloom's Taxonomy:** Applying  
**Difficulty:** Medium  

**Question:** Which physiological mechanisms prevent enzyme dilution or digestive failure in specialized feeders? (Select ALL that apply)
- [ ] A) Filter chamber bypass in sap-sucking Hemiptera
- [ ] B) Symbiotic protozoa fermentation in termite hindgut paunch
- [ ] C) Cuticular intima lining inside the midgut
- [ ] D) Peritrophic membrane compartmentalization of digestive enzymes

<details><summary>Click to view Answer</summary>Correct Options: A, B, D<br>Rationale: Filter chambers shunt excess fluid, hindgut symbionts break down cellulose, and the peritrophic membrane compartmentalizes enzymes. The midgut never has a cuticular intima.</details>

**Question:** Which structures contribute to excretory processing and osmoregulation at or within the hindgut? (Select ALL that apply)
- [ ] A) Malpighian tubules
- [ ] B) Rectal pads (papillae)
- [ ] C) Gastric caeca
- [ ] D) Ileum and colon

<details><summary>Click to view Answer</summary>Correct Options: A, B, D<br>Rationale: Malpighian tubules filter uric acid into the gut lumen, ileum/colon modify filtrate, and rectal pads actively reabsorb water and electrolytes. Gastric caeca belong to the midgut.</details>
