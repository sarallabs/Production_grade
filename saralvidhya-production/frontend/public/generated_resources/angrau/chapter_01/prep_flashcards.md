### Card 1
**Mindmap Location:** Chapter 13 Digestive System > 1. Three Primary Regions  
**Depth Level:** L-1  
**Bloom's Taxonomy:** Remembering  
**Difficulty:** Easy  

**Q:** How does embryonic origin partition the gut into ecdysed (cuticular) vs non-ecdysed (absorptive) functional zones?  
**A:** Ectodermal foregut and hindgut secrete a cuticular intima shed during moulting; Endodermal midgut lacks cuticle, enabling enzyme secretion and nutrient absorption.
