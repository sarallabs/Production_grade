# Entomology: Chapter 13 - Flashcards (Beginner Level)

### Card 1
**Mindmap Location:** Chapter 13 Digestive System > 1. Three Primary Regions  
**Depth Level:** L-1  
**Bloom's Taxonomy:** Remembering  
**Difficulty:** Easy  

**Q:** What basic architectural design describes the insect alimentary canal from mouth to anus?  
**A:** A compartmentalized "tube-within-a-tube" system running through the body cavity (hemocoel).  

![Insect Digestive System Blueprint](../Mindmaps/Digestive%20System%20Infographics/1.Three%20Primary%20Regions/Insect_Digestive_System_Blueprint.png)

### Card 2
**Mindmap Location:** Chapter 13 Digestive System > 1. Three Primary Regions  
**Depth Level:** L-1  
**Bloom's Taxonomy:** Remembering  
**Difficulty:** Easy  

**Q:** What are the names of the three primary anatomical regions of the insect digestive tract?  
**A:** The Foregut (Stomodaeum), Midgut (Mesenteron), and Hindgut (Proctodaeum).  

![Anatomy of the Insect Gut](../Mindmaps/Digestive%20System%20Infographics/1.Three%20Primary%20Regions/1.%20Foregut%20%28Stomodaem%29/Leaf%20Nodes/1.%20Anatomy_of_the_Insect_Gut.png)

### Card 4
**Mindmap Location:** Chapter 13 Digestive System > 1. Three Primary Regions > 1.1 Foregut (Stomodaeum)  
**Depth Level:** L-2  
**Bloom's Taxonomy:** Understanding  
**Difficulty:** Easy  

**Q:** What happens to the protective cuticular intima of the foregut when an insect moults?  
**A:** It is shed (ecdysed) along with the outer body exoskeleton during each moult.

**Mindmap Location:** Chapter 13 Digestive System > 1. Three Primary Regions > 1.2 Midgut (Mesenteron)  
**Depth Level:** L-2  
**Bloom's Taxonomy:** Understanding  
**Difficulty:** Easy  

**Q:** Why does the insect midgut lack a cuticular intima lining?  
**A:** To permit active digestive enzyme secretion and direct absorption of nutrients into the body.
