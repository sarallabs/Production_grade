So, imagine tackling a massive software engineering challenge. If you try to write the entire program in a single breath, it quickly spirals out of control. Computational thinking gives engineers a systematic compass: decompose the challenge into atomic units, locate recurring patterns, abstract away distracting details, and formulate a verifiable, robust algorithm.
