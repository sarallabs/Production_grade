# Study Plan: DBMS UNIT - I (Database System Concepts, ER Model & Relational Design)

## 1. 5-Day Standard Undergraduate Track (60 Mins / Day)

### Day 1: Module 1 — Database Fundamentals & Three-Schema Architecture
- **Mindmaps (L0-L1)** (10 mins) — Macro architectural orientation.
- **Key Takeaways (Topics 1–4)** (15 mins) — File systems vs. DBMS, 3-level abstraction, data independence.
- **Video Script (Part 1)** (20 mins) — Conceptual engine deep-dive.
- **Easy MCQ Quiz** (15 mins) — Formative baseline assessment.

### Day 2: Module 2 — Conceptual Database Design & ER Modeling
- **Full Mindmap (Module 2)** (10 mins) — Notation, simple, composite, multivalued, derived attributes.
- **Key Takeaways (Topics 6–7)** (15 mins) — Cardinality ratios (1:1, 1:N, M:N) and participation constraints.
- **Question Bank v2 (Q4, Q5, Q6)** (25 mins) — University enterprise schema design practice.
- **Medium MCQ Quiz** (10 mins) — Disambiguation drill.

### Day 3: Module 3 — Advanced ER & EER Specialization
- **Key Takeaways (Topics 8–9)** (15 mins) — Weak entities, identifying relationships, specialization constraints.
- **Question Bank v2 (Q7, Q8)** (25 mins) — Discriminator and disjointness synthesis.
- **Hard MCQ Quiz** (10 mins) — Edge-case testing.
- **Match The Following** (10 mins) — Notation and relational mapping alignment.

### Day 4: Modules 4 & 5 — Relational Foundations & Algorithmic Translation
- **Key Takeaways (Topics 10–11)** (15 mins) — Integrity constraints, domain rules, cascade actions.
- **Question Bank v2 (Q10, Q11, Q12, Q13)** (30 mins) — Step-by-step ER-to-table reduction drills.
- **Medium MSQ Quiz** (15 mins) — Multi-select constraint validation.

### Day 5: Module 6 & Final Exam Simulation
- **Key Takeaways (Topic 12)** (10 mins) — Relational algebra, view updatability rules.
- **PYQ Suite (Selected 5M & 10M)** (20 mins) — Decadal high-frequency past exam questions.
- **Preparation Exam (Pre-Final Exam)** (30 mins) — Timed written model exam practice.
