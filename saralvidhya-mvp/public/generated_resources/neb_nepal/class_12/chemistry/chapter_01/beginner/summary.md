In chemistry, "Volumetric Analysis" is a way to find out "how much" of a substance is in a liquid. You slowly add a special liquid whose strength you already know to your mystery liquid. You watch very carefully for a sudden colour change. This change tells you exactly when the reaction is finished. By measuring how much of the special liquid you used, you can figure out how much of the mystery substance was there! It's like a clever measuring game for liquids.
