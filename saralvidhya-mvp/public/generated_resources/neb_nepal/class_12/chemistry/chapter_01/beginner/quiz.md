**Question 1**
What is the primary focus of volumetric analysis?
A. Measuring the weight of substances
B. Identifying unknown elements
C. Measuring the volumes of solutions involved in chemical reactions
D. Separating mixtures

Correct Answer: C.
Explanation: The text states, "The volumetric analysis deals with measurements of the volumes of solutions involved in the chemical reactions..."

**Question 2**
In volumetric analysis, what is a solution of accurately known concentration called?
A. Unknown solution
B. Dilute solution
C. Standard solution
D. Concentrated solution

Correct Answer: C.
Explanation: According to the text, "The solution of accurately known strength is called the standard solution."

**Question 3**
The process of adding a standard solution to a solution of unknown strength until the reaction is just complete is known as what?
A. Filtration
B. Evaporation
C. Titration
D. Distillation

Correct Answer: C.
Explanation: The text defines this process: "The process of adding the standard solution to the solution of unknown strength until the reaction is just complete is known as titration."

**Question 4**
What is typically used to detect the completion of a titration by a sharp colour change?
A. A thermometer

Correct Answer: C.
Explanation: The text mentions, "The sharp clour change is produced... by the addition of an auxiliary reagent, called indicator."

**Question 5**
Which of the following is NOT listed as a type of volumetric analysis?
A. Acidimetry and alkalimetry
B. Oxidation and reduction titration
C. Gravimetric analysis
D. Precipitation titration

Correct Answer: C.
Explanation: Gravimetric analysis is mentioned as a *branch* of quantitative analysis, alongside volumetric analysis, but not as a *type* of volumetric analysis itself. The text lists "Acidimetry and alkalimetry," "Oxidation and reduction titration," and "Precipitation titration" as types of volumetric analysis.
