### 1. Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs)
*(Choose the correct option. 1 Mark each)*

1.  Which type of chemical analysis involves the detection and identification of the constituents of an inorganic substance?
    a) Quantitative analysis
    b) Volumetric analysis
    c) Qualitative analysis
    d) Gravimetric analysis

2.  Quantitative analysis is divided into two main branches. One is volumetric analysis, and the other is:
    a) Spectroscopic analysis
    b) Gravimetric analysis
    c) Chromatographic analysis
    d) Titrimetric analysis

3.  In volumetric analysis, a solution of accurately known concentration is called a:
    a) Unknown solution
    b) Dilute solution
    c) Concentrated solution
    d) Standard solution

4.  The reagent of known concentration used in volumetric analysis is known as the:
    a) Titrate
    b) Indicator
    c) Titrant
    d) Analyte

5.  The process of adding a standard solution to the solution of unknown strength until the reaction is just complete is known as:
    a) Dilution
    b) Filtration
    c) Titration
    d) Evaporation

6.  The point at which the indicator gives a clear visual change in a titration is called the:
    a) Equivalence point
    b) Start point
    c) End point
    d) Reaction point

7.  Which of the following is an advantage of volumetric analysis?
    a) It involves laborious operations.
    b) It is slow.
    c) It is rapid and more accurate.
    d) It cannot be applied to all reactions.

8.  Acidimetry and alkalimetry is a type of volumetric analysis also known as:
    a) Redox titration
    b) Neutralization titration
    c) Precipitation titration
    d) Complexometric titration

9.  In the titration of oxalic acid against KMnO₄ solution in acidic medium, KMnO₄ acts as a:
    a) Reducing agent
    b) Oxidizing agent
    c) Self-indicator
    d) Catalyst

10. Titrations involving AgNO₃ solution are commonly known as:
    a) Redox titrations
    b) Neutralization titrations
    c) Argentometric titrations
    d) Iodometric titrations
