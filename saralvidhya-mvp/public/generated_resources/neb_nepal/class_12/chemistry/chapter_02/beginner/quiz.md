Question 1
What are the positively charged ions called?
A. Anions
B. Cations
C. Molecules
D. Electrolytes

Correct Answer: B
Explanation: In chemistry, positively charged ions formed when an electrolyte dissolves in water are called cations. Negatively charged ions are called anions.

Question 2
When an electrolyte like AB dissolves in water and forms A⁺ and B⁻ ions, what is this process called?
A. Neutralization
B. Combustion
C. Ionization (or Dissociation)
D. Evaporation

Correct Answer: C
Explanation: When an electrolyte breaks down into ions in a solution, it is called ionization or dissociation.

Question 3
According to Arrhenius theory, what is the condition for a solution to conduct electricity?
A. It must contain solid particles.
B. It must contain ions.
C. It must be hot.
D. It must be colorless.

Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Only substances that form ions when dissolved in a solution can conduct electricity because these ions are free to move and carry the electric charge.

Question 4
If a solution is less concentrated, what happens to the ionization of the electrolyte?
A. It decreases.
B. It stays the same.
C. It increases.
D. It stops.

Correct Answer: C
Explanation: The degree of ionization increases as the concentration of the solution decreases (on dilution). This is because there are more solvent molecules available to interact with the solute molecules.

Question 5
What is the term for the fraction of the total number of molecules present as free ions in a solution?
A. Ionization constant
B. Dielectric constant
C. Degree of ionization
D. Equilibrium constant

Correct Answer: C
Explanation: The degree of ionization (often represented by 'a') tells us what proportion of the dissolved electrolyte has actually split into ions.
