### Section 1: Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs)

**Instructions:** Choose the correct option for each question.

1.  Solutions that allow electric current to pass through them are called:
    a) Non-electrolytic solutions
    b) Electrolytic solutions
    c) Pure water
    d) Insulated solutions

2.  Positively charged ions are called:
    a) Anions
    b) Neutrons
    c) Cations
    d) Protons

3.  Negatively charged ions are called:
    a) Cations
    b) Anions
    c) Electrons
    d) Ions

4.  The Arrhenius theory explains the properties of:
    a) Non-electrolytes
    b) Electrolytes
    c) Metals
    d) Gases

5.  When an electrolyte dissolves in water, it breaks down into:
    a) Molecules only
    b) Atoms only
    c) Ions
    d) Neutrons

6.  In an electrolytic solution, there is an equilibrium between ions and:
    a) Solid particles
    b) Undissociated molecules
    c) Gases
    d) Pure solvent

7.  The movement of ions towards oppositely charged electrodes is responsible for the flow of:
    a) Light
    b) Heat
    c) Electric current
    d) Sound

8.  The fraction of the total number of molecules present as free ions is known as:
    a) Ionization constant
    b) Concentration
    c) Degree of ionization
    d) Dielectric constant

9.  Strong electrolytes are ______ ionized.
    a) Partially
    b) Almost completely
    c) Not
    d) Slightly

10. Ostwald's Dilution Law applies to the equilibrium between ions and:
    a) Undissociated molecules
    b) Solvent molecules
    c) Solid electrolyte
    d) Air
