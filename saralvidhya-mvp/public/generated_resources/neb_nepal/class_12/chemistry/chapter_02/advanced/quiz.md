Question 4
A buffer solution is prepared by mixing 100 mL of 0.1 M acetic acid ($\text{CH}_3\text{COOH}$) with 50 mL of 0.1 M sodium acetate ($\text{CH}_3\text{COONa}$). If the $\text{p}K_a$ of acetic acid is 4.74, what is the $\text{pH}$ of the buffer solution?

A. 4.74
B. 4.44
C. 5.04
D. 4.14

Correct Answer: B
Explanation:
This is a buffer solution containing a weak acid ($\text{CH}_3\text{COOH}$) and its conjugate base ($\text{CH}_3\text{COO}^-$ from $\text{CH}_3\text{COONa}$).
We can use the Henderson-Hasselbalch equation:
$\text{pH} = \text{p}K_a + \log \left( \frac{[\text{conjugate base}]}{[\text{weak acid}]} \right)$

First, calculate the moles of the acid and conjugate base.
Moles of $\text{CH}_3\text{COOH} = \text{Volume} \times \text{Concentration} = 0.1 \text{ L} \times 0.1 \text{ mol/L} = 0.01 \text{ mol}$.
Moles of $\text{CH}_3\text{COONa}$ (which dissociates completely to $\text{CH}_3\text{COO}^-$) $= 0.05 \text{ L} \times 0.1 \text{ mol/L} = 0.005 \text{ mol}$.

The total volume of the buffer solution is $100 \text{ mL} + 50 \text{ mL} = 150 \text{ mL} = 0.15 \text{ L}$.

Now calculate the concentrations of the weak acid and conjugate base in the final buffer solution:
$[\text{CH}_3\text{COOH}] = $\frac{\text{moles of acid}}{\text{total volume}} = $\frac{0.01 \text{ mol}}{0.15 \text{ L}} = $\frac{1}{15} \text{ M}$.
$[\text{CH}_3\text{COO}^-] = $\frac{\text{moles of base}}{\text{total volume}} = $\frac{0.005 \text{ mol}}{0.15 \text{ L}} = $\frac{0.005}{0.15} = $\frac{5}{150} = \frac{1}{30} \text{ M}$.

Now, apply the Henderson-Hasselbalch equation:
$\text{pH} = \text{p}K_a + \log \left( \frac{[\text{CH}_3\text{COO}^-]}{[\text{CH}_3\text{COOH}]} \right)$
$\text{pH} = 4.74 + \log \left( \frac{1/30}{1/15} \right)$
$\text{pH} = 4.74 + \log \left( \frac{1}{30} \times \frac{15}{1} \right)$
$\text{pH} = 4.74 + \log \left( \frac{15}{30} \right)$
$\text{pH} = 4.74 + \log \left( \frac{1}{2} \right)$
$\text{pH} = 4.74 + \log (0.5)$
$\text{pH} = 4.74 - 0.3010$
$\text{pH} = 4.439$

Rounding to two decimal places, the pH is 4.44.
