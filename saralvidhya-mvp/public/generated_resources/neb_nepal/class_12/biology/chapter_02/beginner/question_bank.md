## Section B: Very Short Answer Questions (1 mark each)

**11.** Name the father of plant physiology.
**Answer:** Stephan Hales

**12.** What is the full form of DPD?
**Answer:** Diffusion Pressure Deficit

**13.** What happens to a cell placed in a hypotonic solution?
**Answer:** Water enters the cell by endosmosis and the cell becomes turgid (swollen).

**14.** Name the special pores through which guttation occurs.
**Answer:** Hydathodes

**15.** At what time of day does guttation mainly occur?
**Answer:** At night or early morning

## Section C: Fill in the Blanks

**16.** The process by which the protoplast of a cell shrinks away from the cell wall is called __________.
**Answer:** Plasmolysis

**17.** The force that causes water to move from lower concentration to higher concentration is called __________.
**Answer:** Osmotic pressure / Suction pressure

**18.** In monocot plants, the guard cells are __________ shaped.
**Answer:** Dumb-bell

**19.** The upward movement of water from roots to leaves is called __________.
**Answer:** Ascent of sap

**20.** __________ transpiration occurs through tiny openings in the bark of trees.
**Answer:** Lenticular
