### Section 1: Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs)

**Instructions:** Choose the correct option for each question.

1.  Who is known as the "Father of Plant Anatomy"?
    a) C. Nageli
    b) N. Grew
    c) Hanstein
    d) Schmidt

2.  What is the study of the internal structure of plants called?
    a) Plant Physiology
    b) Plant Morphology
    c) Plant Anatomy
    d) Plant Ecology

3.  A group of cells with a common origin that performs a specific function is called a:
    a) Cell
    b) Tissue
    c) Organ
    d) Organelle

4.  Depending on their ability to divide, plant tissues are classified into two main types. What are they?
    a) Primary and Secondary
    b) Apical and Lateral
    c) Meristematic and Permanent
    d) Protoderm and Procambium

5.  Which type of tissue is characterized by its ability to divide?
    a) Permanent tissue
    b) Meristematic tissue
    c) Epidermal tissue
    d) Vascular tissue

6.  The term "meristem" is derived from a Greek word meaning:
    a) Permanent
    b) Divisible
    c) Growing
    d) Organized

7.  Where are apical meristems usually found?
    a) In the middle of stems and roots
    b) At the tips of roots and shoots
    c) Along the sides of stems and roots
    d) Inside the leaves

8.  Which meristem is responsible for increasing the length of the plant?
    a) Lateral meristem
    b) Intercalary meristem
    c) Secondary meristem
    d) Permanent meristem

9.  Which meristem is responsible for increasing the thickness of the plant?
    a) Apical meristem
    b) Intercalary meristem
    c) Lateral meristem
    d) Primary meristem

10. The root apex is protected by a structure called the:
    a) Shoot apex
    b) Root cap
    c) Vascular cambium
    d) Epidermis
