Question 1
What is the main function of meristematic tissue in plants?
A. To store food
B. To absorb water
C. To divide and produce new cells
D. To provide structural support

Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Meristematic tissues are characterized by their ability to divide, which allows plants to grow and produce new cells.

Question 2
Which type of meristem is found at the tips of roots and shoots, responsible for increasing the length of the plant?
A. Lateral Meristem
B. Intercalary Meristem
C. Apical Meristem
D. Secondary Meristem

Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Apical meristems are located at the apex (tip) of roots and shoots and are responsible for the primary growth, which increases the plant's length.

Question 3
The study of the internal structure of plants is called:
A. Plant Physiology
B. Plant Ecology
C. Plant Anatomy
D. Plant Genetics

Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Plant anatomy is the branch of biology that deals with the study of the internal structure of plants.

Question 4
Tissues that have lost their capacity to divide and have taken up specific functions are known as:
A. Meristematic Tissues
B. Permanent Tissues
C. Vascular Tissues
D. Epidermal Tissues

Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Permanent tissues are formed when meristematic cells differentiate and mature, losing their ability to divide but gaining specialized functions.

Question 5
Where is intercalary meristem typically found in plants?
A. At the tip of the root
B. Along the sides of the stem for thickening
C. At the base of leaves or internodes
D. In the central core of the stem

Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Intercalary meristems are found in specific regions, like at the base of leaves or internodes (the part of the stem between two nodes), and contribute to growth in length in those areas.
