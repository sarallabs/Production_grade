## Q1. Describe the types and functions of Central Nervous System (CNS) neuroglial cells.

**Type:** Describe  |  **Bloom's:** Understand  |  **Difficulty:** Medium
**Mindmap Path:** `UNIT 6: ANIMAL TISSUE > Nervous Tissue > Neuroglia`
**Recommended Diagram:** CNS Neuroglia Cell Subtypes Diagram

### 📝 2-Mark Short Answer
CNS neuroglia include Astrocytes (Blood-Brain Barrier formation), Oligodendrocytes (CNS myelin sheath synthesis), Microglia (phagocytic immune defense), and Ependymal cells (ciliated CSF circulation).

### 📜 5-Mark Detailed Answer
1. **Astrocytes**: Star-shaped cells whose vascular feet cover brain capillaries, forming the protective **Blood-Brain Barrier (BBB)** and regulating extracellular K+ concentration.
2. **Oligodendrocytes**: Cells with few branching processes that synthesize and maintain lipid **myelin sheaths** around multiple central nerve axons in brain and spinal cord white matter.
3. **Microglia**: Small mesoderm-derived phagocytic cells that act as resident macrophages in CNS tissue, clearing pathogens and damaged cell debris.
4. **Ependymal Cells**: Simple ciliated cuboidal cells lining brain ventricles and central canal of spinal cord; cilia beat rhythmically to circulate **Cerebrospinal Fluid (CSF)**.
5. *Summary*: CNS glia provide structural support, myelination, immune surveillance, and fluid circulation.
