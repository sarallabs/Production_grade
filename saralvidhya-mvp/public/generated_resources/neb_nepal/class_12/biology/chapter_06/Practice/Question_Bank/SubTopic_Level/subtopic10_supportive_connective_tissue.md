## Q1. Compare Cartilage and Bone in terms of matrix composition, vascularity, and cell arrangement.

**Type:** Compare  |  **Bloom's:** Analyze  |  **Difficulty:** Hard
**Mindmap Path:** `UNIT 6: ANIMAL TISSUE > Connective Tissue > Supportive Connective Tissue`
**Recommended Diagram:** Cartilage vs Bone Microscopic Structure Diagram

### 📝 2-Mark Short Answer
Cartilage has a semi-rigid avascular chondrin matrix with chondrocytes in lacunae. Bone has a rigid vascular mineralized ossein matrix with hydroxyapatite calcium salts and osteocytes in lacunae linked by canaliculi.

### 📜 5-Mark Detailed Answer
1. **Matrix Mineralization**: Cartilage matrix is composed of flexible, semi-rigid **chondrin** (chondroitin sulfate). Bone matrix is composed of rigid **ossein** impregnated with inorganic hydroxyapatite calcium phosphate crystals.
2. **Vascularity**: Cartilage is entirely **avascular** (nourished by perichondrium diffusion). Bone is heavily **vascularized** via Haversian and Volkmann canals.
3. **Cellular Architecture**: Cartilage cells (**chondrocytes**) occur in lacunae in groups of 1–4 without canaliculi. Bone cells (**osteocytes**) lie singly in lacunae interconnected by fine cytoplasmic **canaliculi**.
4. **Growth Pattern**: Cartilage exhibits both appositional and interstitial growth. Bone grows exclusively by appositional deposition.
5. **Structural Roles**: Cartilage provides flexible shock absorption at joint ends. Bone forms the primary load-bearing endoskeleton.

## Q2. Describe the microscopic architecture of an Osteon (Haversian System) in compact bone.

**Type:** Mechanism  |  **Bloom's:** Understand  |  **Difficulty:** Hard
**Mindmap Path:** `UNIT 6: ANIMAL TISSUE > Connective Tissue > Supportive Connective Tissue`
**Recommended Diagram:** Haversian System (Osteon) Compact Bone Diagram

### 📝 2-Mark Short Answer
An Osteon consists of a central Haversian canal containing blood vessels and nerves, surrounded by concentric ossein lamellae, with osteocytes housed inside lacunae interconnected by canaliculi.

### 📜 5-Mark Detailed Answer
1. **Haversian Canal**: The central longitudinal canal containing nutrient artery, vein, lymphatic vessel, and nerve fibers.
2. **Concentric Lamellae**: Concentric rings of hard ossein matrix impregnated with calcium phosphate crystals arranged around the Haversian canal.
3. **Lacunae**: Fluid-filled cavities situated between adjacent lamellae, housing mature **osteocytes**.
4. **Canaliculi**: Radiating microscopic canals housing osteocyte cytoplasmic processes, allowing nutrient and gas transport between osteocytes and the central canal.
5. **Volkmann's Canals**: Transverse or oblique canals connecting adjacent Haversian canals to each other and to the periosteum.
6. *Functional Result*: The Haversian system ensures efficient nutrient delivery to osteocytes in highly mineralized bone.
