## Q1. Describe the composition of human blood plasma and formed elements.

**Type:** Describe  |  **Bloom's:** Understand  |  **Difficulty:** Medium
**Mindmap Path:** `UNIT 6: ANIMAL TISSUE > Connective Tissue > Fluid Connective Tissue`
**Recommended Diagram:** Blood Composition and Formed Elements Diagram

### 📝 2-Mark Short Answer
Blood consists of 55% liquid Plasma (90-92% water, plasma proteins albumin, globulin, fibrinogen) and 45% Formed Elements (RBCs, Granulocyte/Agranulocyte WBCs, and Thrombocytes).

### 📜 5-Mark Detailed Answer
1. **Blood Composition**: Whole blood is a liquid connective tissue consisting of 55% extracellular **Plasma** and 45% **Formed Elements** (corpuscles).
2. **Plasma Constituents**: Contains 90–92% water and 6–8% vital plasma proteins: **Albumin** (osmotic pressure), **Globulins** (immunoglobulins/defense), and **Fibrinogen** (blood clotting).
3. **Erythrocytes (RBCs)**: Biconcave, enucleated corpuscles (5–5.5 million/mm³) packed with hemoglobin (12-16 g/dL) for gas transport; 120-day lifespan.
4. **Leucocytes (WBCs)**: Nucleated immune defense cells (6,000–8,000/mm³). Subdivided into **Granulocytes** (Neutrophils, Eosinophils, Basophils) and **Agranulocytes** (Lymphocytes B/T, Monocytes).
5. **Thrombocytes (Platelets)**: Enucleated cell fragments (150,000–350,000/mm³) derived from bone marrow megakaryocytes; essential for blood coagulation cascades.
