## Q1. Describe the general structural characteristics of epithelial tissue.

**Type:** Describe  |  **Bloom's:** Understand  |  **Difficulty:** Medium
**Mindmap Path:** `UNIT 6: ANIMAL TISSUE > Epithelial Tissue > General Features & Surfaces`
**Recommended Diagram:** General Epithelial Tissue Architecture Diagram

### 📝 2-Mark Short Answer
Epithelial tissue consists of closely packed cells with minimal intercellular matrix, rests on a non-cellular basement membrane, lacks direct blood vessels (avascular), and covers internal and external body surfaces.

### 📜 5-Mark Detailed Answer
1. **High Cellularity**: Composed almost entirely of tightly packed cells with negligible intercellular space (*substantia intercellularis*).
2. **Basement Membrane Attachment**: Basal cell surfaces rest on a specialized non-cellular **basement membrane** (basal lamina + reticular lamina).
3. **Avascularity**: Absolutely devoid of capillary blood vessels; nutrients and oxygen diffuse from underlying vascular connective tissue.
4. **Cell Junctions**: Cells are firmly connected via tight junctions, adherens belts, desmosomes, and gap junctions.
5. **High Regeneration Capacity**: Displays rapid mitotic renewal to repair surface damage caused by friction or chemical wear.
6. **Germ Layer Origins**: Uniquely derived from all three embryonic germ layers: ectoderm (skin epidermis), endoderm (GI tract lining), and mesoderm (vascular endothelium).

## Q2. Compare the structure and functional roles of tight junctions, desmosomes, and gap junctions.

**Type:** Compare  |  **Bloom's:** Analyze  |  **Difficulty:** Hard
**Mindmap Path:** `UNIT 6: ANIMAL TISSUE > Epithelial Tissue > General Features & Surfaces`
**Recommended Diagram:** Cell Junctions in Epithelial Tissue Diagram

### 📝 2-Mark Short Answer
Tight junctions form impermeable fluid seals between cells; desmosomes provide strong mechanical anchoring under shear stress; gap junctions contain connexon channels that allow direct intercellular ion and signal passage.

### 📜 5-Mark Detailed Answer
1. **Tight Junctions (Zonula Occludens)**: Transmembrane occludin and claudin proteins fuse adjacent cell membranes, preventing paracellular diffusion of fluids and solutes across epithelial barriers.
2. **Desmosomes (Macula Adherens)**: Button-like protein plaques containing cadherins linked to keratin intermediate filaments; provide high mechanical tensile strength under shearing forces.
3. **Gap Junctions (Nexus)**: Hexameric **connexon** channel complexes forming hydrophilic tunnels between cells; permit rapid movement of small ions (Ca2+, Na+) and signaling molecules (cAMP).
4. *Functional Summary*: Tight junctions create barriers, desmosomes provide structural cohesion, and gap junctions enable direct metabolic and electrical communication.
