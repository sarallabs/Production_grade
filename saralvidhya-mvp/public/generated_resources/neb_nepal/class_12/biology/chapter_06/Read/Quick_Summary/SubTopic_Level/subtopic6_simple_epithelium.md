# Quick Study — 2.2 Simple Epithelium — Single-Layered Epithelia

**MindmapPath**: `UNIT 6: ANIMAL TISSUE > Epithelial Tissue > Simple Epithelium`
**Depth Level**: `L-2 Sub-Topic`

---

## Must-Know Key Concepts

- **Single-Layer Structure**: Composed of a single layer of cells resting directly on the basement membrane; specialized for filtration, absorption, and secretion.
- **Simple Squamous Epithelium**: Thin, flat, tile-like cells. Includes **Endothelium** (endodermal origin lining blood vessels/heart) and **Mesothelium** (mesodermal origin lining body cavities).
- **Simple Cuboidal Epithelium**: Cube-shaped cells with central round nucleus. Subtypes: non-ciliated (glands), ciliated (bronchioles), and brush-bordered with microvilli (PCT of nephron).
- **Simple Columnar Epithelium**: Tall pillar-like cells with basal oval nucleus. Subtypes: non-ciliated (stomach), ciliated (Fallopian tubes), and brush-bordered (intestinal mucosa).
- **Pseudostratified Epithelium**: Single layer of column-like cells of unequal heights with basal nuclei giving a false multi-layered look; ciliated with goblet cells in upper respiratory tract.
