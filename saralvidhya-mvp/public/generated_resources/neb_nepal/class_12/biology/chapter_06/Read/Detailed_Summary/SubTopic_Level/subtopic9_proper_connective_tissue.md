### 3.1 Proper Connective Tissue — Cellular & Extracellular Architecture

**Connective Tissue Proper** forms the universal packing and binding framework of the vertebrate body. Unlike epithelium, connective tissue is characterized by widely spaced cells embedded within an abundant extracellular matrix (ECM).

#### 1. Cellular Components
- **Fibroblasts**: Most abundant stellate cells; actively synthesize extracellular ground matrix and protein fiber precursors.
- **Mast Cells**: Oval granular cells mediating inflammatory response; secrete **Histamine** (vasodilator), **Heparin** (anticoagulant), and **Serotonin** (vasoconstrictor).
- **Histiocytes (Tissue Macrophages)**: Phagocytic cells derived from blood monocytes that engulf pathogens and cellular debris.
- **Plasma Cells**: Differentiated B-lymphocytes featuring a characteristic **'cartwheel' or 'clock-face' chromatin nucleus**; actively synthesize circulating immunoglobulin antibodies.

#### 2. Extracellular Fibers
- **Collagen (White) Fibers**: Composed of protein tropocollagen organized into unbranched parallel bundles. Flexible but inextensible with immense tensile strength.
- **Elastic (Yellow) Fibers**: Composed of elastin protein; branched single fibers capable of stretching up to 150% of resting length.
- **Reticular Fibers**: Delicate, silver-staining (*argyrophilic*) branching networks of reticulin protein forming structural stromata for soft organs.

#### 3. Loose vs Dense Subtypes

| Type | Structural Organization | Key Examples & Functions |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Loose Areolar** | Loose arrangement of fibers & ground jelly | Universal packing tissue under skin and surrounding nerves/vessels |
| **Loose Adipose** | Adipocytes filled with fat droplets | **White Adipose** (unilocular droplet, energy storage) vs **Brown Adipose** (multilocular droplets + rich mitochondria, thermogenesis) |
| **Dense Regular** | Parallel collagen bundles | **Tendons** (connect muscle to bone, high tensile strength) |
| **Dense Irregular** | Meshwork collagen bundles | Dermis of skin, organ capsules |
| **Dense Elastic** | Branching elastic fibers | **Ligaments** (connect bone to bone) |
