## Must-Know Key Concepts

- **Founder of Microscopic Histology**: Italian physician Marcello Malpighi (1628–1694) is recognized as the pioneer of microscopic anatomy and histology.
- **Discovery of Capillaries**: Proved the existence of microscopic blood capillaries connecting arteries and veins in 1661, confirming William Harvey's theory of closed blood circulation.
- **Eponymous Anatomical Discoveries**: Discovered the Malpighian corpuscles (renal glomerulus and Bowman's capsule) in kidneys, the Malpighian layer (stratum basale) of skin, and Malpighian tubules in insect excretory systems.
- **Pioneered Early Light Microscopy**: Applied primitive compound light microscopes to analyze tissue microarchitecture in lungs, spleen, liver, and brain.
