# Quick Study — 3.3 Fluid Connective Tissue — Blood and Lymph

**MindmapPath**: `UNIT 6: ANIMAL TISSUE > Connective Tissue > Fluid Connective Tissue`
**Depth Level**: `L-2 Sub-Topic`

---

## Must-Know Key Concepts

- **Mobile Liquid Tissue**: Specialized fluid connective tissue lacking fibers synthesized by local cells; functions in internal transport of gases, nutrients, metabolic wastes, and immune cells.
- **Blood Composition**: Composed of **Plasma** (55% fluid matrix) and **Formed Elements / Blood Corpuscles** (45% cellular components).
- **Plasma Composition**: 90–92% water, 6–8% plasma proteins (**Albumin** for osmotic pressure, **Globulin** for immunoglobulins, **Fibrinogen** for clotting cascade).
- **Formed Elements**: **RBCs / Erythrocytes** (enucleated biconcave, hemoglobin 12-16 g/dL, 120-day lifespan), **WBCs / Leucocytes** (Granulocytes: Neutrophils, Eosinophils, Basophils; Agranulocytes: Lymphocytes B/T, Monocytes), **Platelets / Thrombocytes** (enucleated megakaryocyte fragments for blood clotting).
- **Lymph Composition**: Clear interstitial fluid collected by lymphatic vessels; contains plasma and lymphocytes but lacks RBCs, platelets, and large plasma proteins.
