# Quick Study — 5.1 Neuroglia — Non-Neuronal Glial Support Cells

**MindmapPath**: `UNIT 6: ANIMAL TISSUE > Nervous Tissue > Neuroglia`
**Depth Level**: `L-2 Sub-Topic`

---

## Must-Know Key Concepts

- **Non-Neuronal Support Cells**: Non-conducting, mitotic supportive cells outnumbering neurons 10:1 in nervous tissue.
- **CNS Neuroglia**: **Astrocytes** (Blood-Brain Barrier BBB, K+ regulation), **Oligodendrocytes** (myelin sheath in CNS), **Microglia** (phagocytic immune defense), **Ependymal Cells** (ciliated CSF circulation).
- **PNS Neuroglia**: **Schwann Cells** (myelin sheath in PNS, axon regeneration) and **Stellate/Satellite Cells** (ganglionic neuronal support).
