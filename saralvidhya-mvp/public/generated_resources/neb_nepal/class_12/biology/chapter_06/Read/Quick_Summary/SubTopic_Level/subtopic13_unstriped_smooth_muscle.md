# Quick Study — 4.2 Unstriped / Smooth Muscle

**MindmapPath**: `UNIT 6: ANIMAL TISSUE > Muscular Tissue > Unstriped / Smooth Muscle`
**Depth Level**: `L-2 Sub-Topic`

---

## Must-Know Key Concepts

- **Involuntary Visceral Distribution**: Located in walls of internal hollow visceral organs (stomach, intestines, blood vessels, ureters, uterus); controlled by autonomic nervous system.
- **Spindle-Shaped Unbranched Architecture**: Fusiform fibers with pointed tapering ends, containing a single central oval nucleus; lacking cross-striations.
- **Non-Striated Contraction**: Actin and myosin myofilaments arranged in lattice networks attached to dense bodies rather than organized sarcomeres.
- **Single-Unit vs Multi-Unit Subtypes**: **Single-unit** (fibers linked by gap junctions, contract as single syncytium in gut/uterus) vs **Multi-unit** (independently innervated fibers without gap junctions in ciliary eye muscle/arrector pili).
