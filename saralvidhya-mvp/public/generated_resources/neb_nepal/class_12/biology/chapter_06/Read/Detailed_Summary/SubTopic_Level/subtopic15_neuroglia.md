### 5.1 Neuroglia — Non-Neuronal Glial Cell Subtypes

**Neuroglia** (or Glial Cells) constitute the specialized non-neuronal supportive framework of nervous tissue. Comprising over 50% of brain volume and outnumbering neurons 10-to-1, neuroglial cells retain mitotic division capacity throughout life.

#### Central Nervous System (CNS) Glial Cells

1. **Astrocytes**: Star-shaped cells with extensive cytoplasmic processes ending in vascular feet.
   - Form the protective **Blood-Brain Barrier (BBB)** by surrounding brain capillaries.
   - Regulate extracellular \(	ext{K}^+\) concentration and uptake neurotransmitters (glutamate).
2. **Oligodendrocytes**: Cells with few branching processes.
   - Synthesize and maintain lipid-rich **myelin sheaths** around multiple central nerve axons in brain and spinal cord white matter.
3. **Microglia**: Small, elongated cells derived from embryonic mesodermal monocytes.
   - Function as resident phagocytic macrophages in CNS tissue, clearing pathogens and necrotic debris.
4. **Ependymal Cells**: Simple ciliated cuboidal or columnar epithelial cells lining cerebral brain ventricles and the spinal central canal.
   - Cilia beat rhythmically to circulate **Cerebrospinal Fluid (CSF)**.

#### Peripheral Nervous System (PNS) Glial Cells

1. **Schwann Cells (Neurolemmocytes)**: Form neurolemma and myelin sheaths around peripheral nerve fibers; facilitate PNS axon regeneration.
2. **Satellite (Stellate) Cells**: Flattened cells surrounding neuronal cell bodies inside PNS sensory and autonomic ganglia.
