## Must-Know Key Concepts

- **Coined the Term 'Histology'**: German anatomist August Franz Josef Karl Mayer coined the term **Histology** in 1801.
- **Etymological Origin**: Derived from two classical Greek roots: *histos* (meaning 'web' or 'tissue') and *logos* (meaning 'study' or 'discourse').
- **Definition of Histology**: Formally defined as the branch of microscopic anatomy concerned with the minute structure, chemical composition, and physiological function of animal and plant tissues.
- **Academic Discipline Establishment**: Mayer's terminology provided a unified academic identity to the microscopic study of tissue organization.
