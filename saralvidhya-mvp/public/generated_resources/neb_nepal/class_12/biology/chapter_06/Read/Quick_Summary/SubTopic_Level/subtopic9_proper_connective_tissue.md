# Quick Study — 3.1 Proper Connective Tissue — Cells, Matrix, and Fibers

**MindmapPath**: `UNIT 6: ANIMAL TISSUE > Connective Tissue > Proper Connective Tissue`
**Depth Level**: `L-2 Sub-Topic`

---

## Must-Know Key Concepts

- **Mesodermal Origin**: Connective tissue is derived from embryonic mesoderm, functioning to bind, support, and insulate organs.
- **Three Core Components**: Composed of **Cells**, Ground Substance (**Matrix**), and Protein **Fibers**.
- **Cell Types**: Fibroblasts (secretes matrix/fibers), Mast Cells (secretes histamine, heparin, serotonin), Histiocytes/Macrophages (phagocytosis), Lymphocytes (immune defense), and Plasma Cells (antibody synthesis with cartwheel nucleus).
- **Fiber Types**: Collagen/White fibers (unbranched, high tensile strength, tropocollagen), Elastic/Yellow fibers (branched, extensible, elastin), and Reticular fibers (branching networks, reticulin).
- **Loose vs Dense CT**: **Loose** (Areolar tissue, Adipose tissue with white/brown adipocytes) vs **Dense** (Dense Fibrous: Tendons connecting muscle-to-bone; Ligaments connecting bone-to-bone; Elastic CT; Reticular CT).
