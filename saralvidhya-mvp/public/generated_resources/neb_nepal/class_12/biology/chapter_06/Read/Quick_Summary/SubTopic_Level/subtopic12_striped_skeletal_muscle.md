# Quick Study — 4.1 Striped / Skeletal Muscle

**MindmapPath**: `UNIT 6: ANIMAL TISSUE > Muscular Tissue > Striped / Striated / Skeletal Muscle`
**Depth Level**: `L-2 Sub-Topic`

---

## Must-Know Key Concepts

- **Voluntary Skeletal Attachment**: Attached to skeletal bones via tendons; under conscious voluntary somatic nervous system control; rapid contractions, fatigues easily.
- **Multinucleated Syncytium**: Long cylindrical, unbranched fibers containing multiple peripherally located nuclei beneath the **sarcolemma**.
- **Striated Sarcomere Myofilaments**: Alternating dark **A-bands** (anisotropic, myosin thick filaments) and light **I-bands** (isotropic, actin thin filaments); functional contraction unit is the **Sarcomere** (Z-line to Z-line).
- **Red vs White Muscle Fibers**: **Red Fibers** (high myoglobin, rich mitochondria, aerobic, slow fatigue-resistant) vs **White Fibers** (low myoglobin, fast anaerobic glycogenolysis, rapid fatigue).
