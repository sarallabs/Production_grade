# Short 03: Simple Epithelium — Squamous, Cuboidal, Columnar & Pseudostratified

**Duration:** ~90 seconds | **Words:** ~230 | **Mode:** Descriptive & Spatial

---

**[HOOK]**
A single layer of cells — yet four completely different types. Simple epithelium is where one layer does everything from gas exchange to immune filtration.

> [VISUAL: A row of four simple epithelium types side-by-side — flat squamous, cube-like cuboidal, tall columnar, and mixed-height pseudostratified — each glowing as it's introduced.]

---

**[SCRIPT]**
Simple epithelium has just **one cell layer**, all resting on the basement membrane.

**I. Simple Squamous** — flat, tile-like cells with disc-shaped nuclei.
- **Endothelium** (endodermal origin): lines capillaries, endocardium, arteries, veins.
- **Mesothelium** (mesodermal origin): lines body cavities, covers viscera and mesenteries.
- Also in: Bowman's capsule (ultrafiltration), alveoli (gas exchange), loop of Henle (reabsorption).

**II. Simple Cuboidal** — equal height and width, central spherical nucleus.
- Non-ciliated: kidney tubules (reabsorption), thyroid vesicles, ovary surface, cornea.
- Ciliated: respiratory bronchioles (air filtration).
- Brush-bordered: PCT of nephron (microvilli for maximal reabsorption).

**III. Simple Columnar** — taller than wide, basal oval nucleus.
- Non-ciliated: stomach and intestine (secretion, absorption).
- Ciliated: fallopian tube and oviduct (ova movement), neurocoel (CSF circulation).
- Brush-bordered: intestinal mucosa (absorption surface).

**IV. Pseudostratified** — all cells touch the basement membrane but nuclei sit at different heights — appearing layered. Located in trachea and bronchi to trap and clear inhaled particles.
