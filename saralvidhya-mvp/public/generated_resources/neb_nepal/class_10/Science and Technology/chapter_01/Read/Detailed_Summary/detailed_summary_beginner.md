# Detailed Study Notes (Beginner) — Scientific Study

## 1. What are Variables?
In any experiment, we look at cause and effect. A **variable** is a factor or characteristic that can change.
- **Independent Variable:** The factor you change (e.g., amount of water).
- **Dependent Variable:** The factor you measure (e.g., how high the plant grows).
- **Controlled Variable:** The factors you keep the same (e.g., type of soil).

## 2. Measurement Units
- **Fundamental Units:** Basic units that do not depend on others (e.g., meter, kilogram, second).
- **Derived Units:** Units made by combining fundamental units (e.g., speed = meter/second).
