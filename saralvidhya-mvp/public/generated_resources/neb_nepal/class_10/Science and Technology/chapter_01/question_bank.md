## Concept Questions & Answers
### Q1. Differentiate between Independent and Dependent variables.
**Answer:**
- **Independent Variable:** The variable that is intentionally changed or manipulated by the experimenter (e.g., amount of sunlight).
- **Dependent Variable:** The variable that changes in response to the independent variable and is measured (e.g., height of the plant).

### Q2. Why must certain variables be controlled during an experiment?
**Answer:**
To make the findings of the experiment valid, reliable, and accurate. If other variables are not controlled, we cannot determine which factor is responsible for the observed change or effect.

### Q3. Check the validity of the equation $P = mv^2$ for power.
**Answer:**
- LHS: Power ($P$) has unit Watt ($W = kg\cdot m^2/s^3$).
- RHS: $m v^2 \rightarrow kg \times (m/s)^2 = kg\cdot m^2/s^2$.
Since LHS ($kg\cdot m^2/s^3$) is not equal to RHS ($kg\cdot m^2/s^2$), the formula is **invalid**.
