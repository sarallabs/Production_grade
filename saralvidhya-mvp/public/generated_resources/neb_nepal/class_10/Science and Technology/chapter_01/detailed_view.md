# Detailed Study Notes: Scientific Study

## 1. Scientific Research and Variables
A scientific study investigates objects, events, and physical situations in nature in a planned, organized, and logical way using empirical evidence.

The causative factors and traits that have relationship with an event or phenomenon are called **variables**.

### Classification of Variables:
1. **Independent Variable:** The factor manipulated by the researcher.
2. **Dependent Variable:** The factor that changes as a result of manipulation, measured to observe the effect.
3. **Controlled Variable:** Factors kept uniform throughout the experiment to ensure the validity of results.

### Plotting and Equations:
- The independent variable is written on the right side of equations ($s = vt$, $t$ is independent) and plotted on the **X-axis** of a graph.
- The dependent variable is written on the left side of equations ($s = vt$, $s$ is dependent) and plotted on the **Y-axis** of a graph.

## 2. Measurement Units
Physical quantities are measured in units.

### Seven Fundamental Units (SI System):
- **Length:** Meter ($m$)
- **Mass:** Kilogram ($kg$)
- **Time:** Second ($s$)
- **Temperature:** Kelvin ($K$)
- **Luminous Intensity:** Candela ($cd$)
- **Electric Current:** Ampere ($A$)
- **Amount of Substance:** Mole ($mol$)

### Derived Units:
Composed of two or more fundamental units. Examples:
- **Velocity:** $m/s$
- **Force:** $kg\cdot m/s^2$ (Newton, $N$)
- **Work/Energy:** $kg\cdot m^2/s^2$ (Joule, $J$)
- **Pressure:** $kg/(m\cdot s^2)$ (Pascal, $Pa$)

## 3. Unitwise Analysis of Physical Equations
Physical equations are valid only when the units on both sides match.

- **Example 1:** Verify $v^2 = u^2 + 2as$.
  - LHS Unit: $(ms^{-1})^2 = m^2 s^{-2}$
  - RHS Unit: $u^2 \rightarrow m^2 s^{-2}$, $2as \rightarrow (ms^{-2}) \times m = m^2 s^{-2}$
  - Since LHS and RHS units are identical, the equation is **valid**.

- **Example 2:** Verify $s = ut + \frac{1}{2}a^2t$.
  - LHS Unit: $m$
  - RHS Unit: $ut \rightarrow ms^{-1} \times s = m$, $a^2t \rightarrow (ms^{-2})^2 \times s = m^2 s^{-3}$
  - Since $m \neq m + m^2s^{-3}$, the equation is **invalid**.
