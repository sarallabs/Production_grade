# Mindmap: Scientific Study

- **Scientific Study**
  - **Research Variables**
    - *Independent Variable* (Manipulated cause, X-axis, right side of equations)
    - *Dependent Variable* (Measured effect, Y-axis, left side of equations)
    - *Controlled Variable* (Kept constant for fair test validity)
  - **Measurement Units**
    - *Fundamental Units* (7 Base Units: m, kg, s, K, A, cd, mol)
    - *Derived Units* (Combinations of base units: $m/s$, $N$, $J$, $Pa$, $W$)
  - **Equation Analysis**
    - *Validation Rule* (Units on LHS must equal units on RHS)
    - *Addition/Subtraction Rule* (Only add or subtract quantities with identical units)
