# Assessment / Quiz: Scientific Study

Test your understanding of scientific variables and SI units.

## Questions

1. **What is the SI unit of electric current?**
   - A) Volt
   - B) Ampere (Correct)
   - C) Ohm
   - D) Watt

2. **In an experiment studying how temperature affects sugar solubility, the temperature of water is the:**
   - A) Dependent variable
   - B) Controlled variable
   - C) Independent variable (Correct)
   - D) Constant variable

3. **Which of the following is a derived unit of pressure?**
   - A) Pascal (Correct)
   - B) Newton
   - C) Joule
   - D) Candela

4. **Is the equation $s = ut + \frac{1}{2}at$ valid?**
   - A) Yes
   - B) No (Correct, because the unit of $\frac{1}{2}at$ is $m/s$, while $s$ is $m$)

5. **Which of the following is NOT one of the seven fundamental units?**
   - A) Kelvin
   - B) Mole
   - C) Watt (Correct)
   - D) Candela
