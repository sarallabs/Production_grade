# Course Info: Scientific Study

## About this Chapter
This module covers the foundation of grade 10 Science and Technology. It introduces students to scientific methodology, research variables, SI units, and the validation of mathematical-physical equations.

## Prerequisites
- Basic understanding of measurement (length, mass, time).
- Basic algebraic manipulation.

## What You Will Learn
- How to design a controlled scientific experiment.
- How to correctly identify variables.
- How to work with SI base and derived units.
- How to verify the correctness of scientific formulas using unitwise analysis.
