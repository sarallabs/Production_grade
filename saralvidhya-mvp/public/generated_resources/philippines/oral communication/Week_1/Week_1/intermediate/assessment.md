### Question 1
McCornack (2014) alongside Sipacio & Balgos (2016) define communication as a process of sharing and conveying messages across which dimensions?
- [ ] A) Only through oral and acoustic airwaves
- [ ] B) Exclusively within institutional classrooms
- [x] C) Within and across channels, contexts, media, and cultures
- [ ] D) Exclusively in digital and computer-mediated environments

> **Explanation**: McCornack and Sipacio & Balgos highlight that communication occurs across diverse channels, contexts, media, and cultural backgrounds.

---

### Question 2
In the 6-step communication cycle, how does **Encoding** functionally differ from **Decoding**?
- [ ] A) Encoding is performed by the receiver, while decoding is done by the sender
- [x] B) Encoding converts cognitive ideas into symbolic codes; decoding extracts meaning from received symbols
- [ ] C) Encoding refers to channel noise, while decoding refers to feedback
- [ ] D) Encoding occurs after the feedback loop is closed

> **Explanation**: Encoding is the sender's cognitive process of converting thoughts into symbols, whereas decoding is the receiver's process of interpreting those symbols into meaning.

---

### Question 3
A public speaker notices audience members nodding in agreement and leaning forward during key points. In the transactional model, these reactions are best classified as:
- [ ] A) Semantic noise
- [ ] B) Physical interference
- [x] C) Immediate, nonverbal feedback
- [ ] D) Unintended channel distortion

> **Explanation**: Nodding and attentive posture provide real-time nonverbal feedback, allowing the speaker to gauge comprehension and engagement.

---

### Question 4
Wilbur Schramm’s Interactive Model revolutionized communication theory primarily by emphasizing which two components?
- [ ] A) Electronic transmitters and binary code
- [x] B) Overlapping Fields of Experience and circular feedback loops
- [ ] C) Hypodermic effects and passive audience acceptance
- [ ] D) Formal outlines and extemporaneous delivery

> **Explanation**: Schramm introduced the concept of the "Field of Experience" (cultural, educational, and linguistic background) and showed that communication requires overlapping fields and circular feedback.

---

### Question 5
What is the core distinction between **Intrapersonal Communication** and **Interpersonal Communication**?
- [ ] A) Intrapersonal communication involves a crowd of more than 10 people
- [x] B) Intrapersonal communication occurs within oneself (self-talk); interpersonal communication occurs between two people sharing an identifiable relationship
- [ ] C) Interpersonal communication does not produce feedback
- [ ] D) Intrapersonal communication requires electronic media

> **Explanation**: Intrapersonal communication is internal self-dialogue and cognitive processing; interpersonal communication is dyadic interaction between two individuals.

---

### Question 6
Which type of noise is created when a speaker uses specialized medical or technical jargon that non-specialist listeners cannot interpret?
- [ ] A) Environmental noise
- [ ] B) Physiological noise
- [x] C) Semantic noise
- [ ] D) Psychological noise

> **Explanation**: Semantic noise arises from linguistic barriers, confusing jargon, ambiguous terminology, or culturally unfamiliar idioms.

---

### Question 7
According to Lloyd Bitzer and Verderber et al. (2017), what is an **Exigence** in a Rhetorical Situation?
- [ ] A) The physical venue where the speech is delivered
- [x] B) An urgent need, imperfection, or crisis that can be addressed or resolved through discourse
- [ ] C) The microphone setup used during a speech
- [ ] D) The demographic profile of the audience

> **Explanation**: An exigence is a real or perceived defect, urgency, or problem that places pressure on people and can be modified through persuasive rhetoric.

---

### Question 8
Applying the 7 Cs of Effective Communication, which principle requires tailoring tone, examples, and vocabulary to the audience's background and emotional state (*"you-attitude"*)?
- [ ] A) Concreteness
- [ ] B) Conciseness
- [x] C) Consideration
- [ ] D) Correctness

> **Explanation**: Consideration focuses on understanding the receiver's mindset, education, cultural values, and needs.
