# Curated Video Resources & Academic Lecture Links: Oral Communication Week 1

---

## 📺 Recommended Educational Video Modules

This curated list maps directly to the **Oral Communication in Context (Philippine Senior High School)** learning competencies for Lesson 1: Foundations, Elements, Models, and Ethics.

---

### 1. Foundational Nature & The 6-Step Cycle (Beginner / Intermediate)
* **Topic Focus**: Defining Communication, The 6-Step Dynamic Process (Idea Generation to Feedback), Elements of Communication.
* **Curated Lecture / Reference**: *The Communication Process: Crash Course Communication & Media Studies*
  * **Core Concept**: Step-by-step breakdown of source, encoding, channel transmission, decoding, and feedback.
  * **Search Query / Resource Link**: `https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=communication+process+encoding+decoding+feedback`
  * **Estimated Duration**: 8–10 minutes
  * **Key Discussion Question**: How does the Daphne & Rico scenario illustrate that feedback can be entirely nonverbal?

---

### 2. Shannon-Weaver to Transactional Models (Intermediate / Advanced)
* **Topic Focus**: Linear Models vs. Schramm's Interactive Model (Field of Experience) vs. Barnlund's Transactional Model (Simultaneous Transceivers).
* **Curated Lecture / Reference**: *Evolution of Communication Models: Linear, Interactive, and Transactional*
  * **Core Concept**: Why the inclusion of continuous feedback and overlapping fields of experience transforms our understanding of communicative competence.
  * **Search Query / Resource Link**: `https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=shannon+weaver+schramm+transactional+model+communication`
  * **Estimated Duration**: 12–15 minutes
  * **Key Discussion Question**: Why are communicators in face-to-face dialogue categorized as simultaneous transceivers rather than alternating senders and receivers?

---

### 3. Public Speaking, Speech Anxiety & The Audience-Centered Approach
* **Topic Focus**: Beebe & Beebe's Audience-Centered Public Speaking Model, James Humes' "Language of Leadership", Coping with Speech Apprehension.
* **Curated Lecture / Reference**: *Overcoming Public Speaking Apprehension & Audience-Centered Speech Design*
  * **Core Concept**: Practical physiological and cognitive techniques to manage nervousness during speeches; shifting focus from self to audience benefit.
  * **Search Query / Resource Link**: `https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=audience+centered+public+speaking+overcome+speech+anxiety`
  * **Estimated Duration**: 10–12 minutes
  * **Key Discussion Question**: How does thorough audience analysis help reduce a speaker's communication apprehension before a formal presentation?

---

### 4. Rhetorical Situation & Communication Ethics (Advanced)
* **Topic Focus**: Lloyd Bitzer's Rhetorical Situation (Exigence, Audience, Constraints), NCA Credo for Communication Ethics, Netiquette & Cyberplagiarism.
* **Curated Lecture / Reference**: *Bitzer's Rhetorical Situation & Ethics in Public Address*
  * **Core Concept**: How an urgent exigence compels discourse; distinguishing a rhetorical audience from general onlookers; upholding truthfulness and human dignity.
  * **Search Query / Resource Link**: `https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=lloyd+bitzer+rhetorical+situation+exigence+communication+ethics`
  * **Estimated Duration**: 14–18 minutes
  * **Key Discussion Question**: In what ways does digital context collapse heighten an orator's ethical accountability under the NCA Credo?
