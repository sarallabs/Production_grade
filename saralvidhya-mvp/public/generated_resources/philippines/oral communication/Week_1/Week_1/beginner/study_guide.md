## 🔑 Key Vocabulary Checklist

| Word | Simple Meaning | Real-Life Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Speaker** | The person who starts the idea. | You telling a story to a friend. |
| **Message** | The words, feelings, or pictures you share. | "Let's study together at 4 PM." |
| **Channel** | How the message travels. | Face-to-face voice, WhatsApp, phone call. |
| **Receiver** | The person listening or reading. | Your classmate reading your text. |
| **Encoding** | Putting thoughts into words or signs. | Deciding what words to say politely. |
| **Decoding** | Understanding what the words mean. | Figuring out what your teacher is asking for. |
| **Feedback** | The listener's reaction or reply. | A thumbs-up emoji or a nod. |
| **Noise** | Anything that blocks or ruins the message. | A loud motor engine outside the room. |

## 💡 Quick Tips for Doing Your Best

> [!TIP]
> **Beat Speech Butterflies**: Take three slow breaths before speaking. Pick one friendly friend in the room and look at them when you start. Remember: everyone wants you to succeed!

> [!NOTE]
> **Netiquette Rule #1**: Never type your homework or chat replies in ALL CAPS. It makes your teachers and classmates feel like you are shouting at them.

## 📝 5-Minute Practice Activity
Think of your favorite hobby. Write down:
1. One sentence explaining it clearly (Clearness).
2. Use fewer than 15 words (Conciseness).
3. End with a polite question to your friend (Courtesy).
