# Flashcards: Foundations & Nature of Communication (Beginner Level)

---

### Card 1
**Front**: What is the everyday definition of communication?  
**Back**: Communication is sharing messages and creating understanding between people through words, tone, and body language.

---

### Card 2
**Front**: According to Verderber, Sellnow, & Verderber (2017), what is communication?  
**Back**: *"Communication is the process of creating shared meaning."*

---

### Card 3
**Front**: What does James Humes call public speaking?  
**Back**: *"The language of leadership"* — whenever you speak to a group, you are auditioning for leadership.

---

### Card 4
**Front**: What is the Audience-Centered approach in public speaking?  
**Back**: An approach where the speaker keeps the audience as the core focus of speech preparation, rehearsal, and delivery.

---

### Card 5
**Front**: What does writing in ALL CAPS mean in netiquette?  
**Back**: It is interpreted as shouting or yelling at your readers.

---

### Card 6
**Front**: What is "flaming" in online communication?  
**Back**: Sending aggressive, angry, or insulting messages to someone over the internet.

---

### Card 7
**Front**: What is plagiarism?  
**Back**: Using another person's words, ideas, or works and presenting them as your own without crediting the source.

---

### Card 8
**Front**: What are the 6 steps of the communication cycle?  
**Back**: 1. Idea Generation, 2. Encoding, 3. Transmission, 4. Reception, 5. Decoding, 6. Feedback.

---

### Card 9
**Front**: What is the difference between encoding and decoding?  
**Back**: Encoding is turning thoughts into words or symbols (by the speaker); decoding is interpreting those symbols into meaning (by the listener).

---

### Card 10
**Front**: What is feedback?  
**Back**: The verbal or nonverbal reaction the listener sends back to show whether they understood the message.

---

### Card 11
**Front**: What is physical noise? Give an example.  
**Back**: External environmental sounds that block hearing, such as loud traffic, lawn mowers, or weak internet connections.

---

### Card 12
**Front**: What is physiological noise?  
**Back**: Biological barriers inside the body like hunger, a severe headache, illness, or sleepiness.

---

### Card 13
**Front**: What is psychological noise?  
**Back**: Mental or emotional distractions such as stress, anger, anxiety, or daydreaming.

---

### Card 14
**Front**: What is semantic noise?  
**Back**: Confusion caused by unfamiliar words, difficult technical jargon, or confusing slang.

---

### Card 15
**Front**: Name the 7 Cs of effective communication.  
**Back**: Completeness, Conciseness, Consideration, Concreteness, Courtesy, Clearness, and Correctness.
