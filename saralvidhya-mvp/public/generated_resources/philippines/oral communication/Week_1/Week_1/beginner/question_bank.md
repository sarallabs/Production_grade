## 📚 Section A: Multiple-Choice Questions (MCQ)

### Question 1
What is communication?
- [ ] A) Reading a book silently in a room
- [x] B) Sharing thoughts, feelings, and messages between people to create understanding
- [ ] C) Watching a movie without talking
- [ ] D) Only speaking in a formal classroom

*Answer*: **B** | *Explanation*: Communication is the process of conveying information and creating shared understanding between people.

---

### Question 2
According to Verderber, Sellnow, and Verderber (2017), communication is the process of:
- [x] A) Creating shared meaning
- [ ] B) Writing essays
- [ ] C) Winning arguments
- [ ] D) Typing fast on a phone

*Answer*: **A** | *Explanation*: Standard definition: Communication is the process of creating shared meaning.

---

### Question 3
What did James Humes mean when he said public speaking is "the language of leadership"?
- [ ] A) Only government officials are allowed to speak in public
- [x] B) Whenever you speak to a group, you are auditioning for leadership
- [ ] C) Leaders never make mistakes when speaking
- [ ] D) Leadership only exists in companies

*Answer*: **B** | *Explanation*: Speaking well gives you the confidence to voice ideas and lead others.

### Question 4
In online netiquette, typing a message in ALL CAPS means:
- [ ] A) You are whispering
- [x] B) You are shouting or angry
- [ ] C) You have a fast internet connection
- [ ] D) You are being polite

*Answer*: **B** | *Explanation*: Capital letters in text messages and emails are considered shouting.

### Question 5
What is **Physical Noise**?
- [x] A) External environmental sounds like loud traffic, sirens, or dog barking
- [ ] B) Having a headache
- [ ] C) Feeling sleepy during a speech
- [ ] D) Not knowing what a big word means

*Answer*: **A** | *Explanation*: Physical noise is acoustic or environmental interference from the outside world.

### Question 6
What is the correct sequence of the 6-step communication cycle?
- [ ] A) Feedback -> Decoding -> Idea -> Encoding -> Reception -> Channel
- [x] B) Idea Generation -> Encoding -> Transmission -> Reception -> Decoding -> Feedback
- [ ] C) Transmission -> Feedback -> Idea -> Encoding -> Decoding -> Reception
- [ ] D) Reception -> Decoding -> Idea -> Encoding -> Transmission -> Feedback

*Answer*: **B** | *Explanation*: Communication starts with an idea, is encoded into words, transmitted across a channel, received, decoded, and followed by feedback.

### Question 7

- [ ] A) Completeness
- [x] B) Conciseness

## ✍️ Section B: Short-Answer & Conceptual Prompts

### Question 8: The Daphne & Rico Scenario
**Prompt**: In the Daphne & Rico example from Sipacio & Balgos (2016), identify:
1. What message did Daphne encode?
2. What nonverbal feedback did Rico provide?

**Model Answer**:
1. Daphne encoded the message: *"Rico, mahal kita bilang kaibigan"* (I love you as a friend), expressing that her feelings were strictly platonic.
2. Rico's nonverbal feedback was frowning, falling silent, and looking away, which signaled his disappointment and emotional heartache.

---

### Question 9: Differentiating Noise Types
**Prompt**: Match each real-life situation to its correct noise type (Physical, Physiological, Psychological, or Semantic):
1. A student feels a severe headache during a lecture.
2. A foreign tourist cannot understand local street slang.
3. Heavy rain pours loudly on a metal roof while the teacher is speaking.
4. A student is daydreaming about their weekend vacation.

**Model Answer**:
1. **Physiological Noise** (bodily pain/illness).
2. **Semantic Noise** (linguistic barrier/unfamiliar slang).
3. **Physical Noise** (environmental sound).
4. **Psychological Noise** (mental distraction/daydreaming).

---

### Question 10: Reflection on the 7 Cs
**Prompt**: Name three of the 7 Cs of Effective Communication and explain why each is helpful when giving a class presentation.

**Model Answer**:
* **Clearness**: Keeps your main goal focused and simple so classmates understand your key point immediately.
* **Concreteness**: Using real examples and facts makes your presentation interesting and believable.
* **Courteous**: Speaking with a respectful tone and polite manners helps the audience feel comfortable and happy to listen.
