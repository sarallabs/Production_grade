# Pop Quiz & Quick Assessments (Beginner Level)

---

## 📝 Practice Quiz: Foundations & Process of Communication

Test your understanding of Lesson 1! Choose the best answer for each question.

---

### Question 1
According to Verderber, Sellnow, and Verderber (2017), communication is the process of:
- [ ] A) Memorizing long speeches
- [x] B) Creating shared meaning
- [ ] C) Speaking without listening
- [ ] D) Sending messages without feedback

> **Explanation**: Verderber et al. define communication as the process of creating shared meaning between people.

---

### Question 2
Why did presidential speechwriter James Humes call public speaking "the language of leadership"?
- [ ] A) Because only company presidents are allowed to speak in public
- [x] B) Because whenever you speak to a group, you are auditioning for leadership
- [ ] C) Because leaders do not need to prepare their remarks
- [ ] D) Because public speaking requires an elected government position

> **Explanation**: Speaking clearly and persuasively lets you voice ideas, resolve issues, and inspire others to take action.

---

### Question 3
In digital etiquette (netiquette), what does typing your message in ALL CAPITAL LETTERS represent?
- [ ] A) Being extra polite
- [ ] B) Showing that you have good spelling
- [x] C) Shouting or yelling at the reader
- [ ] D) Typing very fast

> **Explanation**: In online communication, writing in ALL CAPS is widely interpreted as aggressive shouting.

---

### Question 4
Which of the following is an example of **Physiological Noise**?
- [ ] A) A lawnmower roaring outside your window
- [x] B) A pounding migraine headache that makes it hard to listen
- [ ] C) An unfamiliar technical term used in a lecture
- [ ] D) Worrying about an upcoming math test

> **Explanation**: Physiological noise comes from biological conditions inside the body, such as headaches, illness, exhaustion, or hunger.

---

### Question 5
In the 6-step communication cycle, what step occurs when the listener interprets the incoming symbols and figures out what they mean?
- [ ] A) Encoding
- [ ] B) Idea Generation
- [x] C) Decoding
- [ ] D) Transmission

> **Explanation**: Decoding is the mental process where the receiver attributes meaning to the received words, gestures, or sounds.

---

### Question 6
What is the major limitation of **Linear Models** of communication (such as the Shannon-Weaver Model)?
- [ ] A) They are too difficult to read
- [x] B) They view communication as a one-way street and omit feedback
- [ ] C) They only apply to face-to-face conversations
- [ ] D) They have too many feedback loops

> **Explanation**: Linear models depict communication as one-way conveyor belts from sender to passive receiver, failing to explain how listeners react and provide feedback.

---

### Question 7
Which of the 7 Cs of Effective Communication reminds a speaker to be polite, show respect, and cultivate positive goodwill?
- [ ] A) Conciseness
- [ ] B) Concreteness
- [x] C) Courtesy
- [ ] D) Completeness

> **Explanation**: Courtesy means showing genuine respect for the listeners' culture, values, and perspectives.
