## 🔬 Critical Theoretical Distinctions

### 1. The Dual-Level Meaning Architecture (Wood, 2012)
* **Content Level**: Transmits explicit propositional assertions (e.g., *"We must finalize the budget report by Monday"*).
* **Relationship Level**: Regulates interpersonal power, social distance, and affective valence through nonverbal inflection and posture (e.g., whether delivered with authoritative condescension or collegial partnership).

### 2. The Transceiver Paradox (Barnlund, 1970)
In transactional paradigms, communicators cannot *not* communicate. While vocalizing arguments, a speaker concurrently monitors, decodes, and adapts to the micro-facial expressions, ocular fixations, and postural shifts of their listeners. Communication is thus **constitutive and simultaneous**, invalidating turn-taking assumptions.
