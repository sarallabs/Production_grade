# Study Guide: Functions of Communication & Functional Language Models (Intermediate Level)

---

## 🎯 Modular Objectives & Senior High School Competencies
By working through this guide, you will master the following core competencies:
1. Differentiate, evaluate, and classify the 5 primary functions of communication in multi-layered oral situations.
2. Trace the historical evolution of functional language theories from extrinsic utility (Malinowski, Bühler, Jakobson) to modern linguistics.
3. Decouple and categorize speech acts across the Philippine Linguistic Landscape.
4. Diagnose interpersonal communication breakdowns and deploy assertive remediation strategies (*"I" statements*, active listening).

## 📊 Quick-Reference Comparison: Extrinsic Functional Theories

| Theorist & Year | Key Framework | Focus on Self (Speaker) | Focus on Other (Addressee) | Focus on Content (Context) | Focus on Social Bond / Channel |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Malinowski (1923)** | Anthropological Fieldwork | — | Pragmatic (Active) | Pragmatic (Narrative) | Phatic Communion; Ritual |
| **Bühler (1934)** | Organon Model | Expressive (1st Person) | Conative (2nd Person) | Representational (3rd Person) | — |
| **Jakobson (1960)** | 6 Communicative Factors | Emotive Function | Conative Function | Referential Function | Phatic; Metalingual; Poetic |
| **Britton (1970)** | Educational Development | Expressive | Transactional (Participant) | Transactional (Participant) | Poetic (Spectator) |
| **Morris (1967)** | Ethological (Naked Ape) | Mood Talking | — | Information Talking | Grooming Talking; Exploratory |

## 💡 Practical Synthesis Prompt
**Case Study**: A team manager is presiding over an urgent project meeting. She needs to address a missed deadline without alienating her staff:
* **Ineffective (Aggressive)**: *"You always ruin our timeline! None of you care about this company!"* $\to$ *Breeds defensiveness and emotional shutdown.*
* **Ineffective (Passive)**: Saying nothing and working overnight alone. $\to$ *Breeds exhaustion and resentment.*
* **Assertive Strategy**: *"I feel concerned when our milestone reports are delayed because it impacts our client obligations. Let's review the roadblocks together and set a realistic delivery time."*
