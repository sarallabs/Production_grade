## 📚 Section A: Multiple-Choice Questions (MCQ)

### Question 1
In Karl Bühler’s (1934) Organon Model, which functional domain is oriented toward the **Second Person (Addressee-Oriented)**?
- [ ] A) Expressive Function
- [x] B) Conative Function
- [ ] C) Representational Function
- [ ] D) Phatic Function

*Answer*: **B** | *Explanation*: The Conative function is designed to command, persuade, or direct the listener (Second Person / *"You"*).

---

### Question 2
Roman Jakobson’s (1960) framework links the **Poetic Function** directly to which communicative factor?
- [ ] A) Addresser
- [ ] B) Context
- [x] C) Message
- [ ] D) Contact

*Answer*: **C** | *Explanation*: The Poetic function focuses on the aesthetic form, rhythm, and linguistic qualities of the message itself.

### Question 3
How does Desmond Morris (1967) describe **"Grooming Talking"** in *The Naked Ape*?
- [ ] A) Providing step-by-step instructions on personal hygiene
- [x] B) Polite, meaningless chatter (phatic communion) used by humans as an evolutionary equivalent to primates picking fleas to establish peaceful coexistence
- [ ] C) Arguing fiercely in a court of law
- [ ] D) Delivering a scientific research presentation

*Answer*: **B** | *Explanation*: Morris identified grooming talking as social small talk that establishes bonds and mutual safety.

### Question 4
In James Britton’s (1970) functional model, what role does a writer or speaker assume when using the **Transactional Function**?
- [x] A) Participant role (using language as an instrument to get things done)
- [ ] B) Spectator role (contemplating aesthetic beauty)
- [ ] C) Passive observer role
- [ ] D) Strictly ceremonial role

*Answer*: **A** | *Explanation*: Britton's transactional function involves an active participant role aimed at informing, explaining, and executing practical tasks.

### Question 5
Why do complex communicative events like a presidential **State of the Nation Address (SONA)** exhibit overlapping functions?
- [ ] A) Because the president forgets which topic to discuss
- [x] B) Because comprehensive political discourse must report objective state data (Information), order executive actions (Control), and rally civic solidarity (Motivation)
- [ ] C) Because linear models forbid single-function speeches
- [ ] D) Because nonverbal channels cancel out verbal messages

*Answer*: **B** | *Explanation*: National addresses naturally combine information dissemination, regulatory commands, and inspirational motivation to achieve comprehensive governance goals.

### Question 6
What is the primary danger of a **Passive Communication Style** in collaborative team settings?
- [ ] A) It causes the speaker to speak too loudly
- [x] B) It causes individuals to conceal their true thoughts and needs, breeding hidden resentment and leading to low-quality group decisions
- [ ] C) It violates classroom netiquette rules
- [ ] D) It eliminates all semantic noise

*Answer*: **B** | *Explanation*: Passive communicators subordinate their rights and withhold input, which harms teamwork and generates personal frustration.

### Question 7
Which of the following statements represents an effective **"I" Statement** for conflict resolution?
- [ ] A) *"You always interrupt me because you don't respect anyone!"*
- [x] B) *"I feel unheard when you start speaking before I finish my thought, and I would appreciate being able to conclude my point."*

## ✍️ Section B: Applied Case Studies & Functional Mapping

### Question 8: Analyzing Multilingual Signs
**Scenario**: In a train station in Manila, three signs are posted side by side:
1. *"Caution: Wet Floor / Mag-ingat: Madulas ang Sahig"*
2. *"Ingatan ang Sariling Gamit Laban sa Mandurukot"* (*Watch Your Belongings Against Pickpockets*)
3. *"Salamat sa Pagsakay sa MRT-3! Have a Safe Journey!"*

* **Task**: Identify the primary and secondary communication function for each sign.

**Model Answer**:
1. **Primary: Information Dissemination** (reporting a physical hazard); **Secondary: Control / Regulation** (cautioning passengers to walk carefully).
2. **Primary: Control / Regulation** (warning commuters to guard belongings); **Secondary: Information Dissemination** (alerting to the threat of pickpockets).
3. **Primary: Social Interaction / Phatic Goodwill** (expressing courtesy and passenger appreciation); **Secondary: Motivation** (wishing a pleasant journey).

### Question 9: Deconstructing Communication Breakdowns
**Scenario**: An engineering team lead sends an email to marketing staff: *"Deploy the API endpoint hotfix onto staging by 18:00 to mitigate throughput latency."* The marketing team panics because they do not know what an API endpoint is or what they are expected to do.
* **Task 1**: Identify the type of noise causing the breakdown.
* **Task 2**: Explain which communication function was intended vs. how it was received.
* **Task 3**: Rewrite the message applying the 7 Cs.

**Model Answer**:
1. **Type of Noise**: **Semantic Noise** (specialized technical software engineering jargon unintelligible to non-technical staff).
2. **Functional Mismatch**: The engineer intended **Information Dissemination and Control/Regulation** (a task instruction), but it was received as an ambiguous crisis producing **Emotional Expression** (panic and confusion).
3. **Rewritten Message (Concise, Clear, Complete)**:
   > *"Hi Marketing Team, our developers will update the website server today at 6:00 PM to make page loading faster. No action is required on your end, but please note that the website may be briefly offline for 5 minutes. Thank you!"*

### Question 10: Comparative Matrix of Extrinsic Functional Theories
**Task**: Complete a brief comparative matrix linking Malinowski, Bühler, and Jakobson regarding how each describes language oriented toward: (a) the Speaker, (b) the Listener, and (c) Social Contact.

**Model Answer**:
* **Oriented toward the Speaker**:
  * *Bühler*: **Expressive Function** (First Person).
  * *Jakobson*: **Emotive Function** (Addresser).
* **Oriented toward the Listener**:
  * *Malinowski*: **Pragmatic Active** (Commands directing physical action).
  * *Bühler*: **Conative Function** (Second Person).
  * *Jakobson*: **Conative Function** (Addressee).
* **Oriented toward Social Contact**:
  * *Malinowski*: **Phatic Communion** (Binding social ties).
  * *Jakobson*: **Phatic Function** (Checking channel/contact).
