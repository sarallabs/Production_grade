# Flashcards: Functions of Communication & Language Models (Intermediate Level)

---

### Card 1
**Front**: Explain the difference between Bronislaw Malinowski's Pragmatic Active and Pragmatic Narrative functions of language.  
**Back**: 
* **Pragmatic Active**: Directs and coordinates immediate physical tasks for survival (e.g., commands during hunting).  
* **Pragmatic Narrative**: Recounts past events to store, plan, and pass down cultural knowledge.

---

### Card 2
**Front**: What is the core structure of Karl Bühler’s (1934) Organon Model?  
**Back**: Derived from grammatical persons:  
1. **Expressive** (1st Person): Speaker-oriented emotional states.  
2. **Conative** (2nd Person): Addressee-oriented commands and persuasion.  
3. **Representational** (3rd Person): Context-oriented external facts.

---

### Card 3
**Front**: How do Roman Jakobson’s (1960) six communicative factors correspond to language functions?  
**Back**: 
* Context $\to$ **Referential**  
* Addresser $\to$ **Emotive**  
* Addressee $\to$ **Conative**  
* Message $\to$ **Poetic**  
* Contact / Channel $\to$ **Phatic**  
* Code $\to$ **Metalingual**

---

### Card 4
**Front**: What is the function of the "Metalingual" function in Roman Jakobson’s model?  
**Back**: Language used to describe, explain, or define language itself (e.g., *"What do you mean by that word?", "'Exigence' means an urgent crisis"*).

---

### Card 5
**Front**: What are James Britton’s (1970) three functional categories of language?  
**Back**: 
1. **Expressive**: Immediate personal thoughts and feelings (the starting point).  
2. **Transactional**: Assuming a participant role to get things done (informing, debating).  
3. **Poetic**: Assuming a spectator role to create contemplated verbal art.

---

### Card 6
**Front**: How did Desmond Morris (1967) categorize human speech in *The Naked Ape*?  
**Back**: 
1. **Information Talking**: Rational data exchange.  
2. **Mood Talking**: Communicating feelings and physical states.  
3. **Exploratory Talking**: Playful, aesthetic wordplay.  
4. **Grooming Talking**: Polite chatter (phatic communion) establishing peaceful coexistence.

---

### Card 7
**Front**: Provide an example of overlapping communicative functions in a State of the Nation Address (SONA).  
**Back**: 
* **Information Dissemination**: Reporting economic data and completed projects.  
* **Motivation**: Inspiring national unity and civic pride.  
* **Control / Regulation**: Mandating government agencies to implement legislative reforms.

---

### Card 8
**Front**: Define "Linguistic Landscape" (Landry & Bourhis, 1997).  
**Back**: The visibility, prominence, and social salience of languages on public road signs, billboards, government notices, and commercial street signs in a territory.

---

### Card 9
**Front**: What communicative function is primarily served by commercial brand slogans like San Miguel Beer's "Iba ang May Pinagsamahan"?  
**Back**: **Motivation and Social Interaction** (evoking cultural camaraderie, long-standing friendship, solidarity, and belonging).

---

### Card 10
**Front**: Differentiate between Passive, Aggressive, and Assertive communication styles.  
**Back**: 
* **Passive**: Subordinating personal needs, hiding true feelings, breeding resentment.  
* **Aggressive**: Intimidating, shouting, interrupting, and destroying psychological safety.  
* **Assertive**: Clearly, directly, and respectfully communicating boundaries while honoring others.

---

### Card 11
**Front**: What are the three primary causes of interpersonal communication breakdowns?  
**Back**: 
1. Selective Listening (hearing only what confirms biases).  
2. Defensive Reactions (treating feedback as an attack).  
3. Ambiguous Messaging (vague words or contradictory nonverbal cues).

---

### Card 12
**Front**: How do "I" statements prevent defensive reactions during conflict resolution?  
**Back**: By framing issues around personal feelings and specific actions (*"I feel overwhelmed when deadlines shift without notice"*) rather than accusatory, attacking labels (*"You always mess up the schedule"*).
