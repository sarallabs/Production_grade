## 🔬 Critical Theoretical Distinctions

### 1. Extrinsic Use vs. Intrinsic Architectural Function
* **Extrinsic Functionalism (Malinowski, Bühler, Morris)**: Views function as synonymous with external social utility—the goals human beings achieve *with* language (e.g., getting fish, grooming social ties, expressing inner moods).
* **Intrinsic Functionalism (Halliday's SFL)**: Proves that functional requirements are inscribed directly into the internal lexicogrammar itself. Grammar did not evolve arbitrarily; it evolved into three simultaneous metafunctional strands because human social life demands concurrent representation of reality, management of relationships, and organization of messages.

## ⚖️ Applied SFL Dissection: Regulatory vs. Commercial Discourse
* **Regulatory Public Warning**: *"BAWAL TUMAWID: NAKAMAMATAY"*
  * *Transitivity*: Material process of crossing (*tumawid*) coupled with relational attributive fatality (*nakamamatay*).
  * *Mood/Modality*: High-obligation negative imperative (*Bawal*) enacting authoritative state control (Tenor).
  * *Thematic Texture*: Marked initial thematic prominence commanding rapid pedestrian attention in high-velocity transit environments (Mode).
* **Commercial Solidarity Slogan**: *"Iba ang May Pinagsamahan"*
  * *Transitivity*: Relational process attributing enduring positive cultural quality (*iba*) to shared historical fellowship (*pinagsamahan*).
  * *Mood/Modality*: Declarative asserting cultural camaraderie, aligning social solidarity with brand loyalty (Interpersonal).
