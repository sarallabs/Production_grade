# Flashcards: Functional Linguistics & Systemic Metafunctions (Advanced Level)

---

### Card 1
**Front**: What is M. A. K. Halliday’s central thesis regarding the functional architecture of natural language?  
**Back**: Function is not an extrinsic afterthought; it is built into the very lexicogrammatical architecture and semantic system of all natural languages.

---

### Card 2
**Front**: What is the principle of "Multifunctionality" in Systemic Functional Linguistics (SFL)?  
**Back**: Every natural clause does not serve an isolated function; it simultaneously integrates three distinct strands of meaning: Ideational, Interpersonal, and Textual.

---

### Card 3
**Front**: Define the Ideational Metafunction and its two subcomponents.  
**Back**: 
Language representing human reality:  
1. **Experiential Meaning**: Realized through Transitivity (Processes, Participants, Circumstances).  
2. **Logical Meaning**: Realized through Parataxis (coordination) and Hypotaxis (subordination).

---

### Card 4
**Front**: How is Experiential Meaning grammatically realized in Systemic Functional Linguistics?  
**Back**: Through the grammatical system of **Transitivity**: Processes (verbs: material, mental, relational), Participants (Actor, Goal, Sensor), and Circumstances (Locative, Manner, Time).

---

### Card 5
**Front**: Define the Interpersonal Metafunction and its grammatical systems.  
**Back**: 
Language functioning as social action to enact roles, status, and attitudes. Realized through:  
1. **Mood Structure**: Declarative, Interrogative, Imperative.  
2. **Modality**: Modulating probability, obligation, necessity, and inclination.

---

### Card 6
**Front**: Define the Textual Metafunction and its operational role in discourse.  
**Back**: 
Language creating **texture**—weaving ideational and interpersonal strands into cohesive, relevant discourse. Realized through Thematic Structure (Theme/Rheme), Information Focus (Given/New), and Cohesion (reference, ellipsis, conjunctions).

---

### Card 7
**Front**: How does the Context of Situation correlate with Halliday’s semantic metafunctions?  
**Back**: 
* **Field of Discourse** (what is happening) $\to$ Realized by **Experiential Meaning** (Transitivity).  
* **Tenor of Discourse** (who is taking part) $\to$ Realized by **Interpersonal Meaning** (Mood/Modality).  
* **Mode of Discourse** (the role assigned to language) $\to$ Realized by **Textual Meaning** (Theme/Cohesion).

---

### Card 8
**Front**: How does Halliday analyze Ben Jonson's line: "Or leave a kiss within the cup, and I'll not ask for wine"?  
**Back**: 
* **Experiential**: Physical acts of leaving a kiss vs. demanding wine.  
* **Interpersonal**: Reciprocal exchange (imperative request followed by an offer).  
* **Textual**: Rhythmical poetic meter balanced paratactically by *"and"*.

---

### Card 9
**Front**: How do the five core functions of communication (Sipacio & Balgos) map onto Halliday’s Metafunctions?  
**Back**: 
* Control / Regulation $\to$ **Interpersonal Metafunction** (Tenor / Imperative mood).  
* Social Interaction $\to$ **Interpersonal & Textual Metafunctions** (Tenor & Mode / Phatic).  
* Motivation $\to$ **Interpersonal & Ideational Metafunctions** (Tenor & Field / Modals).  
* Emotional Expression $\to$ **Interpersonal Metafunction** (Tenor / 1st Person).  
* Information Dissemination $\to$ **Ideational Metafunction** (Field / Transitivity).

---

### Card 10
**Front**: How does sociolinguistic analysis of the Philippine Linguistic Landscape reveal relations of power?  
**Back**: Regulatory public signs (e.g., *"BAWAL TUMAWID: NAKAMAMATAY"*) utilize imperative grammar and stark typography to manifest state authority (Tenor) and enforce pedestrian safety (Field) in public visual space (Mode).
