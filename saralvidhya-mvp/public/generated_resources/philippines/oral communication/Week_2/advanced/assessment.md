### Question 1
M. A. K. Halliday’s Systemic Functional Linguistics (SFL) asserts that language is inherently multifunctional. What does the **Ideational Metafunction** grammatically encode?
- [ ] A) Social distance, politeness markers, and mood structures
- [x] B) The human experiential world of processes, participants, and circumstances (Transitivity), alongside logical relations between clauses
- [ ] C) The rhythm, rhyme, and acoustic poetic meter of a line of verse
- [ ] D) The physical channel medium used to broadcast television signals

> **Explanation**: The Ideational Metafunction represents human reality through the experiential system of Transitivity (what is happening, who is involved, and under what conditions) and logical clause relations.

### Question 2
In Halliday’s correlation between the Context of Situation and Semantic Metafunctions, which situational feature is realized by the **Interpersonal Metafunction**?
- [ ] A) Field of Discourse
- [x] B) Tenor of Discourse
- [ ] C) Mode of Discourse
- [ ] D) Lexical Density

> **Explanation**: The Tenor of Discourse (the social relationships, power asymmetries, and degrees of solidarity among participants) is grammatically realized through the Interpersonal Metafunction (Mood and Modality).

### Question 3
How is the **Textual Metafunction** operationalized within natural language clauses?
- [ ] A) Through the biochemical reactions in the speaker’s vocal cords
- [x] B) Through Thematic Structure (Theme as the point of departure and Rheme), Information Structure (Given/New), and cohesive ties
- [ ] C) Solely through dictionary definitions of words
- [ ] D) Through imperative sentences and legal statutes

> **Explanation**: The Textual Metafunction creates texture by organizing clauses into coherent messages via Theme/Rheme choices, Given/New information flow, and cohesive devices.

### Question 4
Consider the line from Ben Jonson analyzed by Halliday: *"Or leave a kiss within the cup, and I'll not ask for wine."* What does the **Interpersonal** strand of this clause grammatically execute?
- [ ] A) A scientific definition of fermentation
- [x] B) A reciprocal exchange of speech roles: an imperative demand/request followed by a promissory offer
- [ ] C) A narrative recount of ancient Roman battles
- [ ] D) An explicit metalinguistic explanation of the word "cup"

> **Explanation**: Interpersonally, the clause negotiates social relations through paired speech functions: a request (*"leave a kiss..."*) linked paratactically to an offer (*"and I will not ask..."*).

### Question 5
How does Roman Jakobson’s **Metalingual Function** operate in linguistic discourse?
- [ ] A) It focuses entirely on expressing the speaker's emotional distress
- [x] B) It uses language to check, clarify, define, or explain the linguistic code itself
- [ ] C) It coordinates physical manual labor in agricultural communities
- [ ] D) It tests the electronic voltage of microphone hardware

> **Explanation**: The Metalingual function is oriented toward the Code itself, operating whenever speakers define terms, clarify semantics, or discuss grammar.

### Question 6
When analyzing a municipal signage installation reading *"BAWAL MAGTAPON NG BASURA DITO: MULTA PHP 5,000"* through SFL:
* Field = Municipal solid waste management
* Tenor = State authority imposing punitive legal fines on citizens
* Mode = Written public graphic signage
How does this situational configuration realize the **Interpersonal Metafunction**?
- [ ] A) By employing declarative descriptions of biological decomposition
- [x] B) By deploying an imperative mood structure and high-obligation modality establishing authoritative control
- [ ] C) By using poetic rhyme and metered stanza structures
- [ ] D) By offering casual, phatic small talk to tourists

> **Explanation**: The Interpersonal metafunction enacts power and authority (Tenor) through imperative grammar and legal prohibitions (Control/Regulation).
