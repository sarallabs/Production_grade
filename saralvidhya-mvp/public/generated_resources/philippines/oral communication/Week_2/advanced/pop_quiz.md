### Question 1: Halliday's Principle of Multifunctionality
In Systemic Functional Linguistics (SFL), why does M. A. K. Halliday reject the idea that a sentence can be physically sliced into separate functional pieces?
- [ ] A) Because grammar does not contain words
- [x] B) Because every natural clause simultaneously weaves three distinct strands of meaning: Ideational, Interpersonal, and Textual
- [ ] C) Because only poetry contains multiple meanings
- [ ] D) Because written language is fundamentally superior to spoken language

> **Explanation**: Natural language clauses are intrinsically multifunctional. A single clause concurrently represents reality (Ideational), enacts relationships (Interpersonal), and creates texture (Textual).

### Question 2: Experiential Meaning & Transitivity
In Halliday’s Ideational Metafunction, how is **Experiential Meaning** grammatically realized in natural language?
- [ ] A) Through the system of Mood and Modality
- [x] B) Through the system of Transitivity (Processes, Participants, and Circumstances)
- [ ] C) Through Thematic Structure (Theme and Rheme)
- [ ] D) Solely through punctuation marks

> **Explanation**: Experiential meaning represents reality through Transitivity: actions/states (processes), entities involved (participants), and contextual settings (circumstances).

### Question 3: Logical Meaning in the Ideational Metafunction
How does the **Logical** component of the Ideational Metafunction structure semantic relationships between multiple clauses?
- [ ] A) By calculating mathematical algorithms on computers
- [x] B) Through relations of Parataxis (equal/coordinate clauses) and Hypotaxis (subordinate/dependent clauses expressing condition, causality, or time)
- [ ] C) By eliminating all adjectives and adverbs from the sentence
- [ ] D) Through nonverbal body language exclusively

> **Explanation**: Logical meaning expresses the semantic relationships connecting clauses together, deploying coordination (parataxis) and subordination (hypotaxis).

### Question 4: Context of Situation to Semantic Realization
Which situational feature in Halliday's SFL correlates directly with the **Interpersonal Metafunction**?
- [ ] A) Field of Discourse
- [x] B) Tenor of Discourse
- [ ] C) Mode of Discourse
- [ ] D) Information Structure

> **Explanation**: The Tenor of Discourse (social relationships, power asymmetries, and degrees of solidarity among participants) is grammatically realized through the Interpersonal Metafunction (Mood and Modality).

### Question 5: SFL Analysis of "Song: To Celia"
In Halliday’s classic SFL analysis of Ben Jonson’s line: *"Or leave a kiss within the cup, and I'll not ask for wine"*, what does the **Interpersonal** strand grammatically execute?
- [ ] A) A scientific definition of wine fermentation
- [x] B) A reciprocal exchange of speech roles: an imperative demand/request followed by a promissory offer
- [ ] C) A narrative recount of ancient Roman battles
- [ ] D) An explicit metalinguistic explanation of the word "cup"

> **Explanation**: Interpersonally, the clause negotiates social relations through paired speech functions: a request (*"leave a kiss..."*) linked paratactically to an offer (*"and I will not ask..."*).

### Question 6: SFL Dissection of the Philippine Linguistic Landscape
Analyze the public transit warning: *"BAWAL TUMAWID: NAKAMAMATAY"*. How does this sign realize the **Tenor of Discourse** through the **Interpersonal Metafunction**?
- [ ] A) By offering gentle, optional advice through an interrogative mood
- [x] B) By deploying a negative imperative mood (*"Bawal"*) and unhedged high-obligation modality (*"nakamamatay"*) to enact state regulatory authority over the public
- [ ] C) By sharing casual social gossip to build peer camaraderie
- [ ] D) By describing the chemical composition of the road asphalt

> **Explanation**: The Tenor represents an authoritative institutional power asymmetry. It is grammatically realized through an imperative prohibition and high-obligation modality enforcing public behavioral compliance.

### Question 7: Extrinsic Utility vs. Intrinsic Architecture
How does M. A. K. Halliday’s SFL transcend earlier extrinsic functional models (Malinowski, Bühler, Morris)?
- [ ] A) By proving that grammar has no connection to human society
- [x] B) By demonstrating that function is not merely an external use, but is inscribed directly into the internal lexicogrammar and semantic architecture of all natural languages
- [ ] C) By showing that animals have the same grammatical systems as humans
- [ ] D) By eliminating the need for contextual analysis in linguistics

> **Explanation**: Earlier scholars saw function as what people do *with* language (extrinsic use). Halliday proved that functional requirements are inscribed directly into grammar itself (intrinsic architecture).

### Question 8: The Textual Metafunction and Information Flow
How does the Textual Metafunction differentiate between **Theme** and **Rheme** in clause grammar?
- [ ] A) Theme is always a verb; Rheme is always a noun
- [x] B) Theme is the point of departure of the clause (what the message is about), while Rheme is the remainder of the message providing new information
- [ ] C) Theme represents external physical noise; Rheme represents feedback
- [ ] D) Theme is spoken aloud; Rheme is kept completely silent

> **Explanation**: In SFL, Thematic structure organizes the message: the Theme serves as the starting point of the clause, while the Rheme develops and informs the message.

### Question 9: Mapping the 5 Core Functions to SFL
How does the core communicative function of **Information Dissemination** map onto Halliday’s Systemic Functional framework?
- [ ] A) It maps exclusively to the Interpersonal Metafunction through exclamations
- [x] B) It maps primarily to the Ideational (Experiential) Metafunction through declarative mood and transitivity structures describing phenomena
- [ ] C) It maps to the Phatic function through meaningless greetings
- [ ] D) It operates completely outside of natural language grammar

> **Explanation**: Information Dissemination focuses on subject matter, facts, and experiential phenomena (Field), which is grammatically realized through Ideational experiential transitivity structures.

### Question 10: Semiotic Salience in Multilingual Spaces
In the Philippine multilingual landscape, why do regulatory government signs often employ both Filipino (*Tagalog*) and English?
- [ ] A) Because English and Filipino use different alphabets
- [x] B) To maximize semiotic reach across diverse socioeconomic demographics, ensuring legal accessibility and civic compliance across both official languages
- [ ] C) Because computer sign-making software requires two languages by law
- [ ] D) To create poetic rhyme patterns for commuters

> **Explanation**: Multilingual signage ensures civic comprehension across varied linguistic backgrounds, reinforcing state authority (Tenor) and safeguarding public order across the population.
