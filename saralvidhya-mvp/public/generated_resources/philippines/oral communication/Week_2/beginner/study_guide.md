## 🔑 Key Vocabulary Checklist

| Word | Simple Meaning | Everyday Real-Life Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Control / Regulation** | Directing or guiding people's behavior with rules. | A traffic enforcer raising a hand to stop cars. |
| **Social Interaction** | Talking to build friendships and show warmth. | Saying *"Good morning!"* and asking about a friend's weekend. |
| **Motivation** | Giving hope, courage, and energy to reach a goal. | A coach saying, *"Keep working hard; you can do it!"* |
| **Emotional Expression** | Sharing what is in your heart (joy, sadness, relief). | Crying tears of joy when you pass an exam. |
| **Information Dissemination** | Sharing helpful, objective facts and directions. | A teacher explaining how volcanoes erupt. |
| **Phatic Communion** | Polite small talk that breaks awkward silence. | Asking an elevator neighbor, *"Is it raining outside?"* |
| **Assertive Communication** | Speaking honestly and politely without being rude. | *"I feel upset when you take my pen without asking."* |

## 💡 Quick Tips for Success

> [!TIP]
> **Use the 5-Finger Memory Trick**:
> * **Thumb (Control)**: A referee's whistle stopping play.
> * **Index (Information)**: Pointing out directions on a map.
> * **Middle (Motivation)**: Reaching high for your goals.
> * **Ring (Social Interaction)**: A ring symbolizing friendship and connection.
> * **Pinky (Emotional Expression)**: Sharing a soft, honest secret from the heart.

> [!NOTE]
> **Assertive Formula**:
> When you need to resolve a disagreement, say:
> *"I feel [emotion] when [action happens] because [reason]. Could we please [polite solution]?"*

## 📝 5-Minute Practice Activity
Look at these 3 situations. Write down which of the 5 functions is working:
1. A doctor says: *"Take one tablet every 8 hours after meals."* $\longrightarrow$ **__________________**
2. A classmate says: *"Thank you so much for helping me study; I was so scared of failing!"* $\longrightarrow$ **__________________**
3. A school banner reads: *"Enrollment is open from August 1 to August 15."* $\longrightarrow$ **__________________**

*(Answers: 1. Control / Regulation; 2. Emotional Expression; 3. Information Dissemination)*
