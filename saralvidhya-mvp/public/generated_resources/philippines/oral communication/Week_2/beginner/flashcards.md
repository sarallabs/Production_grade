# Flashcards: Functions of Communication (Beginner Level)

---

### Card 1
**Front**: What are the five core functions of communication according to Sipacio & Balgos (2016)?  
**Back**: 
1. Control / Regulation  
2. Social Interaction  
3. Motivation  
4. Emotional Expression  
5. Information Dissemination

---

### Card 2
**Front**: What is the primary purpose of the "Control / Regulation" function?  
**Back**: To manage, direct, and guide the behavior of individuals or groups, enforce obedience, and maintain social order.

---

### Card 3
**Front**: Give two examples of verbal clues used in Control/Regulation.  
**Back**: Imperative sentences and authoritative instructions (e.g., *"Wear face masks at all times," "Keep off the grass"*).

---

### Card 4
**Front**: What is the purpose of the "Social Interaction" function of communication?  
**Back**: To build camaraderie, develop friendships, nurture intimacy, and satisfy the human need for belonging.

---

### Card 5
**Front**: What is the purpose of the "Motivation" function of communication?  
**Back**: To inspire, encourage, and mobilize people to achieve goals, persevere through difficulties, and build confidence.

---

### Card 6
**Front**: What is the purpose of the "Emotional Expression" function of communication?  
**Back**: To vent, share, and express personal feelings, vulnerabilities, passions, grief, and joy.

---

### Card 7
**Front**: What is the purpose of the "Information Dissemination" function of communication?  
**Back**: To convey objective facts, scientific data, news, directions, and educational instructions.

---

### Card 8
**Front**: What does Malinowski's term "Phatic Communion" mean?  
**Back**: Polite small talk (like *"How are you?"* or talking about the weather) used as social grease to break awkward silence and build goodwill.

---

### Card 9
**Front**: In the Philippine Linguistic Landscape, what function does the sign "BAWAL TUMAWID: NAKAMAMATAY" serve?  
**Back**: Control / Regulation (it strictly restricts pedestrian movement to ensure public safety).

---

### Card 10
**Front**: What are the three styles of communication?  
**Back**: 
1. Passive (staying quiet and bottling up feelings)  
2. Aggressive (shouting, interrupting, and bullying)  
3. Assertive (expressing feelings directly, honestly, and politely)

---

### Card 11
**Front**: Why is being an Assertive Communicator the best choice?  
**Back**: Because it allows you to stand up for your own rights and feelings while still respecting the rights and feelings of others.

---

### Card 12
**Front**: What is an "I" statement, and why is it helpful?  
**Back**: It is phrasing your feelings using *"I feel..."* instead of blaming others with accusatory *"You always..."* statements, which prevents defensive arguments.
