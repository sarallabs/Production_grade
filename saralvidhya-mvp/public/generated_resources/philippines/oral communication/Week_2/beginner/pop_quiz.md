# Pop Quiz: Foundations of Everyday Communication (Beginner Level)

---

## 🎯 Quiz Overview
This pop quiz tests your understanding of the basic concepts in **Oral Communication Week 2**:
* The human need to interact (biological, psychological, and social)
* The five core functions of communication (Control, Social Interaction, Motivation, Emotional Expression, Information Dissemination)
* Recognizing communication functions in daily school and family life
* Understanding signs in your community (Philippine Linguistic Landscape)
* How to use an **Assertive Communication Style** and **"I" statements**

---

### Question 1: Why Do Humans Communicate?
Anthropologists and psychologists state that human communication is an essential human need. Which of the following is an example of a **Biological / Survival** need for interaction?
- [ ] A) Reading a fantasy novel before going to sleep
- [x] B) Calling out a warning when someone is about to step on broken glass
- [ ] C) Posting a picture of lunch on social media
- [ ] D) Telling a funny joke at a party

> **Explanation**: Warning others of physical hazards and coordinating collective safety are biological imperatives essential for human survival.

---

### Question 2: The Core Functions of Communication
According to Sipacio & Balgos (2016), communication functions describe the basic purposes of our talk. Which function is being used when a school librarian says, *"Please keep your voices down inside the library"*?
- [ ] A) Emotional Expression
- [x] B) Control / Regulation
- [ ] C) Motivation
- [ ] D) Social Interaction

> **Explanation**: Control / Regulation uses instructions, rules, and commands to manage, guide, and direct human behavior.

---

### Question 3: Building Friendships
Two students meet in the school cafeteria. One smiles and says, *"Hi! Is this seat taken? Would you like to eat lunch together?"* What is the primary function of this interaction?
- [ ] A) Control / Regulation
- [x] B) Social Interaction
- [ ] C) Information Dissemination
- [ ] D) Phatic Disconnection

> **Explanation**: Social Interaction serves to build friendships, show warmth, establish camaraderie, and fulfill the human need for belonging.

---

### Question 4: Inspiring Others
Before an important sports match, the team coach gathers everyone and says: *"You have worked hard every afternoon for this championship. Believe in yourselves, trust your teammates, and give it your all!"* What is the primary function?
- [ ] A) Information Dissemination
- [x] B) Motivation
- [ ] C) Control / Regulation
- [ ] D) Phatic Small Talk

> **Explanation**: Motivation inspires, encourages, and uplifts individuals to pursue goals, overcome adversity, and build confidence.

---

### Question 5: Speaking from the Heart
A student receives news that their family won a university scholarship. They begin crying with joy, hug their parents, and say: *"I feel so relieved and blessed; thank you for always supporting me!"* What is the primary function?
- [ ] A) Control / Regulation
- [x] B) Emotional Expression
- [ ] C) Information Dissemination
- [ ] D) Commercial Advertising

> **Explanation**: Emotional Expression allows individuals to release, vent, and share personal feelings, vulnerabilities, passions, and inner psychological states.

---

### Question 6: Sharing Useful Facts
A science teacher writes on the board: *"The boiling point of water at sea level is 100 degrees Celsius."* What is the primary function?
- [ ] A) Social Interaction
- [ ] B) Motivation
- [x] C) Information Dissemination
- [ ] D) Emotional Expression

> **Explanation**: Information Dissemination conveys objective knowledge, scientific data, announcements, and factual instructions.

---

### Question 7: What is Phatic Communion?
Anthropologist Bronislaw Malinowski (1923) coined the term **Phatic Communion** to describe:
- [ ] A) An angry argument between two drivers
- [x] B) Polite small talk (like *"Good morning! Nice weather, isn't it?"*) used to break awkward silence and build friendly bonds
- [ ] C) A strict military instruction
- [ ] D) An advanced mathematics proof

> **Explanation**: Phatic communion acts as a social lubricant to establish ties of union and show friendliness without transmitting heavy factual data.

---

### Question 8: Community Signs
In the Philippine Linguistic Landscape, you see a sign near a pedestrian overpass that reads: **"BAWAL TUMAWID: NAKAMAMATAY"** (*Do Not Cross: Deadly*). What is the primary function of this sign?
- [ ] A) Social Interaction
- [x] B) Control / Regulation
- [ ] C) Commercial Advertising
- [ ] D) Emotional Expression

> **Explanation**: The sign strictly restricts and regulates pedestrian crossing behavior to ensure public safety and save lives.

---

### Question 9: Choosing Your Communication Style
Which communication style is the healthiest and most respectful for solving disagreements?
- [ ] A) Passive Style (keeping quiet, hiding feelings, and letting others take advantage)
- [ ] B) Aggressive Style (shouting, interrupting, and bullying others to win)
- [x] C) Assertive Style (speaking honestly, clearly, and politely while respecting yourself and others)
- [ ] D) Sarcastic Style (making fun of people behind their backs)

> **Explanation**: Assertive communication stands up for personal rights and feelings while maintaining fairness and respect for others.

---

### Question 10: Using "I" Statements
Which of the following is the best example of an **Assertive "I" Statement**?
- [ ] A) *"You are so annoying and you always ruin our project!"*
- [ ] B) Saying nothing at all and crying alone after class
- [x] C) *"I feel stressed when tasks are submitted late because it leaves us with no time to review. Could we finish by 4 PM?"*
- [ ] D) *"You need to do what I say right now!"*

> **Explanation**: An assertive "I" statement expresses personal feelings and specific needs politely without attacking or blaming the other person.
