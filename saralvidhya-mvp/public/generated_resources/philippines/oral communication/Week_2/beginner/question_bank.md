## 📚 Section A: Multiple-Choice Questions (MCQ)

### Question 1
According to Sipacio & Balgos (2016), communication functions refer to:
- [ ] A) The mechanical parts of a computer keyboard
- [x] B) The fundamental social and practical purposes for which people use language and interaction
- [ ] C) Only formal speeches given by politicians
- [ ] D) The dictionary definitions of difficult words

*Answer*: **B** | *Explanation*: Communication functions explain the underlying goals and jobs that language accomplishes in everyday life.

---

### Question 2
Which function of communication is used when a teacher announces the classroom grading system on the first day of school?
- [x] A) Control / Regulation
- [ ] B) Emotional Expression
- [ ] C) Motivation
- [ ] D) Phatic Communion

*Answer*: **A** | *Explanation*: Announcing rules and policies establishes order and guides student behavior.

---

### Question 3
When you wave to a classmate and say, *"Hi! Do you want to eat lunch together?"*, which function are you practicing?
- [ ] A) Control / Regulation
- [x] B) Social Interaction
- [ ] C) Information Dissemination
- [ ] D) Poetic Language

*Answer*: **B** | *Explanation*: You are initiating friendly contact, building a friendship, and showing warmth.

### Question 4
A basketball coach at halftime shouts: *"You have the skill, the heart, and the power to win! Let's get out there and finish strong!"* What is the primary function?
- [ ] A) Information Dissemination
- [x] B) Motivation
- [ ] C) Emotional Expression
- [ ] D) Control / Regulation

*Answer*: **B** | *Explanation*: The coach is inspiring and encouraging the team to perform at their best.

### Question 5
Which of the following is an example of the **Emotional Expression** function?

- [x] C) Telling your parents through tears of joy, *"I am so happy I passed the scholarship exam!"*
- [ ] D) Pointing to a stop sign at an intersection

- [ ] A) Social Interaction

### Question 7
What is **Phatic Communion** (Malinowski, 1923)?
- [x] A) Friendly small talk used to break awkward silence and build goodwill (like *"Nice day, isn't it?"*)
- [ ] B) A strict military command
- [ ] C) A scientific research paper
- [ ] D) Shouting angrily during a fight

*Answer*: **A** | *Explanation*: Phatic communion acts as social grease to maintain peaceful, friendly relationships without transmitting complex facts.

## ✍️ Section B: Short-Answer & Real-Life Scenarios

### Question 8: Identifying Functions in Action
**Prompt**: Read each sentence below and state its primary communication function:
1. *"Submit your homework through Google Classroom by 5:00 PM today."*
2. *"Congratulations on winning the singing contest! I am so thrilled for you!"*
3. *"The library opens at 8:00 AM and closes at 6:00 PM."*
4. *"Don't worry if you made a mistake; practice every day and you will master this song!"*

**Model Answer**:
1. **Control / Regulation** (gives an explicit instruction and deadline).
2. **Emotional Expression** (shares personal joy and excitement).
3. **Information Dissemination** (conveys factual operating hours).
4. **Motivation** (encourages perseverance and uplifts morale).

---

### Question 9: Communication Styles
**Prompt**: Describe the difference between an **Aggressive** communicator and an **Assertive** communicator. Give an example of how each would ask someone to stop talking during a movie.

**Model Answer**:
* **Aggressive**: Demands things through intimidation, shouting, or insults without caring about others.  
  * *Example*: *"Shut your mouth right now, you idiot! You are ruining the movie!"*
* **Assertive**: Expresses needs clearly, directly, and politely while respecting the other person.  
  * *Example*: *"Excuse me, could you please keep your voice down? We are having trouble hearing the movie."*

### Question 10: Signs in the Philippine Linguistic Landscape
**Prompt**: Explain why public regulatory signs like *"BAWAL MAGTAPON NG BASURA DITO"* are examples of the **Control / Regulation** function.

**Model Answer**:
Because their direct social purpose is to guide and restrict human behavior, prevent illegal littering, and maintain clean and orderly community spaces through rules backed by authority.
