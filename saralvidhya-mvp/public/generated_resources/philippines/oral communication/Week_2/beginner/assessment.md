# Formative Assessment: Functions of Communication (Beginner Level)

---

## 📝 Practice Quiz: Identifying Communication Functions

Test your knowledge of the five core functions of communication! Choose the best answer for each question.

---

### Question 1
Sipacio & Balgos (2016) group communication into five core functions. Which function is being used when a school librarian points to a sign and says, *"Please maintain silence inside the reading room"*?
- [ ] A) Emotional Expression
- [x] B) Control / Regulation
- [ ] C) Social Interaction
- [ ] D) Motivation

> **Explanation**: The librarian is giving an explicit instruction to manage, direct, and control student behavior.

---

### Question 2
Two childhood friends meet unexpectedly at a shopping mall. They hug, laugh, and ask about each other’s families and weekend plans. What is the primary function of their conversation?
- [ ] A) Control / Regulation
- [ ] B) Information Dissemination
- [x] C) Social Interaction
- [ ] D) Formal Public Address

> **Explanation**: Their talk serves to nurture their personal relationship, express goodwill, and satisfy the human need for belonging.

---

### Question 3
Before the final championship basketball game, the team captain delivers a speech saying: *"We have trained for six months for this moment. Leave everything on the court and believe in each other!"* What is the primary function?
- [ ] A) Information Dissemination
- [x] B) Motivation
- [ ] C) Control / Regulation
- [ ] D) Phatic Small Talk

> **Explanation**: The captain is inspiring, uplifting, and encouraging the team to overcome adversity and achieve a goal.

---

### Question 4
A student receives a letter announcing that they won first prize in a national essay writing competition. They burst into tears of joy and exclaim, *"I can't believe this is happening! I am so thankful!"* What is the primary function?
- [ ] A) Control / Regulation
- [x] B) Emotional Expression
- [ ] C) Information Dissemination
- [ ] D) Transactional Instruction

> **Explanation**: The student is venting and releasing deep personal feelings of joy, excitement, and gratitude.

---

### Question 5
A meteorologist on a news channel announces: *"Typhoon Signal No. 2 is raised over Isabela and Cagayan Valley, with sustained winds of 75 kilometers per hour."* What is the primary function?
- [ ] A) Social Interaction
- [ ] B) Motivation
- [x] C) Information Dissemination
- [ ] D) Emotional Expression

> **Explanation**: The meteorologist is sharing objective, factual, scientific data to inform the public.

---

### Question 6
Anthropologist Bronislaw Malinowski coined the term **Phatic Communion** to describe:
- [ ] A) Language used exclusively in courtroom trials
- [ ] B) Complicated scientific formulas written on blackboards
- [x] C) Polite small talk (like *"Nice weather, isn't it?"*) used to break awkward silence and build goodwill
- [ ] D) Shouting angrily during an argument

> **Explanation**: Phatic communion acts as "social grease" to show friendliness without needing to transfer heavy factual data.

---

### Question 7
On a street corner in Metro Manila, you see a bright metal sign that reads: **"BAWAL TUMAWID: NAKAMAMATAY"** (*Do Not Cross: Deadly*). What is the primary function of this public sign?
- [ ] A) Social Interaction
- [x] B) Control / Regulation
- [ ] C) Emotional Expression
- [ ] D) Commercial Advertising

> **Explanation**: The sign strictly restricts pedestrian crossing behavior to protect human lives and enforce order.

---

### Question 8
Which communication style is the healthiest and most effective for resolving disagreements with friends?
- [ ] A) Passive (staying silent and holding a grudge)
- [ ] B) Aggressive (shouting and insulting the other person)
- [x] C) Assertive (expressing your needs honestly and politely while respecting the other person)
- [ ] D) Sarcastic (making fun of the other person behind their back)

> **Explanation**: Assertive communication stands up for personal rights and feelings while maintaining respect for others.
