# Learning Path: Functions of Communication & Functional Language Models

---

## 🗺️ Curriculum Progression Roadmap: Week 2

This learning path guides learners through the functional analysis of oral communication, bridging everyday speech acts with functional linguistics.

```
[ Stage 1: The Human Need ] ──► [ Stage 2: 5 Core Functions ] ──► [ Stage 3: Extrinsic Theories ] ──► [ Stage 4: SFL Metafunctions & Application ]
  • Biological survival           • Control / Regulation            • Malinowski (Phatic)               • Halliday's SFL (Ideational,
  • Psychological needs           • Social Interaction              • Bühler (Organon Model)              Interpersonal, Textual)
  • Societal cohesion             • Motivation                      • Jakobson (6 Factors)              • Context of Situation (Field, Tenor, Mode)
                                  • Emotional Expression            • Britton & Morris                  • Philippine Linguistic Landscape &
                                  • Information Dissemination                                             Assertive Communication Styles
```

## 📅 Suggested 7-Day Study Schedule

| Day / Module | Topic Focus | Core Resources | Guided Learning Activity |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Day 1: Human Imperative** | Why Humans Interact | `summary.md`, `study_guide.md` | Identify 3 biological, psychological, and social reasons you communicated today. |
| **Day 2: Core Functions** | The 5 Functions of Communication | `detailed_view.md` | Categorize 5 real-life conversations from your day into Sipacio & Balgos's 5 functions. |
| **Day 3: Overlapping Scenarios** | Multi-Layered Speech Acts | `detailed_view.md`, `flashcards.md` | Analyze how a doctor's consultation or State of the Nation Address overlaps functions. |
| **Day 4: Extrinsic Models** | Malinowski, Bühler & Jakobson | `mindmap.md`, `detailed_view.md` | Compare Bühler's Organon Model with Jakobson's 6 factors using a Venn diagram. |
| **Day 5: Halliday's SFL** | The 3 Metafunctions & Context | `study_guide.md`, `assessment.md` | Deconstruct a single sentence into its Ideational, Interpersonal, and Textual layers. |
| **Day 6: Sociolinguistics** | Philippine Linguistic Landscape | `summary.md`, `detailed_view.md` | Photograph or recall 2 local public signs and evaluate their Field, Tenor, and Mode. |
| **Day 7: Review & Mastery** | Formative Review & Self-Check | `question_bank.md`, `pop_quiz.md` | Complete Section A (MCQs) and Section B (applied case studies). |
