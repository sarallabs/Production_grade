# Curated Video Resources & Academic Lecture Links: Oral Communication Week 2

---

## 📺 Recommended Educational Video Modules

This curated list maps directly to the **Oral Communication in Context (Philippine Senior High School)** learning competencies for Lesson 2: Functions of Communication and Functional Theories of Language.

---

### 1. The Five Core Functions of Communication (Beginner / Intermediate)
* **Topic Focus**: Sipacio & Balgos's 5 Functions (Control/Regulation, Social Interaction, Motivation, Emotional Expression, Information Dissemination), Everyday Real-Life Examples, Verbal & Nonverbal Markers.
* **Curated Lecture / Reference**: *Functions of Communication in Everyday Life*
  * **Core Concept**: Exploring how human beings use language to manage behavior, build relationships, inspire action, express feelings, and share factual knowledge.
  * **Search Query / Resource Link**: `https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=five+functions+of+communication+oral+communication+shs`
  * **Estimated Duration**: 10–12 minutes
  * **Key Discussion Question**: How does a school principal's assembly speech combine information dissemination with motivation and control?

---

### 2. A Guide to Effective Communication: Styles & Breakdown Resolution (All Levels)
* **Topic Focus**: Interpersonal Communication Styles (Passive, Aggressive, Assertive), Causes of Communication Breakdowns (Selective Listening, Defensiveness), Active Listening & "I" Statements.
* **Curated Lecture / Reference**: *A Guide to Effective Communication* (Featured Textbook Resource)
  * **Direct Video Resource**: `https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JwjAAgGi-90`
  * **Core Concept**: Analyzing common interpersonal friction points, psychological barriers, and practical assertive communication strategies.
  * **Estimated Duration**: 7–9 minutes
  * **Key Discussion Question**: Why are "I" statements more effective at resolving team conflict than accusatory "You" statements?

---

### 3. Extrinsic Functional Theories: Malinowski, Bühler & Jakobson (Intermediate / Advanced)
* **Topic Focus**: Bronislaw Malinowski’s Phatic Communion, Karl Bühler’s Organon Model (1st, 2nd, 3rd Person), Roman Jakobson’s Six Communicative Factors & Functions.
* **Curated Lecture / Reference**: *Jakobson's Functions of Language & The Organon Model*
  * **Core Concept**: How linguistics evolved from treating function as extrinsic social utility to mapping communicative factors (Context, Addresser, Addressee, Message, Contact, Code).
  * **Search Query / Resource Link**: `https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=roman+jakobson+functions+of+language+organon+model`
  * **Estimated Duration**: 12–15 minutes
  * **Key Discussion Question**: How does Jakobson's Phatic function explain why humans use polite small talk when meeting strangers?

---

### 4. Systemic Functional Linguistics & Halliday's Metafunctions (Advanced)
* **Topic Focus**: M. A. K. Halliday’s SFL, The Principle of Multifunctionality, Ideational (Transitivity), Interpersonal (Mood/Modality), and Textual (Theme/Cohesion) Metafunctions, Context of Situation (Field, Tenor, Mode).
* **Curated Lecture / Reference**: *Introduction to Systemic Functional Linguistics (SFL) by M.A.K. Halliday*
  * **Core Concept**: Demonstrating that natural language grammar is inherently functional and mirrors the social purposes of human interaction.
  * **Search Query / Resource Link**: `https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=systemic+functional+linguistics+halliday+metafunctions+field+tenor+mode`
  * **Estimated Duration**: 15–20 minutes
  * **Key Discussion Question**: In what ways does Halliday's analysis of Ben Jonson's poetry prove that a clause cannot be sliced into separate functional pieces?
