### Question 12
**Mindmap Location:** 3. Guidelines > Formal Syllogistic Validity  
**Question:** Examine the syllogism: 'All philosophers are immortal; Socrates is a philosopher; therefore Socrates is immortal.' What is its logical status?  
- A) Formally invalid and materially false  
- B) Formally valid, but materially false (soundness failure due to false major premise)  
- C) Formally invalid, but materially true  
- D) A classic example of an Ad Populum fallacy  

<details><summary>Click to view Answer</summary>
<b>Correct Answer: B</b><br/>
<b>Rationale:</b> The deductive form is valid (Barbara syllogism), but the argument is unsound because the major premise ('All philosophers are immortal') is empirically false.
</details>
