### Question 1
**Mindmap Location:** 1. Taxonomy of Reading > Hirschberg Spectrum  
**Question:** Which dimension fundamentally distinguishes 'Critical Reading' from 'Reading for Information'?  
- A) Critical reading requires high reading speed, whereas reading for information requires slow reading  
- B) Critical reading engages in a questioning dialectical dialogue with the text, whereas reading for information accepts text as an authoritative repository of facts  
- C) Critical reading is exclusively applied to fictional literature, while reading for information is for science  
- D) Critical reading is done without taking written notes to avoid bias  

<details><summary>Click to view Answer</summary>
<b>Correct Answer: B</b><br/>
<b>Rationale:</b> Hirschberg establishes that reading for information is utilitarian memorization, while critical reading is an active mental dialogue questioning claims and evaluating warrants.
</details>
