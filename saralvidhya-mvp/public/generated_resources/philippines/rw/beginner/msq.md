### Question 1
**Mindmap Location:** 1. Taxonomy of Reading > Four Kinds  
**Question:** Which of the following are recognized among Hirschberg's FOUR kinds of reading? (Select ALL that apply)  
- [ ] A) Casual Reading  
- [ ] B) Reading for Pleasure  
- [ ] C) Hypnotic Reading  
- [ ] D) Critical Reading  

<details><summary>Click to view Answer</summary>
<b>Correct Options: A, B, D</b><br/>
<b>Rationale:</b> Hirschberg defines Casual reading, Reading for pleasure, Reading for information, and Critical reading. 'Hypnotic reading' is not one of them.
</details>

### Question 3
**Mindmap Location:** 3. Guidelines > Active Reading Habits  
**Question:** Which habits reflect ACTIVE reading according to Metherell & Winkler? (Select ALL that apply)  
- [ ] A) Underlining the author's central thesis statement  
- [ ] B) Writing marginal notes to record personal reactions and questions  
- [ ] C) Skimming without a pen so the book can be resold in mint condition  
- [ ] D) Flagging errors of fact or logic where they occur on the page  

<details><summary>Click to view Answer</summary>
<b>Correct Options: A, B, D</b><br/>
<b>Rationale:</b> Active reading means annotating, underlining the thesis, and recording margin reactions. Skimming without writing to keep the book clean is criticized as penny-wise and pound-foolish.
</details>

### Question 5
**Mindmap Location:** 4. Essay Architecture > Premise Signals  
**Question:** Which of the following words are PREMISE SIGNALS that introduce supporting evidence or reasons? (Select ALL that apply)  
- [ ] A) Because  
- [ ] B) Since  
- [ ] C) Therefore  
- [ ] D) Inasmuch as  

<details><summary>Click to view Answer</summary>
<b>Correct Options: A, B, D</b><br/>
<b>Rationale:</b> 'Because', 'since', and 'inasmuch as' signal premises. 'Therefore' signals a conclusion.
</details>

### Question 9
**Mindmap Location:** 5. Intertextuality > Transformative Effects  
**Question:** Which of the following are the TRANSFORMATIVE EFFECTS produced by intertextuality? (Select ALL that apply)  
- [ ] A) Transformation of the Primary Book  
- [ ] B) Transformation of the Prior Text (Reflexive re-reading)  
- [ ] C) Simultaneous Reinterpretation of Both Texts

<details><summary>Click to view Answer</summary>

</details>

### Question 10
**Mindmap Location:** 3. Guidelines > Demystifying Authors  
**Question:** Why do Metherell and Winkler urge students to demystify writers? (Select ALL that apply)  
- [ ] A) Because writers are only human and prone to error  
- [ ] B) Because writers have personal biases and hidden assumptions  
- [ ] C) Because seeing writers as fallible empowers students to think independently  
- [ ] D) Because all published writers intentionally lie to readers  

<details><summary>Click to view Answer</summary>
<b>Correct Options: A, B, C</b><br/>
<b>Rationale:</b> Writers are fallible humans with personal biases; recognizing this empowers students to read critically. It is false that all writers intentionally lie.
</details>
