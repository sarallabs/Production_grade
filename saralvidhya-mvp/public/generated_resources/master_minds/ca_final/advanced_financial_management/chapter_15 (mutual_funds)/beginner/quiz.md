# Mutual Funds

This practice quiz consists of 5 multiple-choice questions designed to test basic conceptual understanding and simple formula applications of mutual funds for CA Final AFM.

---

Question 1
Which independent entity is registered with SEBI to take safe physical and electronic custody of the securities and assets purchased by a mutual fund scheme?
A. Asset Management Company (AMC)
B. Custodian
C. Sponsor
D. Registrar and Transfer Agent (RTA)

Correct Answer: B
Explanation: The Custodian is an independent entity registered with SEBI that takes safe physical and electronic custody of all securities and assets purchased by the mutual fund scheme, separating asset management from asset custody to prevent fraud.

---

Question 2
An investor wants to purchase units of an open-ended mutual fund scheme that has a Net Asset Value (NAV) of ₹25.00. If the scheme charges an entry load of 2.0%, what is the purchase price (Public Offer Price) per unit for the investor?
A. ₹24.50
B. ₹25.00
C. ₹25.50
D. ₹26.00

Correct Answer: C
Explanation: The purchase price (Public Offer Price) is calculated as NAV * (1 + Entry Load %). In this case: Purchase Price = ₹25.00 * (1 + 0.02) = ₹25.50.

Question 3
Under the provisions of Section 80C of the Income Tax Act, 1961, what is the mandatory lock-in period for investments made in an Equity Linked Savings Scheme (ELSS)?
A. 1 year
B. 3 years
C. 5 years
D. 15 years

Correct Answer: B
Explanation: As per Section 80C of the Income Tax Act, 1961, Equity Linked Savings Schemes (ELSS) carry a mandatory lock-in period of 3 years from the date of allotment of units.

---

Question 4
Which type of mutual fund scheme invests exclusively in government securities and Treasury bills, thereby carrying zero credit (default) risk?
A. Corporate Bond Fund
B. Gilt Fund
C. Dynamic Bond Fund
D. Credit Risk Fund

Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Gilt funds invest exclusively in securities and Treasury bills issued by the central and state governments. Because these are backed by sovereign guarantees, they carry zero credit (default) risk, although they still carry interest rate risk.

Question 5
A mutual fund scheme has total assets valued at ₹520 lakhs and accrued liabilities of ₹20 lakhs. If the number of outstanding units is 20 lakhs, what is the Net Asset Value (NAV) per unit of the scheme?
A. ₹25.00
B. ₹26.00
C. ₹27.00
D. ₹50.00

Correct Answer: A
Explanation: Net Asset Value (NAV) per unit = (Total Assets - Total Liabilities) / Total Number of Outstanding Units. Here, NAV = (₹520 lakhs - ₹20 lakhs) / 20 lakhs units = ₹500 lakhs / 20 lakhs units = ₹25.00.
