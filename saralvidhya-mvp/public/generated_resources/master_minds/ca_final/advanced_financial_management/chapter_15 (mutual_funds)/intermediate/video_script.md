## Scene 2: The Three-Tier Structural Framework
* **Visual Prompt:**
  > An elegant, 3D architectural model of three floating glass platforms stacked vertically in a high-tech laboratory environment. The bottom platform is labeled 'Sponsor' in metallic gold letters. The middle platform is labeled 'Trustees' and has a glowing shield icon. The top platform is labeled 'AMC (Asset Management Company)' with a professional trading desk setup. Off to the side, a secure steel vault labeled 'Custodian' lights up in green. Cinematic camera rotation, depth of field.
* **Audio / Voiceover Narration:**
  "To protect investor interest, SEBI mandates a strict three-tier structure. First, the Sponsor, who acts as the promoter and registers the trust. Second, the Board of Trustees, who act as the guardians of the investors' wealth. And third, the AMC, the professional brain of the fund that designs and executes the portfolio strategies. A separate Custodian holds the assets securely, preventing any conflict of interest."
* **On-Screen Text Overlay:**
  * **Level 1:** Sponsor (Min. 40% Net Worth)
  * **Level 2:** Trustees (2/3rd Independent)
  * **Level 3:** Asset Management Company (AMC)
  * **Safety:** Independent Custodian

## Scene 3: Capital Protection Oriented Funds (CPOF)
* **Visual Prompt:**
  > A sleek holographic scale balancing in mid-air. On one side of the scale, a heavy, solid steel safe block sits labeled 'AAA Bonds (77.2%)'. On the other side of the scale, a vibrant, green glowing seedling represents 'Equity (22.8%)'. Mathematical equations flash in neon blue on the screen, showing the present value of money compounding over time. Future-tech aesthetic, high contrast.
* **Audio / Voiceover Narration:**
  "How do Capital Protection Oriented Funds guarantee your principal at maturity? It's simple present value mathematics. The fund manager calculates the exact amount of money needed today to grow to the total principal value at maturity using high-quality corporate bonds. The remaining corpus is placed in equity, allowing the investor to enjoy capital growth with zero risk to their starting principal."
* **On-Screen Text Overlay:**
  * **Formula:** $\text{Bond Allocation} = $\frac{\text{Principal}}{(1 + r)^n}$
  * **Allocation Example:** 77% Debt / 23% Equity
  * **Outcome:** Capital Protected + Equity Upside

## Scene 4: The Mechanics of Side-Pocketing
* **Visual Prompt:**
  > A clean digital grid of glowing green cube blocks representing a healthy credit portfolio. Suddenly, one cube in the center turns dark red and flashes a warning symbol. A digital laser beam isolates the red cube, physically pulling it out of the grid and placing it inside a separate, sealed transparent glass box. The remaining green cubes instantly light back up and continue pulsing. 3D motion graphics, crisp animations.
* **Audio / Voiceover Narration:**
  "When a credit event occurs and an issuer defaults, fund managers use side-pocketing to contain the damage. The distressed asset is separated into a segregated, locked portfolio. New subscriptions and redemptions continue normally in the main, healthy portfolio. This prevents panic redemptions from forcing a fire sale of good assets, protecting existing, incoming, and outgoing investors alike."
* **On-Screen Text Overlay:**
  * **Credit Event:** Default / Downgrade
  * **Action:** Segregated Portfolio (Locked) + Main Portfolio (Active)
  * **Benefit:** Stabilizes NAV & Prevents Liquidity Collapse

## Scene 5: Quantitative Models & Risk-Adjusted Performance
* **Visual Prompt:**
  > A split-screen showing three distinct neon equations hovering over moving stock market charts. The left screen shows a standard deviation bell curve, the middle screen shows a beta volatility line, and the right screen shows a CAPM regression line. The camera smoothly zooms into each equation as it illuminates. Dark mode UI, vibrant blue and purple neon hues.
* **Audio / Voiceover Narration:**
  "In your exams, evaluating a mutual fund requires adjusting for risk. Use the Sharpe Ratio to measure excess return against total risk, using standard deviation. Use the Treynor Ratio to measure excess return against systematic risk, using Beta. And use Jensen's Alpha to measure the absolute abnormal return generated by the manager relative to the Capital Asset Pricing Model. A positive alpha is the mark of a skilled manager."
* **On-Screen Text Overlay:**
  * **Sharpe Ratio:** $\frac{R_p - R_f}{\sigma_p}$ (Total Risk)
  * **Treynor Ratio:** $\frac{R_p - R_f}{\beta_p}$ (Systematic Risk)
  * **Jensen's Alpha:** $R_p - [R_f + \beta_p(R_m - R_f)]$
