Question 1
A portfolio has an average annualized return of 14% with a standard deviation of 16% and a beta of 0.8. If the risk-free rate of return is 6%, what are the Sharpe and Treynor ratios for this portfolio respectively?
A. 0.50 and 10.00%
B. 0.50 and 8.00%
C. 0.88 and 10.00%
D. 0.88 and 8.00%

Correct Answer: A
Explanation: 
* **Sharpe Ratio** measures the excess return per unit of total risk (Standard Deviation):
  $$\text{Sharpe Ratio} = $\frac{R_p - R_f}{\sigma_p} = $\frac{14\% - 6\%}{16\%} = $\frac{8\%}{16\%} = 0.50$$
* **Treynor Ratio** measures the excess return per unit of systematic risk (Beta):
  $$\text{Treynor Ratio} = $\frac{R_p - R_f}{\beta_p} = $\frac{14\% - 6\%}{0.8} = $\frac{8\%}{0.8} = 10.00\%$$
Thus, the correct option is A (0.50 and 10.00%).

Question 2
An open-ended equity mutual fund scheme has 10,00,000 units outstanding. The market value of its investments is ₹2,40,00,000, and it holds a cash balance of ₹10,00,000. If the scheme has accrued operating expenses of ₹1,50,000 and management fees payable of ₹3,50,000, what is the Net Asset Value (NAV) per unit?
A. ₹24.50
B. ₹24.85
C. ₹25.00
D. ₹23.85

Correct Answer: A
Explanation: 
The Net Assets of the scheme are calculated as:
$$\text{Net Assets} = \text{Market Value of Investments} + \text{Cash Balances} - \text{Accrued Expenses} - \text{Management Fees Payable}$$
$$\text{Net Assets} = ₹2,40,00,000 + ₹10,00,00,0 - ₹1,50,000 - ₹3,50,000$$
$$\text{Net Assets} = ₹2,50,00,000 - ₹5,00,000 = ₹2,45,00,000$$
The NAV per unit is calculated as:
$$\text{NAV per Unit} = $\frac{\text{Net Assets}}{\text{Total Outstanding Units}} = $\frac{₹2,45,00,000}{10,00,000 \text{ units}} = ₹24.50$$
Thus, the correct option is A.

Question 3
For a 5-year Capital Protection Oriented Fund (CPOF) with a unit face value of ₹10, the prevailing interest rate on high-quality AAA-rated bonds of similar maturity is 8% p.a. compounded annually. What is the required asset allocation to the bond component to protect the principal at maturity?
A. ₹6.21 (62.1%)
B. ₹6.81 (68.1%)
C. ₹7.25 (72.5%)
D. ₹5.00 (50.0%)

Correct Answer: B
Explanation: 
To protect the capital of ₹10 per unit at the end of 5 years, the amount invested in the bond component must grow to ₹10 via interest compounding.
Using the Present Value formula:
$$PV = \frac{\text{Future Value}}{(1 + r)^n} = $\frac{₹10}{(1 + 0.08)^5}$$
$$PV = \frac{₹10}{1.469328} = ₹6.8058 \approx ₹6.81$$
Thus, the fund must allocate ₹6.81 (or 68.1% of the corpus) to the bond component, leaving the remaining ₹3.19 (31.9%) for equity investments. The correct option is B.

Question 4
In the context of 'side-pocketing' in debt mutual funds, which of the following statements is correct?
A. New investors can subscribe to both the main portfolio and the segregated portfolio at their respective NAVs.
B. Existing unit holders cannot redeem their units in the main portfolio until the segregated portfolio's assets are recovered.
C. The segregated portfolio is closed for subscriptions and redemptions, whereas the main portfolio remains open for normal transactions.
D. The segregated portfolio continues to charge standard management fees to recover transaction losses.

Correct Answer: C
Explanation: 
When side-pocketing is executed, the mutual fund split results in two portfolios:
* The **Main Portfolio** containing the healthy, liquid assets. It continues normal subscriptions and redemptions.
* The **Segregated Portfolio** containing the defaulted or distressed assets. It is completely locked and closed for both new subscriptions and redemptions to prevent arbitrage and ensure that recovery proceeds go only to original unit holders. No management fees are charged on the segregated portfolio.
Thus, statement C is correct.

Question 5
An investor purchases units of a mutual fund at a Public Offer Price (POP) of ₹12.50 per unit. If the scheme charges a front-end load of 4.00%, what is the underlying NAV per unit at which the units are issued?
A. ₹12.00
B. ₹12.02
C. ₹13.00
D. ₹12.98

Correct Answer: A
Explanation: 
The Public Offer Price (POP) includes the front-end load, and it relates to the Net Asset Value (NAV) as:
$$\text{POP} = \frac{\text{NAV}}{1 - \text{Front-end Load Fraction}}$$
Which can be rewritten as:
$$\text{NAV} = \text{POP} \times (1 - \text{Front-end Load Fraction})$$
$$\text{NAV} = ₹12.50 \times (1 - 0.04) = ₹12.50 \times 0.96 = ₹12.00$$
Thus, the underlying Net Asset Value per unit is ₹12.00, and the front-end load is ₹0.50 (4% of ₹12.50). The correct option is A.
