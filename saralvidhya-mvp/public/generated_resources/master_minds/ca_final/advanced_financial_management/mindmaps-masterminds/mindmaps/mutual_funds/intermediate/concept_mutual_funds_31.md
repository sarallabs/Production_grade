### 📝 Source Institutional Content
> **Reference:** Page 4
> **Content:** International Funds: Indian registered, invest in global markets. For Indian investors seeking geographical diversification.

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept Summary:** Indian mutual funds that invest in foreign equities, either directly or through overseas feeder funds.
- **Formula/Rule:** Standard guidelines apply.
- **Application Context:** No specific application context available.

### 🔍 Detailed View
These funds invest in global markets (US, Europe, emerging markets). They provide a hedge against the depreciation of the Indian Rupee; if the USD rises against the INR, the NAV increases even if the underlying stock prices remain flat. Note: In India, international funds are taxed as debt funds.
