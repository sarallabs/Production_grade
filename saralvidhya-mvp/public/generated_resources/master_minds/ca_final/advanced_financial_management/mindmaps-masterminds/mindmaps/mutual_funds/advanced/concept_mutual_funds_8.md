### 📋 Summary View
- **Strategic Context:** **Concept Summary:** Hybrid structures that remain closed-ended for specified durations and open for transaction windows at predetermined intervals.
**Key Takeaway:** Balances long-term asset positioning with scheduled investor exit windows.
**Analogy:** Private credit funds that offer quarterly redemption gates with strict limits.
- **Quantitative Model:** No specific quantitative model details.
- **Risk/Opportunity:** No specific risk/opportunity details.

### 🔍 Detailed View
The interval periods (e.g., 3 days every quarter) are defined in the Scheme Information Document. During these windows, investors can buy or redeem units at NAV directly from the AMC. The fund manager structures the portfolio's debt maturities to align with these windows, utilizing instruments like commercial papers and short-term deposits that mature just before the interval date to provide liquidity.
