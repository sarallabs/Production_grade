# Fund of Funds (FOF)

> **Sub-Topic 3.2.4:** Specialized Strategies | **Chapter:** Mutual Funds | **Level:** Intermediate

--- 

### 📝 Source Institutional Content
> **Reference:** Page 7
> **Content:** Fund of Funds (FOF): Invest in other mutual funds. Higher expense ratio due to double layering.

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept Summary:** A mutual fund scheme that invests in units of other mutual fund schemes rather than directly in shares or bonds.
- **Formula/Rule:** Standard guidelines apply.
- **Application Context:** No specific application context available.

### 🔍 Detailed View
An FOF can invest in domestic funds, international funds, or ETFs. While it simplifies portfolio management (one folio for multiple funds), the investor pays the management fee of the FOF itself, plus the underlying expense ratios of all the funds it holds. Taxed as a debt fund in India, regardless of the underlying assets.
