### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept Summary:** ETFs require a demat/trading account, incur brokerage commissions, and can suffer from liquidity discounts/premiums.
- **Formula/Rule:** Standard guidelines apply.
- **Application Context:** No specific application context available.

### 🔍 Detailed View
**Bid-Ask Spread:** If an ETF has low trading volume, the selling price (bid) might be significantly lower than the buying price (ask), creating an implicit cost.
**Premium/Discount to NAV:** In volatile markets, the market price of an ETF on the exchange can deviate from its actual NAV.
**Demat Charges:** Annual maintenance charges (AMC) for demat accounts and transaction taxes (STT) add to the cost.
