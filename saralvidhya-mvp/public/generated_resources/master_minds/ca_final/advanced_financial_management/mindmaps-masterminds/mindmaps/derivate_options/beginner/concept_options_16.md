# Lower Transaction Costs

> **Sub-Topic 2.2.1:** Key Benefits | **Chapter:** Derivatives Options | **Level:** Beginner

--- 

### 📝 Source Institutional Content
> **Reference:** Page 1
> **Content:** Lower transaction costs compared to futures.

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept:** Options have much lower transaction costs, taxes, and brokerages compared to buying physical shares or futures.
- **Analogy:** Paying a small entry ticket fee to a museum instead of purchasing a museum membership.
- **Key Takeaway:** Less money is lost in friction and fees.

### 🔍 Detailed View
**Explanation:** Because the trade value (premium) is small, the government taxes (like Securities Transaction Tax - STT) and brokerage fees calculated on options are much lower than trading physical stocks or futures.
**Worked Example:** Trading Nifty futures worth ₹5,50,000 might attract ₹150 in government taxes and fees. Trading a Nifty option with a premium of ₹5,000 will attract only a fraction of that tax, saving you transaction costs.
