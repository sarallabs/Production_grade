### 📝 Source Institutional Content

> **Content:** Contra: Invest in underperforming/out-of-focus stocks expecting turnaround.

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Strategic Context:** **Concept Summary:** Portfolios executing a contrarian value strategy, targeting companies trading at deep discounts due to temporary operational or sectoral stress.
**Key Takeaway:** Harvests the Value factor by exploiting cognitive biases and market overreaction.
**Analogy:** Distressed asset investing or purchasing deep value special situations.
- **Quantitative Model:** No specific quantitative model details.
- **Risk/Opportunity:** No specific risk/opportunity details.

### 🔍 Detailed View
The manager targets stocks where current market sentiment is negative but fundamentals remain intact. This involves analyzing debt levels, asset backing, and cyclical recovery timelines. The primary risks are:
1. **Value Trap:** A stock that appears cheap but continues to deteriorate.
2. **Timing Risk:** The contrarian theme may take several years to play out, leading to prolonged underperformance against growth benchmarks.
