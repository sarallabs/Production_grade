# Offshore Funds

> **Sub-Topic 3.2.4:** Specialized Strategies | **Chapter:** Mutual Funds | **Level:** Beginner

--- 

### 📝 Source Institutional Content
> **Reference:** Page 4
> **Content:** Offshore Funds: Registered outside India, invest in India. For non-residents.

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept:** Funds registered outside India that collect money from international investors to invest in Indian companies.
- **Analogy:** A foreign tourist hiring a local Indian guide to shop for genuine Indian handicrafts.
- **Key Takeaway:** Designed for foreigners who want to invest in India's growth.

### 🔍 Detailed View
These funds are created in tax-friendly countries (like Mauritius or Singapore) and are registered with SEBI as Foreign Portfolio Investors (FPIs). They allow global investors to participate in the Indian stock market without needing to open local bank or demat accounts in India.
