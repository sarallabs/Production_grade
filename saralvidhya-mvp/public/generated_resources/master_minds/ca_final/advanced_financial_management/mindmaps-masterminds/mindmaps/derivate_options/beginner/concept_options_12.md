# Loss Visibility

> **Sub-Topic 2.1.3:** Futures vs. Options | **Chapter:** Derivatives Options | **Level:** Beginner

--- 

### 📝 Source Institutional Content
> **Reference:** Page 1
> **Content:** Loss Visibility: Futures (Unknown in advance) vs. Options (Known in advance for buyer).

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept:** An option buyer knows their maximum possible loss (the premium paid) before entering the trade. A future trader faces unlimited, unknown losses.
- **Analogy:** Option buying is like buying a movie ticket—you know exactly how much money you spent. Futures is like eating at an expensive buffet with dynamic per-gram pricing.
- **Key Takeaway:** Options = Maximum loss is known and fixed. Futures = Loss is unknown and can be huge.

### 🔍 Detailed View
**Explanation:** Risk management is simple for option buyers because the worst-case scenario is that the option becomes worthless (zero). For futures, if a stock hits a lower circuit due to bad news, losses can compile overnight with no limit.
**Worked Example:** You buy a Call option for ₹2,000. Your maximum loss is ₹2,000. You buy Futures with ₹1,00,000 margin. If the stock crashes 30% due to fraud news, your loss could be ₹3,000,000, far exceeding your margin.
