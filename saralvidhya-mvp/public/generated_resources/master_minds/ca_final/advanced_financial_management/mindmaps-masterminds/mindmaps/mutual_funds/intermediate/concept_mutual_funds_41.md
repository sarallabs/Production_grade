# Side-Pocketing

> **Sub-Topic 3.4.3:** SEBI Regulations | **Chapter:** Mutual Funds | **Level:** Intermediate

--- 

### 📝 Source Institutional Content
> **Reference:** Page 7
> **Content:** Side-Pocketing: Isolating risky/defaulted assets from rest of portfolio. Protects new investors and allows existing investors to choose retention/sale of bad assets.

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept Summary:** A regulatory mechanism allowing debt mutual funds to segregate toxic, defaulted debt assets from the healthy portfolio.
- **Formula/Rule:** Standard guidelines apply.
- **Application Context:** No specific application context available.

### 🔍 Detailed View
When a debt issuer defaults or is downgraded below investment grade:
1. **Split:** The AMC splits the fund's units into a Main portfolio (healthy assets) and a Segregated portfolio (bad debt).
2. **Valuation:** The segregated portfolio is valued at zero or heavily written down.
3. **Redemptions:** No new subscriptions or redemptions are allowed in the segregated portfolio.
4. **Recovery:** If the defaulted company repays in the future, the recovered money is paid back to the original unit holders.
