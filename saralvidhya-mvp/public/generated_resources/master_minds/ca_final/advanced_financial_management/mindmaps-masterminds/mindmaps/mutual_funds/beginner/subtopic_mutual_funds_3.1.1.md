# Sub-Topic 3.1.1: Definition & Structure

> **Reference:** Page 1, Page 2

---

### 📋 Summary View

#### 🔸 Mutual Fund
- **Concept:** A mutual fund is a trust that collects money from many investors with a common investment goal and invests that pooled money in shares, bonds, or other securities.
- **Analogy:** A group of friends pooling money to buy a shared Netflix subscription instead of paying the full price individually.
- **Key Takeaway:** It is a shared pool of money managed by an expert.

#### 🔸 Entities in a Mutual Fund
- **Concept:** A mutual fund is run by four key entities: the Sponsor (founder), the Trust (guardian), the AMC (manager), and the Custodian (safekeeper).
- **Analogy:** A school where the Sponsor is the founder, the Trustees are the management board, the AMC is the team of teachers, and the Custodian is the security guard who holds the keys to the lockers.
- **Key Takeaway:** Different entities handle different jobs to keep your money safe.

#### 🔸 Sponsor Net Worth Requirement
- **Concept:** The Sponsor must have a solid track record and contribute at least 40% of the AMC's minimum net worth (which must be at least ₹10 Crore).
- **Analogy:** A bank requiring a builder to invest a huge portion of their own money in a project before giving a construction loan.
- **Key Takeaway:** The promoter must invest their own money to prove they are serious.

---

### 🔍 Detailed View

#### 🔸 Mutual Fund
A mutual fund acts as a link between savers and the financial markets. Instead of buying shares of one or two companies, your money is spread across 30 to 100 companies. This reduces risk. In India, mutual funds are registered with the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI). When you invest, you receive "units" representing your share of the fund. The value of each unit is called Net Asset Value (NAV), which goes up or down depending on the performance of the underlying assets.

#### 🔸 Entities in a Mutual Fund
To prevent fraud, SEBI mandates this 4-tier structure:
1. **Sponsor:** The promoter who sets up the mutual fund (usually a reputed bank or financial institution).
2. **Trustee:** A group of people who act as guardians. They make sure the fund is run honestly and protect unit-holders' interests.
3. **Asset Management Company (AMC):** The office that actually runs the fund, decides where to invest, and hires the Fund Manager.
4. **Custodian:** An independent bank that physically holds the securities (shares/bonds) so the AMC cannot run away with them.

#### 🔸 Sponsor Net Worth Requirement
SEBI ensures that only serious and financially strong companies can start mutual funds. The Sponsor is required to fund at least 40% of the AMC's equity capital. Because the minimum required net worth for an AMC in India is ₹10 Crore, the Sponsor must put in at least ₹4 Crore of their own money. This ensures "skin in the game" and protects retail investors from fake or unstable financial companies.
