### Quantitative Modeling & Mathematical Context

> **Model Parameters:** No specific quantitative model details.

**Explanation:** This relationship is measured by Delta ($\Delta$). For a Call option, the derivative of option price ($V$) with respect to stock price ($S$) is positive ($\frac{\partial V}{\partial S} > 0$). For Puts, it is negative ($\frac{\partial V}{\partial S} < 0$).
**Worked Example:** A Call option has a Delta of 0.60. If the stock price rises by ₹20, the call option premium will increase by approximately ₹12 (₹20 * 0.60), assuming other variables remain unchanged.
