# European Option

> **Sub-Topic 2.1.2:** Exercise Styles | **Chapter:** Derivatives Options | **Level:** Beginner

--- 

### 📝 Source Institutional Content
> **Reference:** Page 2
> **Content:** European Option: Can only be exercised on expiration date.

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept:** A European Option can only be exercised on the *exact day of expiration*, not a day before.
- **Analogy:** A train ticket that is only valid for travel on one specific date and time.
- **Key Takeaway:** European = Exercise ONLY on the last day.

### 🔍 Detailed View
**Explanation:** Despite the name, European options are the standard style traded on the Indian stock market (NSE and BSE) for indices like Nifty and Bank Nifty (denoted as 'CE' for Call European and 'PE' for Put European). Although you cannot *exercise* them early, you can always buy or sell them to other traders on the exchange at any time before expiry to close your position.
**Worked Example:** You buy a Nifty European Put option (PE) expiring on the last Thursday of the month. Even if Nifty crashes on the second Thursday, you cannot force the seller to buy Nifty from you that day. However, you can sell your option contract back to the market for a huge profit because its market premium has increased.
