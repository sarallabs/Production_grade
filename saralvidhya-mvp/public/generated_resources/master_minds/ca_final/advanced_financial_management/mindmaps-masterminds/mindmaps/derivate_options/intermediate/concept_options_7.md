### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept Summary:** A contract style that can only be exercised on the final expiration date.
- **Formula/Rule:** Standard guidelines apply.
- **Application Context:** No specific application context available.

### 🔍 Detailed View
**Explanation:** On the NSE, all equity options are European style (denoted as CE and PE). This simplifies pricing because the time-to-expiry ($T$) is fixed. Although you cannot exercise them early, you can close out your trade at any time by selling/buying back the contract on the exchange.
**Worked Example:** You buy a Nifty 22,000 PE. If Nifty falls to 21,500 mid-week, you cannot exercise it to sell Nifty. However, you can sell the option contract back to another trader at its current high market premium on your Zerodha/AngelOne app to lock in profits.
