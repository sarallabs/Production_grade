### 📋 Summary View
- **Strategic Context:** **Concept Summary:** The Call Seller assumes a short volatility ($\text{Vega} < 0$) and short convexity ($\text{Gamma} < 0$) position. They collect premium income but face uncapped directional risk.
**Key Takeaway:** Writing calls involves harvesting time value (positive Theta) while accepting significant tail-risk exposure.
**Analogy:** Underwriting catastrophic insurance policies for coastal properties in Orissa. The insurer collects steady premiums but faces massive liabilities if a severe cyclone hits.
- **Quantitative Model:** No specific quantitative model details.
- **Risk/Opportunity:** No specific risk/opportunity details.

### 🔍 Detailed View
**Explanation:** Call writers are exposed to "unlimited risk" because there is no upper limit on stock prices. The writer's position has a negative Gamma ($\Gamma < 0$), meaning that as the stock price rises, the position's Delta becomes increasingly negative, compounding losses.
**Worked Example:** You write an naked Call option on Nifty at strike ₹22,000 for a ₹100 premium. If Nifty jumps to ₹23,000 due to an unexpected policy announcement, you must deliver Nifty at ₹22,000. Your loss is ₹900 per unit (₹1,000 market difference - ₹100 premium collected), requiring immediate margin cash to avoid broker liquidation.
