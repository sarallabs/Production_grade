### 📋 Summary View
- **Strategic Context:** **Concept Summary:** The institutional creation/redemption process managed by Authorized Participants (APs) through an "in-kind" exchange of assets.
**Key Takeaway:** Prevents price divergence from NAV through arbitrage-driven creation and redemption of units.
**Analogy:** A market marker exchanging baskets of shares directly for ETF units at a designated clearinghouse.
- **Quantitative Model:** No specific quantitative model details.
- **Risk/Opportunity:** No specific risk/opportunity details.

### 🔍 Detailed View
The creation process is institutional:
1. **Arbitrage Opportunity:** If the ETF trades at a premium to NAV on the exchange, the AP buys the underlying shares in the spot market.
2. **In-Kind Delivery:** The AP delivers these shares to the ETF Custodian.
3. **Unit Creation:** The ETF Trust issues "Creation Units" (large blocks of ETF units) to the AP.
4. **Supply Increase:** The AP sells these ETF units on the stock exchange, driving the price back down to NAV.
5. **Redemption:** The reverse process occurs if the ETF trades at a discount.
