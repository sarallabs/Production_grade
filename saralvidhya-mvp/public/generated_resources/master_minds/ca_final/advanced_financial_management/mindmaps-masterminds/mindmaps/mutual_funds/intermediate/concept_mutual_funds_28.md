# Thematic Funds

> **Sub-Topic 3.2.4:** Specialized Strategies | **Chapter:** Mutual Funds | **Level:** Intermediate

--- 

### 📝 Source Institutional Content
> **Reference:** Page 7
> **Content:** Thematic Funds: Invest around a theme (e.g., Manufacturing) across connected sectors. More diversified than sector funds.

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept Summary:** Equity funds investing in multiple sectors that are linked by a common economic theme (e.g., ESG, Infrastructure, Consumption).
- **Formula/Rule:** Standard guidelines apply.
- **Application Context:** No specific application context available.

### 🔍 Detailed View
SEBI mandates a minimum 80% investment in stocks matching the theme. An Infrastructure theme will invest in Cement, Steel, Capital Goods, Power, and Banking. This provides better diversification than a pure sector fund, but the portfolio remains vulnerable to theme-specific downturns (e.g., government spending cuts).
