# Multi-Cap Funds

> **Sub-Topic 3.2.2:** Equity Funds | **Chapter:** Mutual Funds | **Level:** Advanced

--- 

### 📝 Source Institutional Content

### 📋 Summary View
- **Strategic Context:** **Concept Summary:** Equity funds with a mandatory allocation of at least 25% each in Large-cap, Mid-cap, and Small-cap stocks.
**Key Takeaway:** Structured capitalization diversification, limiting the manager's ability to run style-drift portfolios.
**Analogy:** A multi-factor portfolio with static risk-weight constraints across capitalization buckets.
- **Quantitative Model:** No specific quantitative model details.
- **Risk/Opportunity:** No specific risk/opportunity details.

### 🔍 Detailed View
Under SEBI's 2020 mandate, Multi-Cap funds must allocate:
Large-cap: Minimum 25% of total assets.
Mid-cap: Minimum 25% of total assets.
Small-cap: Minimum 25% of total assets.
The remaining 25% is tactical. This structure forces the fund to maintain a high allocation to smaller, illiquid companies (minimum 50% combined in mid and small-caps), which increases the portfolio's systematic risk and liquidity risk compared to large-cap portfolios.
