# Capital Protection Funds

> **Sub-Topic 3.2.3:** Debt & Other Funds | **Chapter:** Mutual Funds | **Level:** Intermediate

--- 

### 📝 Source Institutional Content
> **Reference:** Page 5
> **Content:** Capital Protection: Closed-end hybrid. Debt portion ensures capital return at maturity; equity adds returns.

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept Summary:** Closed-end hybrid funds structured to protect the principal investment through debt allocation while boosting yield via minor equity allocation.
- **Formula/Rule:** Standard guidelines apply.
- **Application Context:** No specific application context available.

### 🔍 Detailed View
The portfolio is structured as follows:
**Debt Portion:** Approximately $100 / (1 + r)^t$ is invested in high-quality zero-coupon debt instruments, where $r$ is the yield and $t$ is the tenure. This portion grows to exactly ₹100 (principal) at maturity.
**Equity Portion:** The remaining amount is invested in equities or options. Any gain here is pure profit. The fund is rated by credit agencies to ensure capital protection probability is high.
