# Diversified Equity Funds

> **Sub-Topic 3.2.2:** Equity Funds | **Chapter:** Mutual Funds | **Level:** Beginner

--- 

### 📝 Source Institutional Content
> **Reference:** Page 4
> **Content:** Diversified: Invest across wide range of sectors/caps.

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept:** Funds that invest in a wide range of companies across different industries and company sizes.
- **Analogy:** A mixed-fruit basket containing apples, bananas, grapes, and oranges, so if one fruit spoils, the rest are fine.
- **Key Takeaway:** Spreads risk widely across the entire economy.

### 🔍 Detailed View
Diversified funds do not limit themselves to one industry. The manager buys stocks from banking, IT, pharmaceuticals, automobiles, etc. This ensures that even if one sector is suffering (e.g., auto sales are down), other sectors (like IT or pharma) can support the portfolio's performance.
