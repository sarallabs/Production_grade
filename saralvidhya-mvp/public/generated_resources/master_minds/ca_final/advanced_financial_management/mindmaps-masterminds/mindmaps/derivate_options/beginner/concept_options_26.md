# Rho

> **Sub-Topic 2.4.1:** Sensitivity Measures | **Chapter:** Derivatives Options | **Level:** Beginner

--- 

### 📝 Source Institutional Content
> **Reference:** Page 4
> **Content:** Rho: Change in option value per 1% change in interest rate.

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept:** Rho measures how much the option price changes when interest rates in the economy change by 1%.
- **Analogy:** How much the price of a house changes because bank home loan rates changed by a tiny fraction.
- **Key Takeaway:** Rho has very little impact on short-term options trading.

### 🔍 Detailed View
**Explanation:** Rho measures interest rate sensitivity. Since most retail options expire in a week or a month, interest rate changes have negligible impact on their pricing. It only matters for long-term options (called LEAPS) that expire in a year or more.
**Worked Example:** If an option has a Rho of 0.05 and the RBI unexpectedly hikes interest rates by 1%, the premium of the option will increase by a tiny ₹0.05.
