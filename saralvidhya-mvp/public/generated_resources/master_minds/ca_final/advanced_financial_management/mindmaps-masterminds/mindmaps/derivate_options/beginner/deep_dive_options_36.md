## 🔬 Advanced Context & Institutional Insights

In deep dive mode, we explore the institutional application and regulatory background of this concept. Under SEBI rules, Indian derivatives are subject to standard margins (SPAN + Exposure) and are tightly regulated to ensure systemic safety.

### Quantitative Modeling & Mathematical Context

> **Model Parameters:** No specific quantitative model details.

**Explanation:** While stock price, strike, interest rate, and time are known facts, volatility is a forecast. If a trader estimates historical volatility is 20% but future volatility turns out to be 35%, their options will be mispriced.
**Worked Example:** A trader uses a 15-day historical volatility average to price a Nifty option. Suddenly, the RBI announces an unscheduled interest rate meeting. Volatility spikes, making the trader's pricing model obsolete.
