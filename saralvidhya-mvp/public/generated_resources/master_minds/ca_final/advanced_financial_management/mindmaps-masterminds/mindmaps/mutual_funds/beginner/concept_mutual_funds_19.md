# Money Market Funds

> **Sub-Topic 3.2.3:** Debt & Other Funds | **Chapter:** Mutual Funds | **Level:** Beginner

--- 

### 📝 Source Institutional Content
> **Reference:** Page 4
> **Content:** Money Market: Invest in instruments with maturity < 1 year (T-bills, CP, CD). Low volatility.

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept:** Debt funds that invest in very short-term, high-safety debt instruments that mature in less than one year.
- **Analogy:** Keeping money in a digital wallet that earns daily interest and can be withdrawn anytime.
- **Key Takeaway:** Extremely low risk and highly liquid; a good alternative to bank savings accounts.

### 🔍 Detailed View
Money Market funds invest in safe, short-term papers like Treasury Bills (T-Bills), Commercial Paper (CP), and Certificates of Deposit (CD). Since these papers mature quickly (often in 90 to 365 days), the risk of losing money is extremely low, and interest rates are highly stable.
