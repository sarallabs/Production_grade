### 📋 Summary View
- **Strategic Context:** **Concept Summary:** Multi-sector equity funds designed to eliminate idiosyncratic (stock-specific) risk through broad diversification.
**Key Takeaway:** A core portfolio allocation tool designed to capture the broad equity risk premium.
**Analogy:** A global multi-sector index portfolio like the MSCI World Index.
- **Quantitative Model:** No specific quantitative model details.
- **Risk/Opportunity:** No specific risk/opportunity details.

### 🔍 Detailed View
The manager uses bottom-up stock selection combined with top-down sectoral allocations. The portfolio tracking error is typically kept moderate (3% to 5%) relative to a broad index like the Nifty 500. This ensures that the portfolio return is dominated by systematic market exposure rather than extreme sector bets.
