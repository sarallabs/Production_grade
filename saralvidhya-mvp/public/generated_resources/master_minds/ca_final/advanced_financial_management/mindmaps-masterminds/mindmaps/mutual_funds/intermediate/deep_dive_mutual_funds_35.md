## 🔬 Advanced Context & Institutional Insights

In deep dive mode, we explore the institutional application and regulatory background of this concept. Under SEBI rules, Indian derivatives are subject to standard margins (SPAN + Exposure) and are tightly regulated to ensure systemic safety.

### Quantitative Modeling & Mathematical Context

> **Model Parameters:** No specific quantitative model details.

**Bid-Ask Spread Drag:** In illiquid ETFs, the spread between the buying price and selling price acts as an implicit fee.
**NAV Deviations:** In highly volatile markets, the exchange trading price can diverge from the actual NAV (premium or discount), introducing tracking risk.
**Trading Costs:** Every trade incurs brokerage, exchange transaction charges, GST, SEBI turnover fees, and Securities Transaction Tax (STT).
