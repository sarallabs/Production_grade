# Sector Funds

> **Sub-Topic 3.2.4:** Specialized Strategies | **Chapter:** Mutual Funds | **Level:** Beginner

--- 

### 📝 Source Institutional Content
> **Reference:** Page 7
> **Content:** Sector Funds: Invest in specific sector (e.g., Banking). High volatility.

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept:** Funds that invest only in companies of one specific industry (e.g., Banking, Technology, or Pharma).
- **Analogy:** Putting all your money into a single crop (like cotton) on a farm: a good season makes you rich, a pest attack ruins you.
- **Key Takeaway:** Very high risk and high return; not recommended for beginners.

### 🔍 Detailed View
Sector funds are highly concentrated. If you buy a Banking Sector Fund, all your money goes into banks. If the banking industry faces bad loans, your fund will crash. Beginners should avoid sector funds because they lack the diversification needed to protect savings.
