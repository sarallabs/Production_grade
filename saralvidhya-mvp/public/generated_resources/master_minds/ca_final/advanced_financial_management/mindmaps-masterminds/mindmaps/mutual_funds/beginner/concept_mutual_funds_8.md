# Interval Funds

> **Sub-Topic 3.2.1:** By Function | **Chapter:** Mutual Funds | **Level:** Beginner

--- 

### 📝 Source Institutional Content
> **Reference:** Page 3
> **Content:** Interval: Combines features, open for sale/redemption only at pre-determined intervals.

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept:** Funds that combine features of open-ended and closed-ended funds. They open for buying and selling only during specific pre-set time windows.
- **Analogy:** A school admission window that opens only for 10 days every term.
- **Key Takeaway:** Semi-flexible funds that allow transactions only at set intervals.

### 🔍 Detailed View
Interval funds remain closed-ended for most of the year but become open-ended for brief periods (intervals) determined by the AMC (e.g., for 1 week every quarter). During this window, you can purchase new units or redeem existing units at the current NAV without paying brokerage on stock exchanges.
