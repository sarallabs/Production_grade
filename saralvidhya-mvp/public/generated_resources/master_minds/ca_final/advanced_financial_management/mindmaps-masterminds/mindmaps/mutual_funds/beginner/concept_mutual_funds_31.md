# International Funds

> **Sub-Topic 3.2.4:** Specialized Strategies | **Chapter:** Mutual Funds | **Level:** Beginner

--- 

### 📝 Source Institutional Content
> **Reference:** Page 4
> **Content:** International Funds: Indian registered, invest in global markets. For Indian investors seeking geographical diversification.

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept:** Indian mutual funds that invest in stocks of foreign companies (like Apple, Microsoft, or Google).
- **Analogy:** An Indian resident buying global brand shares through a local agent.
- **Key Takeaway:** Allows Indian investors to diversify their money globally and profit from foreign currencies like the US Dollar.

### 🔍 Detailed View
International funds collect Indian Rupees, convert them into US Dollars or Euros, and invest in global stock exchanges (like NASDAQ). This protects your portfolio from a domestic market downturn in India and gives you a hedge against the depreciation of the Indian Rupee.
