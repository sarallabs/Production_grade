### 📝 Source Institutional Content
> **Reference:** Page 2, Table
> **Content:** Counterparty Risk: Forwards (Borne by client) vs. Futures (Shouldered by clearing corporation).

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept Summary:** Futures carry negligible counterparty risk due to clearinghouse novation and the settlement guarantee fund, while forwards expose parties to direct counterparty default risk.
- **Formula/Rule:** Counterparty Risk: Forwards (Borne by client) vs. Futures (Shouldered by clearing corporation).
- **Application Context:** Traded on Indian exchanges (NSE/BSE/MCX) with standard contract specifications (Nifty lot size of 25, Bank Nifty lot size of 15).

### 🔍 Detailed View
In the OTC forward market, if party A defaults, party B suffers a credit loss. In the futures market, the Clearing Corporation performs **Novation**:
$$\text{Party A} \longleftrightarrow \text{Party B} \quad \text{becomes} \quad \text{Party A} \longleftrightarrow \text{Clearing Corp} \longleftrightarrow \text{Party B}$$
By stepping into the middle of every trade, the clearinghouse guarantees performance. It mitigates this risk by enforcing daily MTM and maintaining a Settlement Guarantee Fund (SGF) financed by member deposits.
