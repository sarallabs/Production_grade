# Lower Investment

> **Sub-Topic 2.2.1:** Key Benefits | **Chapter:** Derivatives Options | **Level:** Beginner

--- 

### 📝 Source Institutional Content
> **Reference:** Page 1
> **Content:** Lower investment compared to futures.

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept:** Buying options requires much less money (capital) than trading futures or buying the actual stocks.
- **Analogy:** Renting a luxury wedding outfit for ₹5,000 instead of buying it for ₹1,00,000.
- **Key Takeaway:** You can trade stock movements with very small funds.

### 🔍 Detailed View
**Explanation:** Because you only pay the premium (which is a small fraction of the stock price), you don't need lakhs of rupees. This allows retail traders with small budgets to participate in stock price movements.
**Worked Example:** Buying 100 shares of Reliance at ₹3,000 costs ₹3,00,000. Buying a Reliance Future contract requires a margin of ₹60,000. But buying a Reliance Call option might only cost a premium of ₹5,000.
