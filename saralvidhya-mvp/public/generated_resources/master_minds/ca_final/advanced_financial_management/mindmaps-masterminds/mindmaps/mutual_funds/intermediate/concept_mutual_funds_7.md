# Closed-End Funds

> **Sub-Topic 3.2.1:** By Function | **Chapter:** Mutual Funds | **Level:** Intermediate

--- 

### 📝 Source Institutional Content
> **Reference:** Page 3
> **Content:** Closed-End: Fixed maturity (3-15 years), open only during NFO, traded on exchange thereafter.

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept Summary:** Funds with a fixed unit capital and a specified maturity period. Units can only be purchased during the New Fund Offer (NFO).
- **Formula/Rule:** Standard guidelines apply.
- **Application Context:** No specific application context available.

### 🔍 Detailed View
Once the NFO closes, the AMC does not issue or redeem units until maturity (e.g., 3 or 5 years). Units are listed on stock exchanges (NSE/BSE) to provide an exit route. However, these units often trade at a discount to their actual NAV due to low trading volumes and lack of liquidity on the exchange. The fund manager enjoys the advantage of not having to maintain cash buffers for redemptions, allowing 100% investment of assets.
