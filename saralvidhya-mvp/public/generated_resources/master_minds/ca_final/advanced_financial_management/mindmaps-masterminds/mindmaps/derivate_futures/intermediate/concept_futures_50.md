# Expiry Cycle & Last Thursday Rule

> **Sub-Topic 4.2:** Expiry & Rollover Methods | **Chapter:** Derivates Futures | **Level:** Intermediate

--- 

### 📝 Source Institutional Content
> **Reference:** Page 15, Expiry Chapter
> **Content:** NSE equity derivative contracts expire on the last Thursday of the respective contract month. (Ref: NSE Rules)

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept Summary:** The standardized expiration schedule for exchange-traded equity derivatives in India, set by NSE and BSE, occurring on the last Thursday of each contract month.
- **Formula/Rule:** Expiry Day = Last Thursday of Month T. If Last Thursday is a Holiday, Expiry Day = Wednesday before Last Thursday.
- **Application Context:** Trading platforms show monthly tickers: NEAR (current month), NEXT (middle month), and FAR (outer month). Near-month contract volume rises sharply and peaks close to expiry.

### 🔍 Detailed View
Contracts are categorized based on their time-to-expiration. Traders monitor the expiry schedule closely as margins can rise in the final week of a contract, and brokers might restrict trading to prevent accidental physical delivery obligations.
