# Regulation Differences

> **Sub-Topic 3.5.2:** Hedge Funds vs. Mutual Funds | **Chapter:** Mutual Funds | **Level:** Intermediate

--- 

### 📝 Source Institutional Content
> **Reference:** Page 8
> **Content:** Regulation: MF (Well regulated) vs. HF (Unregistered/Less regulated).

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept Summary:** Mutual funds are retail products under strict SEBI oversight, while Hedge Funds (AIFs) are private placement vehicles with flexible rules.
- **Formula/Rule:** Standard guidelines apply.
- **Application Context:** No specific application context available.

### 🔍 Detailed View
**Mutual Funds:** Highly regulated under SEBI (Mutual Funds) Regulations, 1996. Retail-focused, mandatory daily NAV disclosure, strict concentration limits (e.g., max 10% in a single company's stock).
**Hedge Funds (AIF Category III):** Regulated under SEBI (AIF) Regulations, 2012. Private placement only, minimum investment size of ₹1 Crore, lower frequency of disclosure, and flexible leverage rules.
