# Index Funds

> **Sub-Topic 3.2.4:** Specialized Strategies | **Chapter:** Mutual Funds | **Level:** Beginner

--- 

### 📝 Source Institutional Content
> **Reference:** Page 4
> **Content:** Index Funds: Passive funds mimicking market indices. Subject to tracking error.

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept:** Passive funds that copy a stock market index (like the Nifty 50 or Sensex) by buying the exact same stocks in the same proportion.
- **Analogy:** Photocopying the answers of the class topper instead of writing your own exam paper.
- **Key Takeaway:** A low-cost, simple way to match market returns.

### 🔍 Detailed View
Index funds do not try to beat the market; they only try to copy it. Because the fund manager does not need to do active research, the fees (Expense Ratio) are extremely low (often 0.1% to 0.2%). It is the simplest and safest way for beginners to start investing in the top companies of India.
