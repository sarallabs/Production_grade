# American Option

> **Sub-Topic 2.1.2:** Exercise Styles | **Chapter:** Derivatives Options | **Level:** Beginner

--- 

### 📝 Source Institutional Content
> **Reference:** Page 2
> **Content:** American Option: Can be exercised any time between purchase and expiration.

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept:** An American Option can be exercised (used) by the buyer at *any time* they want between the day they buy it and the final expiration date.
- **Analogy:** A theme park pass that you can use on any day you choose during the summer season.
- **Key Takeaway:** American = Exercise anytime before expiration.

### 🔍 Detailed View
**Explanation:** American options give the buyer maximum flexibility. If you see a good profit opportunity on day 5 of a 30-day contract, you don't have to wait. You can exercise your option immediately to claim your shares or cash.
**Worked Example:** You buy an American-style Call option on a stock expiring in 3 weeks. On day 10, the company announces great earnings and the stock price spikes. You can exercise your option immediately on day 10 to lock in profits, rather than waiting for the 3 weeks to end.
