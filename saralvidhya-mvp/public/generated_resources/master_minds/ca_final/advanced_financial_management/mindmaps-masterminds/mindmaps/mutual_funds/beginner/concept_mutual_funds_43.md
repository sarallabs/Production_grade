# Valuation of Non-Traded Securities

> **Sub-Topic 3.4.3:** SEBI Regulations | **Chapter:** Mutual Funds | **Level:** Beginner

--- 

### 📝 Source Institutional Content
> **Reference:** Page 9
> **Content:** Valuation of Non-Traded Securities: Equity (Capitalization of earnings/NAV with liquidity discount); Debt (YTM basis).

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept:** The rules used by AMCs to calculate the fair price of assets that are not active or traded daily in the market.
- **Analogy:** Valuing an old family house; since houses aren't sold daily, you hire a licensed surveyor to estimate its fair market value based on recent local sales.
- **Key Takeaway:** Ensures NAV is calculated fairly using strict SEBI formulas.

### 🔍 Detailed View
If a mutual fund holds shares or bonds that are not actively traded on stock exchanges, it cannot use market prices. SEBI mandates that:
**Non-Traded Equities:** Must be valued based on earnings capitalization or net worth, with a liquidity discount.
**Non-Traded Debt:** Must be valued using Yield to Maturity (YTM) and valuation matrices provided by independent agencies (like CRISIL or ICRA) to prevent the AMC from artificially inflating its NAV.
