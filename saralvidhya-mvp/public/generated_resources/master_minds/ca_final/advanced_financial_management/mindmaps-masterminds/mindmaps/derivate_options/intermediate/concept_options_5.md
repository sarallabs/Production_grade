### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept Summary:** The Put Seller collects the premium but is obligated to buy the underlying asset if the buyer exercises the option.
- **Formula/Rule:** Standard guidelines apply.
- **Application Context:** No specific application context available.

### 🔍 Detailed View
**Explanation:** Put writing is used by institutional investors to buy quality stocks at a discount. By writing an out-of-the-money put, they either collect the premium (if the stock stays high) or are forced to buy the stock at a net cost lower than the original market price.
**Worked Example:** You sell a Put Option of TCS at strike ₹4,000 for a ₹100 premium. If TCS stays above ₹4,000, you earn ₹100. If TCS drops to ₹3,800, you must buy TCS at ₹4,000. Your net purchase price is ₹3,900 (Strike ₹4,000 - Premium ₹100), which is still cheaper than buying it at the original price of ₹4,050.
