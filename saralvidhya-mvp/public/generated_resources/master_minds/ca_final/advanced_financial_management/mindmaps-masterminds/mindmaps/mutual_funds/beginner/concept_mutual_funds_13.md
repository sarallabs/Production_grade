# Multi-Cap Funds

> **Sub-Topic 3.2.2:** Equity Funds | **Chapter:** Mutual Funds | **Level:** Beginner

--- 

### 📝 Source Institutional Content
> **Reference:** Page 4
> **Content:** Multi-Cap: Allocate min 25% each to large, mid, small cap (75% total equity).

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept:** Funds that are mandated to invest at least 25% of their money in large companies, 25% in medium companies, and 25% in small companies.
- **Analogy:** A balanced diet containing a fixed portion of carbs, proteins, and vitamins.
- **Key Takeaway:** Structured allocation across all company sizes to balance stability and high growth.

### 🔍 Detailed View
SEBI rules require Multi-Cap funds to invest:
Minimum 25% in Large-Cap (top 100 stable companies).
Minimum 25% in Mid-Cap (medium-sized growing companies).
Minimum 25% in Small-Cap (small, high-growth but risky companies).
This ensures that the portfolio always has a fixed blend of safety (large-cap) and high growth potential (mid/small-cap).
