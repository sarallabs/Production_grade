### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept Summary:** A plan where the AMC reinvests all capital gains and dividends back into the scheme to compound the NAV.
- **Formula/Rule:** Standard guidelines apply.
- **Application Context:** No specific application context available.

### 🔍 Detailed View
In the growth plan, the NAV reflects the total value of the assets. No payouts are made. Tax efficiency: Capital gains are only taxed when you redeem the units.
**Short-Term Capital Gains (STCG):** Held < 12 months (Equity) = 15% tax.
**Long-Term Capital Gains (LTCG):** Held > 12 months (Equity) = 10% tax on gains exceeding ₹1 Lakh. (Note: standard pre-July 2024 rates for academic context).
