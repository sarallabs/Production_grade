# Delta

> **Sub-Topic 2.4.1:** Sensitivity Measures | **Chapter:** Derivatives Options | **Level:** Beginner

--- 

### 📝 Source Institutional Content
> **Reference:** Page 4
> **Content:** Delta: Change in option premium per unit change in underlying price. (Call: 0 to +1; Put: -1 to 0).

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept:** Delta tells you how much the option premium will change if the stock price moves by ₹1.
- **Analogy:** How many kilometers a car travels for every liter of petrol.
- **Key Takeaway:** Delta is the speed meter of the option premium.

### 🔍 Detailed View
**Explanation:** Delta ranges from 0 to 1 for Call options (as stock goes up, call goes up) and -1 to 0 for Put options (as stock goes up, put goes down). A Delta of 0.50 means the option premium will move by ₹0.50 for every ₹1 move in the stock.
**Worked Example:** You buy a Nifty Call option with a Delta of 0.60 for a premium of ₹100. If Nifty rises by ₹10, your option premium will rise by ₹6 (₹10 * 0.60) to ₹106.
