### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept Summary:** Forwards are bilateral Over-the-Counter (OTC) transactions, whereas Futures are standardized contracts traded on regulated centralized exchanges.
- **Formula/Rule:** Trading: Forwards (Over phone/OTC) vs. Futures (Exchange).
- **Application Context:** Traded on Indian exchanges (NSE/BSE/MCX) with standard contract specifications (Nifty lot size of 25, Bank Nifty lot size of 15).

### 🔍 Detailed View
The trading venue dictates regulatory oversight and transaction mechanics:
**Forwards (OTC)**: Bilateral contracts negotiated directly between counterparties (often via banks or brokers). There is no centralized order book, leading to search costs and opaque pricing.
**Futures (Exchange-Traded)**: Traded on exchanges like the National Stock Exchange of India (NSE). Trading occurs via an electronic limit order book, providing price transparency, instantaneous execution, and public data reporting.
