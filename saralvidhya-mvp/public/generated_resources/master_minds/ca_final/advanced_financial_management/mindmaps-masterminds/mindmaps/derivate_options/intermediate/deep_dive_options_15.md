### Quantitative Modeling & Mathematical Context

> **Model Parameters:** No specific quantitative model details.

**Explanation:** By combining long and short options at different strike prices and expirations, traders can construct strategies like Spreads (Bull Call, Bear Put), Butterflies, Condors, and Straddles to target specific market views.
**Worked Example:** Bull Call Spread: You buy a ₹22,000 Nifty Call for ₹200 and sell a ₹22,200 Nifty Call for ₹80. Your net debit is ₹120. This caps your maximum loss at ₹120 and caps your maximum profit at ₹80 (Difference in strikes ₹200 - Net premium ₹120), creating a defined-risk trade.
