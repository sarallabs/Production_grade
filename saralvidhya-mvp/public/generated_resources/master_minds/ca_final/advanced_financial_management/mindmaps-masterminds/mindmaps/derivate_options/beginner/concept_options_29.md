# No Restrictions on Short Selling

> **Sub-Topic 2.5.1:** Assumptions | **Chapter:** Derivatives Options | **Level:** Beginner

--- 

### 📝 Source Institutional Content
> **Reference:** Page 3
> **Content:** No restrictions on short selling.

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept:** The model assumes you can easily borrow and sell shares you don't own (short selling) without any restrictions or extra costs.
- **Analogy:** Assuming you can borrow your neighbor's bike anytime to sell it, and buy it back later without any fee.
- **Key Takeaway:** BSM assumes perfect freedom to bet against a stock.

### 🔍 Detailed View
**Explanation:** In real Indian markets, retail traders face strict rules, margins, and limitations when short selling shares, especially overnight. BSM ignores these real-world frictions.
**Worked Example:** A trader wants to create a risk-free trade by shorting the stock and buying calls. In BSM, this is free and instant. In reality, the broker charges lending fees to borrow the stock, making the trade less profitable than BSM predicts.
