# Fund of Funds (FOF)

> **Sub-Topic 3.2.4:** Specialized Strategies | **Chapter:** Mutual Funds | **Level:** Beginner

--- 

### 📝 Source Institutional Content
> **Reference:** Page 7
> **Content:** Fund of Funds (FOF): Invest in other mutual funds. Higher expense ratio due to double layering.

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept:** A mutual fund that does not buy stocks directly, but instead invests in other mutual funds.
- **Analogy:** Hiring an agent to pick the best travel packages for you, instead of buying the tickets yourself.
- **Key Takeaway:** Convenient access to multiple funds, but charges higher overall fees.

### 🔍 Detailed View
A Fund of Funds offers ultimate diversification. However, since you are buying a fund that buys other funds, you pay two levels of management fees: one fee for the FOF itself and another fee for the underlying funds. This double layering of fees can reduce your final returns.
