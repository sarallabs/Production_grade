# MTM (Mark-to-Market)

> **Sub-Topic 2.1.3:** Futures vs. Options | **Chapter:** Derivatives Options | **Level:** Beginner

--- 

### 📝 Source Institutional Content
> **Reference:** Page 1
> **Content:** MTM: Futures (Yes) vs. Options (No).

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept:** Futures require daily cash settlement of profits and losses (Mark-to-Market). Options buyers do not have daily MTM settlements.
- **Analogy:** Futures is like a prepaid taxi meter ticking every second. Options buying is like buying a one-time bus ticket.
- **Key Takeaway:** Futures = Daily cash adjustments. Options Buyers = No daily cash deductions from your account.

### 🔍 Detailed View
**Explanation:** In futures, at the end of every trading day, the exchange calculates your profit or loss and adjusts your trading account balance. If you are losing, money is debited immediately. For option buyers, you pay the premium once at the start, and no daily MTM money is taken from your account.
**Worked Example:** You buy Nifty Futures. If Nifty drops by 100 points today, ₹2,500 is immediately cut from your broker account today. If you buy a Nifty option instead, even if Nifty drops, no money is cut from your account; only the market value of your option paper drops, which can rise again tomorrow.
