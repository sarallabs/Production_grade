# Sub-Topic 3.4.3: SEBI Regulations

> **Reference:** Page 7, Page 9

---

### 📋 Summary View

#### 🔸 Side-Pocketing
- **Concept:** A mechanism that allows mutual funds to separate bad/defaulted bonds from healthy assets in a debt fund.
- **Analogy:** A basket of apples where one apple starts rotting. You put the rotten apple in a separate plastic bag (side pocket) so it does not spoil the healthy apples.
- **Key Takeaway:** Prevents a single bad debt from ruining the entire mutual fund.

#### 🔸 Total Expense Ratio (TER) & Direct vs. Regular Plans
- **Concept:** The annual fees charged by the AMC to manage your money, capped by SEBI. It includes the critical choice between Direct and Regular plans.
- **Analogy:** Buying vegetables directly from a farmer (Direct Plan) versus buying from a local grocery store that charges a distributor markup (Regular Plan).
- **Key Takeaway:** Lower expenses mean higher returns. Direct plans are always cheaper than Regular plans.

#### 🔸 Valuation of Non-Traded Securities
- **Concept:** The rules used by AMCs to calculate the fair price of assets that are not active or traded daily in the market.
- **Analogy:** Valuing an old family house; since houses aren't sold daily, you hire a licensed surveyor to estimate its fair market value based on recent local sales.
- **Key Takeaway:** Ensures NAV is calculated fairly using strict SEBI formulas.

---

### 🔍 Detailed View

#### 🔸 Side-Pocketing
When a company defaults on its loan payments, its bond value crashes to zero. To protect investors, the AMC splits the fund into two:
1. **Main Portfolio:** Holds all the healthy, safe investments. Trading and withdrawals continue normally here.
2. **Side-Pocket Portfolio:** Holds the bad debt. This is locked. If the company pays back the money in the future, the recovered cash is distributed to the old investors. New investors entering the fund do not get a share in this bad asset.

#### 🔸 Total Expense Ratio (TER) & Direct vs. Regular Plans
The Total Expense Ratio (TER) covers management fees, administration, and marketing. SEBI caps this fee (e.g., max 2.25% for equity schemes, reducing as the fund size grows).
**Regular Plan:** You invest through an agent/broker. The AMC pays a commission to the broker from your fund value. This results in a higher TER (e.g., 2.0%).
**Direct Plan:** You invest directly on the AMC website. No brokers are involved, so the AMC pays no commission. This leads to a lower TER (e.g., 1.0%). Over 20 years, choosing a Direct Plan can save you lakhs of rupees in commission fees.

#### 🔸 Valuation of Non-Traded Securities
If a mutual fund holds shares or bonds that are not actively traded on stock exchanges, it cannot use market prices. SEBI mandates that:
**Non-Traded Equities:** Must be valued based on earnings capitalization or net worth, with a liquidity discount.
**Non-Traded Debt:** Must be valued using Yield to Maturity (YTM) and valuation matrices provided by independent agencies (like CRISIL or ICRA) to prevent the AMC from artificially inflating its NAV.
