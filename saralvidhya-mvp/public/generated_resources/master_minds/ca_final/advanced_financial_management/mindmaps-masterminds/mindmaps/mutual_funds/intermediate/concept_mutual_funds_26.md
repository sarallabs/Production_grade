> **Content:** Arbitrage Funds: Exploit mispricing between cash and futures markets. Low risk, tax-efficient (equity taxation).

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept Summary:** Funds exploiting price differentials between the cash market and the futures market for the same stock.
- **Formula/Rule:** Standard guidelines apply.
- **Application Context:** No specific application context available.

### 🔍 Detailed View
If Stock A trades at ₹100 in the cash market and the 1-month futures contract trades at ₹100.80, the fund buys the stock and shorts the futures contract, locking in an 80 paise (0.8%) risk-free return. At contract expiry, the prices converge. Classified as equity because the fund maintains a minimum 65% equity exposure (long stocks + short futures).
