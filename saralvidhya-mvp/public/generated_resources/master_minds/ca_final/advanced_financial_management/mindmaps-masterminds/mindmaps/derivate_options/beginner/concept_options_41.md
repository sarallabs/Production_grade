# Seller Payoff: -1 * Buyer Payoff

> **Sub-Topic 2.6.1:** Formulas | **Chapter:** Derivatives Options | **Level:** Beginner

--- 

### 📝 Source Institutional Content
> **Reference:** Page 2
> **Content:** Seller Payoff: -1 * Buyer Payoff.

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept:** The option seller's profit is the exact opposite of the option buyer's payoff. The buyer's gain is the seller's loss, and vice versa.
- **Analogy:** Betting on a coin toss with a friend. If you win ₹10, your friend must lose ₹10.
- **Key Takeaway:** Options are a zero-sum game (what one wins, the other loses).

### 🔍 Detailed View
**Explanation:** The seller collects the premium upfront. Their maximum profit is this premium. If the buyer makes a massive profit, that profit is paid directly out of the seller's pocket, leading to unlimited risk for the seller.
**Worked Example:** You sell a Call option for a ₹100 premium.
If the buyer's final payoff is ₹200, your payoff is -₹200. Net Loss = -₹200 + ₹100 premium collected = -₹100.
If the buyer's final payoff is 0 (stock didn't move), your payoff is 0. Net Profit = 0 + ₹100 premium collected = +₹100.
