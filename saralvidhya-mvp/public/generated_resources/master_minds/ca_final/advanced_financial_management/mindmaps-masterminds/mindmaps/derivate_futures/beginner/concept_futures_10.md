# Market Dealers and Market Makers

> **Sub-Topic 1.1:** Core Definitions | **Chapter:** Derivates Futures | **Level:** Beginner

--- 

### 📝 Source Institutional Content
> **Reference:** Page 1, User (d)
> **Content:** Dealers: Provide two-way quotes, hedge positions, exploit inefficiencies/arbitrage.

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept:** Dealers and market makers act like wholesalers. They are always ready to buy and sell derivatives, making sure there is always a buyer for every seller. They make money on the small difference between their buy and sell prices.
- **Analogy:** Think of a local gold jeweler in a market. They are always ready to buy old gold from you or sell new gold to you. They maintain a small margin (spread) between their buying and selling rates to make a profit.
- **Key Takeaway:** Dealers provide liquidity, ensuring you can enter or exit a trade instantly at a fair price.

### 🔍 Detailed View
Dealers are the financial intermediaries. They provide continuous 'two-way quotes' (buy price and sell price). If you want to buy Nifty options, a dealer is on the other side selling it. To avoid taking too much risk, they immediately hedge their position in another market, or lock in risk-free profits by exploiting tiny price differences between different exchanges.
