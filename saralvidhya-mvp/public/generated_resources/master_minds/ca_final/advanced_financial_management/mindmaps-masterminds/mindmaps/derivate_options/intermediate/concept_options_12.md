# Loss Visibility

> **Sub-Topic 2.1.3:** Futures vs. Options | **Chapter:** Derivatives Options | **Level:** Intermediate

--- 

### 📝 Source Institutional Content
> **Reference:** Page 1
> **Content:** Loss Visibility: Futures (Unknown in advance) vs. Options (Known in advance for buyer).

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept Summary:** Option buyers have 100% loss visibility at entry (limited to premium). Future traders face theoretically unlimited losses with no pre-defined cap.
- **Formula/Rule:** Standard guidelines apply.
- **Application Context:** No specific application context available.

### 🔍 Detailed View
**Explanation:** Because an option buyer can choose not to exercise, the worst-case scenario is letting the premium decay to zero. In futures, a black swan event (like a war or political event) can cause the stock to gap down below your stop-loss, causing massive slippage and unexpected losses.
**Worked Example:** A trader holds Nifty futures overnight. An international crisis causes Nifty to open 5% down the next morning. The trader's stop-loss is skipped, and they lose ₹27,500 per lot. If they had bought a Call option, their loss would be strictly capped at the premium paid (e.g., ₹5,000).
