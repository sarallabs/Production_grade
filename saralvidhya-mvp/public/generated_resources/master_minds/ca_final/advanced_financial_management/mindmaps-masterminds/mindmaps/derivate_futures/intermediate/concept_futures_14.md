### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept Summary:** Futures require initial and maintenance margin deposits subject to daily marking-to-market, whereas forwards typically involve credit extensions with no upfront margin.
- **Formula/Rule:** Margin: Forwards (No margin) vs. Futures (Requires margin).
- **Application Context:** Traded on Indian exchanges (NSE/BSE/MCX) with standard contract specifications (Nifty lot size of 25, Bank Nifty lot size of 15).

### 🔍 Detailed View
Margin systems protect the financial system from default risk:
**Forwards**: Generally rely on mutual credit lines. No money changes hands at inception. The performance risk is carried until the settlement date.
**Futures**: Requires an **Initial Margin** (usually 15-20% of contract value) to open a position. If the equity in the account falls below the **Maintenance Margin** due to adverse price movements, the broker issues a margin call to restore the account balance to the initial margin level.
