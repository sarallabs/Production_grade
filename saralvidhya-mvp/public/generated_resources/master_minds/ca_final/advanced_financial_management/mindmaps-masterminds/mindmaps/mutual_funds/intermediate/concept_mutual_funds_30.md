> **Content:** Offshore Funds: Registered outside India, invest in India. For non-residents.

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept Summary:** Funds registered in foreign jurisdictions (like Mauritius) to pool foreign capital for investment in Indian securities.
- **Formula/Rule:** Standard guidelines apply.
- **Application Context:** No specific application context available.

### 🔍 Detailed View
These funds operate outside India (offshore) but invest in Indian assets. They are structured to optimize tax treaties (e.g., double taxation avoidance agreements). They must comply with both their home country regulators and SEBI's FPI guidelines.
