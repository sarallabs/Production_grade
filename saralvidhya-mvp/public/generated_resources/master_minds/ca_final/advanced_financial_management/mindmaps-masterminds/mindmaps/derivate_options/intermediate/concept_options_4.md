### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept Summary:** The Call Seller collects the premium but is obligated to deliver/sell the underlying asset if the buyer exercises the option.
- **Formula/Rule:** Standard guidelines apply.
- **Application Context:** No specific application context available.

### 🔍 Detailed View
**Explanation:** Call writing is a high-risk strategy that requires depositing margin (often ₹1 Lakh+ per lot on NSE). Writers exploit time decay (Theta) and falling volatility. They believe the underlying price will stay below the strike price plus premium collected.
**Worked Example:** You sell a Call Option of Infosys at strike ₹1,600 for a ₹30 premium. If Infosys expires at ₹1,580, you keep the entire ₹30. If it surges to ₹1,700, you are forced to deliver the shares at ₹1,600. Your loss is ₹70 per share (₹100 loss on delivery - ₹30 premium collected).
