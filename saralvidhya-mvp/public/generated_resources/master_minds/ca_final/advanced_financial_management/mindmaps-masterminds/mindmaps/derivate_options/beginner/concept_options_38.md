# Misprices Options on High-Dividend Stocks

> **Sub-Topic 2.5.2:** Limitations | **Chapter:** Derivatives Options | **Level:** Beginner

--- 

### 📝 Source Institutional Content
> **Reference:** Page 3
> **Content:** Misprices options on high-dividend stocks.

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept:** Stocks that pay large, irregular dividends are frequently mispriced by standard BSM.
- **Analogy:** Valuing a rental property without subtracting the tax deductions the owner takes out every quarter.
- **Key Takeaway:** Do not rely on basic BSM for high-dividend PSU stocks.

### 🔍 Detailed View
**Explanation:** Companies like Coal India, ONGC, or RECL pay massive dividends. The standard BSM formula doesn't have a variable for dividend yield, leading to distorted option values.
**Worked Example:** A PSU stock trading at ₹250 is about to pay a ₹15 dividend. The call option at ₹250 strike should drop in value because the stock will drop to ₹235. Standard BSM will show the call option is worth much more than it actually is because it doesn't know about the dividend.
