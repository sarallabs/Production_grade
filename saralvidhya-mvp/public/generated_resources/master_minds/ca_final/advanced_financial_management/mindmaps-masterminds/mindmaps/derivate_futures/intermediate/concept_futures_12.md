### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept Summary:** Forwards allow fully customized terms, while Futures feature standardized lot sizes, quality specifications, and fixed expiration dates defined by the exchange.
- **Formula/Rule:** Contract Terms: Forwards (Customized) vs. Futures (Standardized).
- **Application Context:** Traded on Indian exchanges (NSE/BSE/MCX) with standard contract specifications (Nifty lot size of 25, Bank Nifty lot size of 15).

### 🔍 Detailed View
Standardization is a prerequisite for exchange trading:
**Forwards**: Parties negotiate contract size, underlying quality/grade, delivery location, and maturity date to fit their exact commercial needs.
**Futures**: The exchange defines all specifications:
**Lot Size**: Nifty 50 is 25, Bank Nifty is 15.
**Expiry Cycle**: Weekly or monthly (always the last Thursday of the month for equity derivatives on the NSE).
**Tick Size**: The minimum price movement (₹0.05 on NSE).
