# Total Expense Ratio (TER) & Direct vs. Regular Plans

> **Sub-Topic 3.4.3:** SEBI Regulations | **Chapter:** Mutual Funds | **Level:** Beginner

--- 

### 📝 Source Institutional Content
> **Reference:** Page 9
> **Content:** TER (Total Expense Ratio): Max permissible up to 2.50% of average daily net assets.

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept:** The annual fees charged by the AMC to manage your money, capped by SEBI. It includes the critical choice between Direct and Regular plans.
- **Analogy:** Buying vegetables directly from a farmer (Direct Plan) versus buying from a local grocery store that charges a distributor markup (Regular Plan).
- **Key Takeaway:** Lower expenses mean higher returns. Direct plans are always cheaper than Regular plans.

### 🔍 Detailed View
The Total Expense Ratio (TER) covers management fees, administration, and marketing. SEBI caps this fee (e.g., max 2.25% for equity schemes, reducing as the fund size grows).
**Regular Plan:** You invest through an agent/broker. The AMC pays a commission to the broker from your fund value. This results in a higher TER (e.g., 2.0%).
**Direct Plan:** You invest directly on the AMC website. No brokers are involved, so the AMC pays no commission. This leads to a lower TER (e.g., 1.0%). Over 20 years, choosing a Direct Plan can save you lakhs of rupees in commission fees.
