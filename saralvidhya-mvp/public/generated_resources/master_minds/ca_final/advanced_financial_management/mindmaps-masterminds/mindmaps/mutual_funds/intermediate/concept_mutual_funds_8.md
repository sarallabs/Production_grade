### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept Summary:** Hybrid funds that combine features of open and closed-end funds, opening for transaction windows at predetermined intervals.
- **Formula/Rule:** Standard guidelines apply.
- **Application Context:** No specific application context available.

### 🔍 Detailed View
These funds remain closed-ended for the majority of their tenure but open for subscription and redemption directly with the AMC for a short period (e.g., 2 to 5 business days) at regular intervals (e.g., monthly, quarterly, or semi-annually). This allows managers to buy semi-illiquid, higher-yielding debt papers without fearing sudden redemptions.
