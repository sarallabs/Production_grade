# Dividend Yield Funds

> **Sub-Topic 3.2.2:** Equity Funds | **Chapter:** Mutual Funds | **Level:** Beginner

--- 

### 📝 Source Institutional Content
> **Reference:** Page 4
> **Content:** Dividend Yield: Invest in companies with strong financials and regular dividends.

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept:** Funds that invest primarily in companies that pay high dividends relative to their stock price.
- **Analogy:** A high-interest savings account that pays you regular interest cash.
- **Key Takeaway:** Focuses on safe, cash-generating companies that pay regular dividends.

### 🔍 Detailed View
According to SEBI, a Dividend Yield fund must invest at least 65% of its assets in dividend-yielding stocks. These companies are typically mature, have strong cash flows, and are less likely to experience huge price crashes, making these funds a conservative option within equity investing.
