# Seller (Writer) of Call

--- 

### 📝 Source Institutional Content
> **Reference:** Page 2
> **Content:** Seller (Writer) of Call: Obligation to sell if buyer exercises.

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept:** The Seller (Writer) of a Call option collects the premium upfront but is *obligated* to sell the stock if the buyer decides to exercise their right.
- **Analogy:** A real estate broker who takes a non-refundable token from a buyer and is legally forced to sell the house at that locked price if the buyer returns with the remaining money.
- **Key Takeaway:** Call Seller = Takes fee, must sell if buyer asks.

### 🔍 Detailed View
**Explanation:** Selling a call option is also known as "writing" a call. You do this if you believe the stock price will *not* rise. You pocket the premium immediately. However, if the stock price rises dramatically, you are forced to sell the stock at the lower strike price to the buyer, which can result in massive losses.
**Worked Example:** You sell a Call Option of ITC at a strike price of ₹450 and collect a ₹10 premium. If ITC stays below ₹450, the buyer walks away, and you keep the ₹10. But if ITC jumps to ₹500, you must sell ITC at ₹450, losing ₹40 net (₹50 market difference - ₹10 premium collected).
