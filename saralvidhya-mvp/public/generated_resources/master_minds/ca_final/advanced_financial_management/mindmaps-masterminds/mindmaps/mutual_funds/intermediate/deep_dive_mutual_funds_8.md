## 🔬 Advanced Context & Institutional Insights

In deep dive mode, we explore the institutional application and regulatory background of this concept. Under SEBI rules, Indian derivatives are subject to standard margins (SPAN + Exposure) and are tightly regulated to ensure systemic safety.

### Quantitative Modeling & Mathematical Context

> **Model Parameters:** No specific quantitative model details.

The interval periods (e.g., 3 days every quarter) are defined in the Scheme Information Document. During these windows, investors can buy or redeem units at NAV directly from the AMC. The fund manager structures the portfolio's debt maturities to align with these windows, utilizing instruments like commercial papers and short-term deposits that mature just before the interval date to provide liquidity.
