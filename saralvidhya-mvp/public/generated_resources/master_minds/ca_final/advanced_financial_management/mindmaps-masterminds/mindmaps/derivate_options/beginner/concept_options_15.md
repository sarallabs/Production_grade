# Expanded Opportunity Set

> **Sub-Topic 2.2.1:** Key Benefits | **Chapter:** Derivatives Options | **Level:** Beginner

--- 

### 📝 Source Institutional Content
> **Reference:** Page 1
> **Content:** Expanded opportunity set for strategies (risk-return combinations).

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept:** Options let you make money in any market condition: rising, falling, or even when the market is moving sideways (flat).
- **Analogy:** A multi-terrain SUV that can drive on roads, sand, mud, and snow.
- **Key Takeaway:** You can design strategies for any view of the market.

### 🔍 Detailed View
**Explanation:** If you buy stocks, you only make money when they go up. In options, you can buy Puts to profit from falls, or combine calls and puts to make money when a stock stays completely flat (which happens 70% of the time).
**Worked Example:** Before a budget announcement, if you expect a huge move but don't know if it will go up or down, you can buy both a Call and a Put option (a strategy called a Straddle). If Nifty moves big in either direction, the profit from one side will easily cover the loss of the other.
