### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept Summary:** A plan where profits realized by the fund are distributed as cash to the investor's bank account.
- **Formula/Rule:** Standard guidelines apply.
- **Application Context:** No specific application context available.

### 🔍 Detailed View
Under current tax rules, mutual fund dividends (IDCW payouts) are added to the investor's individual income and taxed at their marginal income tax slab rate (which can be up to 30% + surcharge). Additionally, the AMC deducts 10% TDS (Tax Deducted at Source) on dividend payouts exceeding ₹5,000 in a financial year. The NAV of the fund drops by the exact amount of the dividend distributed.
