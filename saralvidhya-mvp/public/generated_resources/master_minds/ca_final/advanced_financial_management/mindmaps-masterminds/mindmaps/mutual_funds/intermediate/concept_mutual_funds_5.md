# Drawbacks of Mutual Funds

> **Sub-Topic 3.1.2:** Benefits vs. Drawbacks | **Chapter:** Mutual Funds | **Level:** Intermediate

--- 

### 📝 Source Institutional Content
> **Reference:** Page 2
> **Content:** Drawbacks: Market risk, no control over securities, fund manager performance risk, lower returns than direct stock picking, reliance on past performance, management fees reduce returns.

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept Summary:** Limitations include market/systematic risk, management fees (TER), lack of control over stock selection, and the potential for manager underperformance (active risk).
- **Formula/Rule:** Standard guidelines apply.
- **Application Context:** No specific application context available.

### 🔍 Detailed View
**Systematic Risk:** Diversification cannot eliminate market-wide risk (e.g., recessions, geopolitical crises).
**Cost Drag:** The Total Expense Ratio (TER) is charged regardless of whether the fund makes profit or loss, dragging down compounding returns.
**Style Drift:** Managers may deviate from the stated mandate (mitigated by SEBI's 2017 categorization).
**Tax Inefficiencies:** Investors cannot control the timing of capital gains realizations within the fund, which can lead to capital gains distributions (under IDCW).
