# Physical vs. Cash Settlement

> **Sub-Topic 4.2:** Expiry & Rollover Methods | **Chapter:** Derivates Futures | **Level:** Intermediate

--- 

### 📝 Source Institutional Content
> **Reference:** Page 17, Regulations Chapter
> **Content:** SEBI mandate requiring physical settlement for stock derivatives and cash settlement for index derivatives. (Ref: SEBI circulars)

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept Summary:** The mechanical process of fulfilling derivatives contracts: physical settlement requires delivery of underlying shares into demat accounts; cash settlement computes the net monetary difference at expiry.
- **Formula/Rule:** For Physical Settlement: Buyer pays (Lot Size * Strike Price) and receives shares. Seller delivers shares and receives cash. For Cash Settlement: Net Cash Flow = (Closing Spot Price - Entry Price) * Lot Size.
- **Application Context:** Brokers impose heavy physical delivery margins (up to 40-50% of contract value) starting from the Monday of expiry week to prevent defaults in stock futures.

### 🔍 Detailed View
Since SEBI's mandate, physical settlement has increased capital requirements for retail traders. If a trader fails to maintain sufficient funds or shares for delivery, they face auction penalties and broker liquidations.
