### 📋 Summary View

- **Formula/Rule:** Standard guidelines apply.
- **Application Context:** No specific application context available.

### 🔍 Detailed View
Rupee Cost Averaging works because the investment amount is fixed:
When markets are low, the NAV is low, and the SIP purchases **more units**.
When markets are high, the NAV is high, and the SIP purchases **fewer units**.
Mathematically, the average cost per unit of the investor will be lower than the simple average of NAVs over the period. This is the harmonic mean of the NAVs.
