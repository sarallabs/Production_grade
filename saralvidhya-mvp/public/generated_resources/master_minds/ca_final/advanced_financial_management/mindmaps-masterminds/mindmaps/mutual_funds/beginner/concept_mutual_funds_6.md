# Open-End Funds

> **Sub-Topic 3.2.1:** By Function | **Chapter:** Mutual Funds | **Level:** Beginner

--- 

### 📝 Source Institutional Content
> **Reference:** Page 3
> **Content:** Open-End: Available for subscription/repurchase all year, no fixed maturity, key feature is liquidity.

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept:** Funds that allow investors to buy or sell units at any time of the year. They do not have a fixed end date.
- **Analogy:** A public bus where passengers can board or get off at any bus stop along the route.
- **Key Takeaway:** These funds are highly flexible and offer easy entry and exit.

### 🔍 Detailed View
Open-ended funds do not have a fixed maturity date. You can invest today and withdraw tomorrow (though some funds charge an "exit load" fee if you withdraw within a few months). The AMC continuously issues new units when people invest and cancels units when people withdraw. The buying and selling happen directly with the AMC at the daily NAV.
