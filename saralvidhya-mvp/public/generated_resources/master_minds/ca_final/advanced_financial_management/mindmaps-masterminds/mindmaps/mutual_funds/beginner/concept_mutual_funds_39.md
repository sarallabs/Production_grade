# SIP (Systematic Investment Plan)

> **Sub-Topic 3.4.2:** SIP & SWP | **Chapter:** Mutual Funds | **Level:** Beginner

--- 

### 📝 Source Institutional Content
> **Reference:** Page 8
> **Content:** SIP (Systematic Investment Plan): Fixed amount invested regularly. Benefits: Rupee cost averaging, compounding.

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept:** An investment method where you invest a fixed amount of money at regular intervals (e.g., ₹500 every month).
- **Analogy:** Buying gold every month for ₹1,000; you get more grams when gold is cheap and fewer grams when gold is expensive.
- **Key Takeaway:** Promotes disciplined saving and lowers purchase cost through Rupee Cost Averaging.

### 🔍 Detailed View
SIP is not a fund itself, but a method of investing. It helps in:
1. **Rupee Cost Averaging:** Since you invest a fixed amount, you automatically buy more units when the market is low (prices are cheap) and fewer units when the market is high.
2. **Compounding:** Small amounts invested regularly over 15-20 years can grow into a massive corpus.
3. **Discipline:** Money is automatically auto-debited from your bank account on a fixed date.
