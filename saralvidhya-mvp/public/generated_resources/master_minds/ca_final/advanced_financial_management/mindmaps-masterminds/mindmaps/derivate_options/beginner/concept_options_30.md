# No Commissions or Taxes

> **Sub-Topic 2.5.1:** Assumptions | **Chapter:** Derivatives Options | **Level:** Beginner

--- 

### 📝 Source Institutional Content
> **Reference:** Page 3
> **Content:** No commissions/taxes.

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept:** The model assumes there are zero brokerage fees, clearing fees, or government taxes when buying or selling.
- **Analogy:** Assuming you can buy vegetables at wholesale prices without paying for transport or local market entry taxes.
- **Key Takeaway:** BSM assumes a friction-free trading world.

### 🔍 Detailed View
**Explanation:** Every trade in India incurs GST, STT, exchange transaction charges, and broker fees. BSM assumes you can trade continuously for free, which is impossible in real life.
**Worked Example:** If BSM shows a risk-free arbitrage profit of ₹2 per share by buying and selling quickly, a real trader might find that brokerages and taxes cost ₹2.50 per share, turning the expected profit into a loss.
