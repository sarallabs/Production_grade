# Theta

> **Sub-Topic 2.4.1:** Sensitivity Measures | **Chapter:** Derivatives Options | **Level:** Beginner

--- 

### 📝 Source Institutional Content
> **Reference:** Page 4
> **Content:** Theta: Rate of decrease in option premium per day decrease in time to expiry.

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept:** Theta measures the rate of time decay—how much value the option loses every single day as it gets closer to expiry.
- **Analogy:** An hourglass where sand is constantly slipping away.
- **Key Takeaway:** Theta is negative for option buyers, reducing your option value daily.

### 🔍 Detailed View
**Explanation:** Theta is the cost of holding an option. Every day that passes, the option loses some premium, even if the stock price does not move at all. Theta decay speeds up dramatically during the final week before expiration.
**Worked Example:** You buy a Nifty Call option for ₹150. The Theta is -₹5. If Nifty stays exactly flat over the weekend (Saturday and Sunday), when the market opens on Monday, your option premium will have dropped by ₹10 (2 days * ₹5) to ₹140.
