### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept Summary:** The process of creating ETF units via Authorized Participants (APs) who deliver the underlying basket of stocks in exchange for ETF units.
- **Formula/Rule:** Standard guidelines apply.
- **Application Context:** No specific application context available.

### 🔍 Detailed View
The creation process does not involve cash transactions with the fund:
1. **Index Composition:** The AP (typically a large institutional broker) checks the index weightings.
2. **Stock Purchasing:** The AP buys the underlying shares in the exact weightings on the stock exchange.
3. **In-Kind Swap:** The AP delivers this basket of shares to the ETF Custodian.
4. **Unit Issuance:** The ETF trust issues "Creation Units" (large blocks of ETF units, e.g., 50,000 units) to the AP.
5. **Retail Listing:** The AP sells these individual ETF units to retail investors on the NSE/BSE.
