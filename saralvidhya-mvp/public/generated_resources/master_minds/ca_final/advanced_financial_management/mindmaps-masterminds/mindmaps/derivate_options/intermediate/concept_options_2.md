### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept Summary:** A Call Option gives the holder the right to buy the underlying asset at the strike price. The buyer is bullish on the stock.
- **Formula/Rule:** Standard guidelines apply.
- **Application Context:** No specific application context available.

### 🔍 Detailed View
**Explanation:** The buyer of a call option has a long position in option contracts but a bullish outlook on the underlying. Call option premiums rise when the underlying asset's price rises, or when implied volatility rises, and decay as the expiration date approaches.
**Worked Example:** You buy 1 lot of Nifty 22,200 Call (CE) at a ₹150 premium (Lot size = 25). Your total outlay is ₹3,750. If Nifty rises to ₹22,500 at expiry, the option's value becomes ₹300. Your net profit is ₹150 per unit (₹3,750 total profit).
