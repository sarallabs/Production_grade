# Vega

> **Sub-Topic 2.4.1:** Sensitivity Measures | **Chapter:** Derivatives Options | **Level:** Beginner

--- 

### 📝 Source Institutional Content
> **Reference:** Page 4
> **Content:** Vega: Change in option premium per 1% change in implied volatility.

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept:** Vega measures how much the option premium changes when the stock's expected volatility changes by 1%.
- **Analogy:** How much the price of an umbrella rises when the weather forecast changes from cloudy to heavy rain.
- **Key Takeaway:** Vega shows how sensitive your option is to market fear or excitement.

### 🔍 Detailed View
**Explanation:** If the market gets nervous, volatility goes up, and Vega causes option premiums to swell. If the market calms down, volatility falls, and Vega causes premiums to shrink (even if stock prices stay flat).
**Worked Example:** You buy an option for ₹100 with a Vega of 4. If the market panic increases and Implied Volatility rises by 3%, your option premium will increase by ₹12 (3 * 4) to ₹112, even if the stock price hasn't moved.
