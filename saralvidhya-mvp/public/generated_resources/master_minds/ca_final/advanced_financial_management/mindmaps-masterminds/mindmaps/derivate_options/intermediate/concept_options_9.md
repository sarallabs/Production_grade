### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept Summary:** Futures have symmetrical pay-offs (unlimited profit/loss). Options have asymmetrical payoffs (limited loss for buyers, unlimited profit).
- **Formula/Rule:** Standard guidelines apply.
- **Application Context:** No specific application context available.

### 🔍 Detailed View
**Explanation:** The payoff graph of a Future is a straight line at a 45-degree angle. The payoff graph of an option is "bent" or "hockey-stick" shaped. The bend occurs at the strike price, where the option goes from worthless to intrinsically valuable.
**Worked Example:**
Long Future: If stock goes from ₹1,000 to ₹1,100, profit is +₹100. If it falls to ₹900, loss is -₹100.
Long Call Option (Strike ₹1,000, Premium ₹30): If stock goes to ₹1,100, profit is +₹70. If it falls to ₹900, loss is capped at -₹30.
