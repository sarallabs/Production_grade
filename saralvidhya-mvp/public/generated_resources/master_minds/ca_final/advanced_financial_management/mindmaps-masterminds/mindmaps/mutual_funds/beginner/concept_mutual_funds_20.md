# Capital Protection Funds

> **Sub-Topic 3.2.3:** Debt & Other Funds | **Chapter:** Mutual Funds | **Level:** Beginner

--- 

### 📝 Source Institutional Content
> **Reference:** Page 5
> **Content:** Capital Protection: Closed-end hybrid. Debt portion ensures capital return at maturity; equity adds returns.

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept:** Closed-ended hybrid funds that invest majorly in safe debt (to guarantee your capital back) and a small portion in equity (to generate extra return).
- **Analogy:** A fixed deposit where the bank uses the interest to buy lottery tickets; you get your principal back, plus a chance at a lottery win.
- **Key Takeaway:** Aims to ensure you don't lose your starting investment while offering some stock market gains.

### 🔍 Detailed View
For example, if you invest ₹100, the fund puts ₹80 in safe long-term government bonds. By the end of 5 years, that ₹80 grows back to ₹100. The remaining ₹20 is invested in the stock market. If stocks rise, you get a bonus return. If stocks crash, you still get your original ₹100 back.
