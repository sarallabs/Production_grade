### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept Summary:** A mutual fund is a SEBI-registered trust that pools capital from retail and institutional investors to invest in a diversified portfolio of securities based on a predefined investment objective.
- **Formula/Rule:** Standard guidelines apply.
- **Application Context:** No specific application context available.

### 🔍 Detailed View
In India, mutual funds are governed by the SEBI (Mutual Funds) Regulations, 1996. The fund issues units to investors, representing fractional ownership of the overall pool. The valuation of these units is represented by the Net Asset Value (NAV), calculated daily as:
$$\text{NAV} = \frac{\text{Market Value of Securities} + \text{Receivables} + \text{Accrued Income} - \text{Accrued Expenses} - \text{Liabilities}}{\text{Total Number of Outstanding Units}}$$
NAV is declared by 11:00 PM on all business days on the AMFI (Association of Mutual Funds in India) website.
