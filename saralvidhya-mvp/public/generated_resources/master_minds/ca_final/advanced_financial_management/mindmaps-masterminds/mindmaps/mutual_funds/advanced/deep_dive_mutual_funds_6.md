## 🔬 Advanced Context & Institutional Insights

In deep dive mode, we explore the institutional application and regulatory background of this concept. Under SEBI rules, Indian derivatives are subject to standard margins (SPAN + Exposure) and are tightly regulated to ensure systemic safety.

### Quantitative Modeling & Mathematical Context

> **Model Parameters:** No specific quantitative model details.

Open-ended funds have no cap on the number of units. The AMC must manage daily inflows and outflows. To manage redemption risk, managers must match portfolio liquidity with potential outflows. This requires maintaining a portion of assets in cash, cash equivalents (TREPS), or highly liquid blue-chip stocks. Large redemptions during panic periods can force the liquidation of liquid assets first, increasing the concentration of illiquid assets in the remaining portfolio.
