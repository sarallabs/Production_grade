# Constant Known Interest Rates

> **Sub-Topic 2.5.1:** Assumptions | **Chapter:** Derivatives Options | **Level:** Beginner

--- 

### 📝 Source Institutional Content
> **Reference:** Page 3
> **Content:** Constant known interest rates.

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept:** The model assumes that bank interest rates will remain completely unchanged throughout the life of the option.
- **Analogy:** Assuming your home loan EMI interest rate will never change for the next 20 years.
- **Key Takeaway:** BSM assumes interest rates are static.

### 🔍 Detailed View
**Explanation:** In the real economy, central banks (like the RBI) frequently change interest rates. Even market interest rates fluctuate daily. BSM ignores these shifts, which can affect long-term options.
**Worked Example:** If you buy a 1-year option and the RBI suddenly raises interest rates by 1.5% mid-year, the option's actual value will shift, but the BSM pricing model (which assumed a constant rate) will fail to predict this change.
