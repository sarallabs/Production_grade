> **Reference:** Page 2
> **Content:** American Option: Can be exercised any time between purchase and expiration.

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept Summary:** A contract style allowing the buyer to exercise the option at any point up to and including the expiration date.
- **Formula/Rule:** Standard guidelines apply.
- **Application Context:** No specific application context available.

### 🔍 Detailed View
**Explanation:** American options have path-dependent values. The pricing must account for early exercise opportunities, especially right before a dividend payout (to capture the dividend on the underlying stock) or when the option is deep in-the-money and interest rates are high.
**Worked Example:** You hold an American Call option on a stock with a strike of ₹500. The stock climbs to ₹650 and is about to pay a large dividend tomorrow. To capture the dividend, you exercise the option early today, converting it into shares before the stock price drops ex-dividend.
