# MTM (Mark-to-Market)

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept Summary:** Futures require daily marking to market with cash settlement. Options do not subject the buyer to daily MTM cash debit/credit cycles.
- **Formula/Rule:** Standard guidelines apply.
- **Application Context:** No specific application context available.

### 🔍 Detailed View
**Explanation:** Under SEBI rules, futures accounts must maintain SPAN + Exposure margins daily. If the market moves against a futures position, the trader gets a margin call and must deposit funds. Option buyers pay the premium once; their account balance is never debited for daily price swings.
**Worked Example:** You buy Nifty Futures. A sudden 2% drop on NSE means you must deposit ₹15,000 in cash by the next morning (margin call) to avoid auto-liquidation. If you had bought a Call option instead, no extra cash is required, regardless of the drop.
