# Time to Expiry (T)

> **Sub-Topic 2.3.1:** Five Variables | **Chapter:** Derivatives Options | **Level:** Beginner

--- 

### 📝 Source Institutional Content
> **Reference:** Page 3
> **Content:** Time to Expiry (T): Time passes -> Time value decreases for both Call and Put.

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept:** Options have an expiration date. As time passes, the option loses value. This is called time decay.
- **Analogy:** An ice cream melting in your hand. If you don't eat it soon, it turns to nothing.
- **Key Takeaway:** Time is the enemy of the option buyer.

### 🔍 Detailed View
**Explanation:** An option has a certain "time value" because there is a chance the stock price might move in your favor. As the expiration date approaches, that chance shrinks, so the option premium decays daily, even if the stock price doesn't move.
**Worked Example:** You buy a Call option with 30 days left for ₹100. If the stock price stays exactly the same for 15 days, your option premium will drop to around ₹60 just because time has run out. On the expiry day, the time value becomes zero.
