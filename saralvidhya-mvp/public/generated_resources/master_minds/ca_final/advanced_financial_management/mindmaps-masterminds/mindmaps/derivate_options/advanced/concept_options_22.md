# Delta

> **Sub-Topic 2.4.1:** Sensitivity Measures | **Chapter:** Derivatives Options | **Level:** Advanced

--- 

### 📝 Source Institutional Content
> **Reference:** Page 4
> **Content:** Delta: Change in option premium per unit change in underlying price. (Call: 0 to +1; Put: -1 to 0).

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Strategic Context:** **Concept Summary:** Delta ($\Delta$) is the rate of change of the option premium relative to a change in the price of the underlying asset.
**Key Takeaway:** Shows the directional exposure of the option. Call Delta: 0 to +1. Put Delta: -1 to 0.
**Analogy:** The slope of a hill. It tells you how many meters you rise for every meter you walk forward.
- **Quantitative Model:** No specific quantitative model details.
- **Risk/Opportunity:** No specific risk/opportunity details.

### 🔍 Detailed View
**Explanation:** Delta represents the equivalent share exposure of the option. An ATM option has a Delta of approximately 0.50 (Calls) or -0.50 (Puts). Deep ITM options approach a Delta of 1.00 (moving 1:1 with the stock), while deep OTM options approach 0.00.
**Worked Example:** You hold 10 lots of Nifty Call Options (250 units) with a Delta of 0.40. Your position behaves like you own 100 shares of Nifty ($250 \times 0.40 = 100$ shares). If Nifty rises by ₹50, your portfolio value increases by ₹5,000 ($100 \times \text{₹}50$).
