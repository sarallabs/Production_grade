# Thematic Funds

> **Sub-Topic 3.2.4:** Specialized Strategies | **Chapter:** Mutual Funds | **Level:** Beginner

--- 

### 📝 Source Institutional Content
> **Reference:** Page 7
> **Content:** Thematic Funds: Invest around a theme (e.g., Manufacturing) across connected sectors. More diversified than sector funds.

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept:** Funds that invest in a economic theme (e.g., Infrastructure or Manufacturing) that covers multiple related industries.
- **Analogy:** A "Clean Energy" theme that includes solar panel makers, battery manufacturers, electric car companies, and wind energy companies.
- **Key Takeaway:** Slightly safer than sector funds, but still higher risk than general diversified funds.

### 🔍 Detailed View
Thematic funds invest in a trend. For example, a "Rural Consumption" theme will invest in fertilizer companies, tractor makers, rural banks, and consumer goods companies. It is broader than a sector fund but still depends heavily on the success of that single theme.
