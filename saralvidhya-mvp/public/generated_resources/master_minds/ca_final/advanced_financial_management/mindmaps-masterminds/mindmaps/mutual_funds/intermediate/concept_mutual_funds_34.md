--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept Summary:** ETFs trade intraday on exchanges, offer lower expense ratios (due to no RTA/marketing costs), and allow limit orders.
- **Formula/Rule:** Standard guidelines apply.
- **Application Context:** No specific application context available.

### 🔍 Detailed View
**Intraday Trading:** Investors can buy/sell at fluctuating market prices throughout the day.
**Lower Expenses:** Passive ETFs do not pay distributor commissions or active management fees. Expense ratios are often $<0.20\%$.
**Trading Flexibility:** Supports limit orders, stop-losses, and short-selling.
