--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept:** Trading derivatives is often cheaper, faster, and more flexible than buying the actual assets. It also allows you to invest in things that are hard to buy physically, like crude oil or market indices.
- **Analogy:** Instead of buying and storing 100 heavy bars of gold in a home safe, you buy a gold contract on the exchange, saving on storage, security, and insurance costs.
- **Key Takeaway:** Derivatives offer lower transaction costs, greater execution speed, and access to hard-to-hold assets.

### 🔍 Detailed View
Buying 100 shares of different companies to replicate the Nifty 50 index would require high brokerage fees and taxes. Buying 1 lot of Nifty futures achieves the same exposure in a single trade with low fees. Additionally, trading oil or copper futures avoids the need to physically store and transport these commodities, improving market access.
