# Lower Transaction Costs

> **Sub-Topic 2.2.1:** Key Benefits | **Chapter:** Derivatives Options | **Level:** Advanced

### 📝 Source Institutional Content

> **Content:** Lower transaction costs compared to futures.

### 📋 Summary View
- **Strategic Context:** **Concept Summary:** Transaction fees, GST, and Securities Transaction Tax (STT) are calculated on the option premium, making transaction costs lower than futures.
**Key Takeaway:** Reduced transaction friction preserves trading capital.
**Analogy:** Toll tax calculated on the weight of a motorcycle vs. a multi-axle cargo truck.
- **Quantitative Model:** No specific quantitative model details.
- **Risk/Opportunity:** No specific risk/opportunity details.

### 🔍 Detailed View
**Explanation:** For stock futures, STT is charged at 0.0125% on the entire contract value. For options, STT is charged at 0.0625% but only on the *sell premium* value (unless exercised, where it is charged on intrinsic value). This makes active option trading much cheaper than futures.
**Worked Example:** Selling Nifty futures worth ₹11,00,000 incurs ~₹137 in STT. Selling a Nifty option lot for ₹5,000 premium incurs only ₹3.12 in STT, significantly reducing transaction costs.
