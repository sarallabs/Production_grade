# Simultaneous Price Movement

> **Sub-Topic 1.1:** Core Definitions | **Chapter:** Derivates Futures | **Level:** Beginner

--- 

### 📝 Source Institutional Content
> **Reference:** Page 1, Feature (b)
> **Content:** A change in underlying results in a simultaneous change in the derivative price.

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept:** When the market price of the actual asset changes, the price of the derivative changes at the exact same time.
- **Analogy:** If you look in a mirror and raise your hand, your reflection raises its hand at the exact same moment. There is no lag.
- **Key Takeaway:** There is no delay; when the stock moves, its derivative moves instantly.

### 🔍 Detailed View
In the stock market, computer programs link the cash market and the derivatives market. The moment a stock like TCS moves up by ₹1 on the BSE or NSE cash market, the TCS futures and options contracts adjust their prices instantly. This happens in milliseconds, ensuring that the two markets stay in perfect sync.
