# Sub-Topic 4.2: Expiry & Rollover Methods

> **Reference:** Page 15, Expiry Chapter, Page 16, Execution Chapter, Page 17, Regulations Chapter

---

### 📋 Summary View

#### 🔸 Expiry Cycle & Last Thursday Rule
- **Concept Summary:** The standardized expiration schedule for exchange-traded equity derivatives in India, set by NSE and BSE, occurring on the last Thursday of each contract month.
- **Formula/Rule:** Expiry Day = Last Thursday of Month T. If Last Thursday is a Holiday, Expiry Day = Wednesday before Last Thursday.
- **Application Context:** Trading platforms show monthly tickers: NEAR (current month), NEXT (middle month), and FAR (outer month). Near-month contract volume rises sharply and peaks close to expiry.

#### 🔸 Rollover of Futures Positions
- **Concept Summary:** The execution of a roll spread trade, simultaneously squaring off the near-month position and establishing an identical position in the mid-month or far-month contract.
- **Formula/Rule:** Roll Spread = Future Price (Next Month) - Future Price (Near Month). Positive roll spread = Contango; Negative roll spread = Backwardation.
- **Application Context:** Rollover transactions are typically executed in the final 3-5 days of the near-month contract. NSE reports rollover percentages daily (e.g. 70% rollover in Nifty futures is standard).

#### 🔸 Physical vs. Cash Settlement
- **Concept Summary:** The mechanical process of fulfilling derivatives contracts: physical settlement requires delivery of underlying shares into demat accounts; cash settlement computes the net monetary difference at expiry.
- **Formula/Rule:** For Physical Settlement: Buyer pays (Lot Size * Strike Price) and receives shares. Seller delivers shares and receives cash. For Cash Settlement: Net Cash Flow = (Closing Spot Price - Entry Price) * Lot Size.
- **Application Context:** Brokers impose heavy physical delivery margins (up to 40-50% of contract value) starting from the Monday of expiry week to prevent defaults in stock futures.

---

### 🔍 Detailed View

#### 🔸 Expiry Cycle & Last Thursday Rule
Contracts are categorized based on their time-to-expiration. Traders monitor the expiry schedule closely as margins can rise in the final week of a contract, and brokers might restrict trading to prevent accidental physical delivery obligations.

#### 🔸 Rollover of Futures Positions
Rollover percentage is a key metric. A high rollover percentage with positive roll spread indicates strong bullish sentiment carrying forward, whereas a low rollover suggests traders are closing positions rather than extending them.

#### 🔸 Physical vs. Cash Settlement
Since SEBI's mandate, physical settlement has increased capital requirements for retail traders. If a trader fails to maintain sufficient funds or shares for delivery, they face auction penalties and broker liquidations.
