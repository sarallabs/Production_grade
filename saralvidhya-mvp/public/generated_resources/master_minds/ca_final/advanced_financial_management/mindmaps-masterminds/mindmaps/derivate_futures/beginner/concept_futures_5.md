# Dependency of Value

> **Sub-Topic 1.1:** Core Definitions | **Chapter:** Derivates Futures | **Level:** Beginner

--- 

### 📝 Source Institutional Content
> **Reference:** Page 1, Feature (d)
> **Content:** Without the underlying, the derivative has no value.

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept:** A derivative is like a shadow. If you remove the person, the shadow disappears. If the underlying asset has no value, the derivative is completely worthless.
- **Analogy:** Think of a photo of a famous painting. If the painting is destroyed and forgotten, and people lose interest in the original, the photo also loses its significance and value.
- **Key Takeaway:** A derivative has zero intrinsic value of its own; it is entirely dependent on the underlying.

### 🔍 Detailed View
Imagine a contract that says you can buy a special stone from a specific quarry. If that quarry runs completely dry and the stones become worthless or unavailable, your contract is now just a useless piece of paper. The derivative only holds value because someone wants the underlying asset.
