### 🔍 Detailed View

#### 🔸 Mutual Fund
In India, mutual funds are governed by the SEBI (Mutual Funds) Regulations, 1996. The fund issues units to investors, representing fractional ownership of the overall pool. The valuation of these units is represented by the Net Asset Value (NAV), calculated daily as:
$$\text{NAV} = \frac{\text{Market Value of Securities} + \text{Receivables} + \text{Accrued Income} - \text{Accrued Expenses} - \text{Liabilities}}{\text{Total Number of Outstanding Units}}$$
NAV is declared by 11:00 PM on all business days on the AMFI (Association of Mutual Funds in India) website.

#### 🔸 Entities in a Mutual Fund
**Sponsor:** The promoter who applies for SEBI registration. Must have at least 5 years of financial services experience and a positive net worth.
**Board of Trustees:** Minimum 2/3rd must be independent. They hold the property of the fund in trust and monitor AMC's compliance with SEBI rules.
**Asset Management Company (AMC):** Appointed by Trustees. Must have a minimum net worth of ₹10 Crore. They manage the portfolio and charge management fees.
**Custodian:** Independent entity registered with SEBI that holds physical/electronic custody of securities bought by the AMC.
**Registrar & Transfer Agent (RTA):** Processes investor applications, redemptions, and maintains unit-holder accounts (e.g., CAMS, KFintech).

#### 🔸 Sponsor Net Worth Requirement
SEBI mandates that the Sponsor must contribute at least 40% of the net worth of the AMC. Since the minimum net worth of an AMC is ₹10 Crore (increased from ₹5 Crore earlier), the Sponsor's contribution must be at least ₹4 Crore. If the AMC net worth drops below ₹10 Crore, the Sponsor must inject capital. The Sponsor must also show profits in at least 3 of the immediately preceding 5 years (including the 5th year) to qualify.
