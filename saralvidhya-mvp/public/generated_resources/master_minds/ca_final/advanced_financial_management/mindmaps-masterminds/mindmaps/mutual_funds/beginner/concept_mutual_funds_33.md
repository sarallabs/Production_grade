# ETF Custodian

> **Sub-Topic 3.3.1:** ETF Mechanics | **Chapter:** Mutual Funds | **Level:** Beginner

--- 

### 📝 Source Institutional Content
> **Reference:** Page 5
> **Content:** Custodian: Keeps underlying securities safe, collects/pays dividends.

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept:** An independent financial institution that holds and safeguards the actual stocks that back the ETF units.
- **Analogy:** A bank locker keeper who holds the gold bars, while you carry a certificate saying you own the gold.
- **Key Takeaway:** The custodian ensures the ETF units are backed by real assets.

### 🔍 Detailed View
The Custodian holds physical custody of the shares, bonds, or gold representing the ETF. They make sure the ETF does not issue units without having the actual underlying assets in their vaults. They also collect dividends from the companies and transfer them to the ETF scheme.
