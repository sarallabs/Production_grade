# Gold Funds

> **Sub-Topic 3.2.3:** Debt & Other Funds | **Chapter:** Mutual Funds | **Level:** Beginner

--- 

### 📝 Source Institutional Content
> **Reference:** Page 5
> **Content:** Gold Funds: Invest in Gold ETFs. No demat needed. Hedge against inflation.

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept:** Mutual funds that invest in Gold Exchange Traded Funds (ETFs) to track the price of physical gold.
- **Analogy:** Buying a "Gold Card" from a jeweler that increases in value as the price of gold in the market goes up.
- **Key Takeaway:** Invest in gold electronically without worrying about lockers, purity, or high making charges.

### 🔍 Detailed View
Gold funds allow you to buy gold with as little as ₹100 through an SIP. You do not need a demat account. The AMC manages the gold backing paper electronically. This protects you from the theft risk of physical gold and the making charges (wastage) of buying gold jewelry.
