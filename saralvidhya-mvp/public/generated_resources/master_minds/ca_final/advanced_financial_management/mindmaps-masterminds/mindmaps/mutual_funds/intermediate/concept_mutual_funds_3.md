# Sponsor Net Worth Requirement

> **Sub-Topic 3.1.1:** Definition & Structure | **Chapter:** Mutual Funds | **Level:** Intermediate

--- 

### 📝 Source Institutional Content
> **Reference:** Page 2
> **Content:** Sponsor must contribute at least 40% of AMC minimum net worth (₹10 Cr).

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept Summary:** The Sponsor must possess a minimum 40% stake in the net worth of the AMC, which must be at least ₹10 Crore, ensuring long-term financial commitment.
- **Formula/Rule:** Standard guidelines apply.
- **Application Context:** No specific application context available.

### 🔍 Detailed View
SEBI mandates that the Sponsor must contribute at least 40% of the net worth of the AMC. Since the minimum net worth of an AMC is ₹10 Crore (increased from ₹5 Crore earlier), the Sponsor's contribution must be at least ₹4 Crore. If the AMC net worth drops below ₹10 Crore, the Sponsor must inject capital. The Sponsor must also show profits in at least 3 of the immediately preceding 5 years (including the 5th year) to qualify.
