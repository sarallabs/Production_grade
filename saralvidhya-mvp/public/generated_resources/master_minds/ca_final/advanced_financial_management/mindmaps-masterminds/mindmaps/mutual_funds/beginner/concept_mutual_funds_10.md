# Aggressive Funds

> **Sub-Topic 3.2.2:** Equity Funds | **Chapter:** Mutual Funds | **Level:** Beginner

--- 

### 📝 Source Institutional Content
> **Reference:** Page 3
> **Content:** Aggressive: Invest in high beta stocks/IPOs for frequent speculative gains.

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept:** Funds that invest in fast-moving, high-risk shares and initial public offerings (IPOs) to make quick profits.
- **Analogy:** Riding a sports bike at high speed: you reach faster, but the chance of an accident is much higher.
- **Key Takeaway:** High risk, but offers the potential for high short-term returns.

### 🔍 Detailed View
Aggressive funds buy stocks with high volatility (beta). They search for rapid price jumps and speculative opportunities. While they can perform exceptionally well in a rising market (bull market), they can also lose value very quickly if the stock market crashes. They are not suitable for conservative investors.
