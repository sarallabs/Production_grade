## 🔬 Advanced Context & Institutional Insights

In deep dive mode, we explore the institutional application and regulatory background of this concept. Under SEBI rules, Indian derivatives are subject to standard margins (SPAN + Exposure) and are tightly regulated to ensure systemic safety.

### Quantitative Modeling & Mathematical Context

> **Model Parameters:** No specific quantitative model details.

**Explanation:** In futures, both the long and short positions have a linear payoff ($S_T - F_0$ and $F_0 - S_T$ respectively). In options, the buyer's payoff is non-linear ($\max(S_T - X, 0)$ for Calls), which is purchased by paying a premium to the seller.
**Worked Example:** A portfolio manager hedges ₹1 Crore of equity. If they sell Nifty Futures, they lock in the portfolio value, but they will not benefit if the market rises. If they buy Nifty Puts instead, they protect their downside while retaining the ability to profit if the market surges.
