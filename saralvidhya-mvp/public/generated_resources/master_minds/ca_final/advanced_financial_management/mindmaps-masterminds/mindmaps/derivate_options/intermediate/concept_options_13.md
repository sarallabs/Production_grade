# Lower Investment

> **Sub-Topic 2.2.1:** Key Benefits | **Chapter:** Derivatives Options | **Level:** Intermediate

### 📝 Source Institutional Content
> **Reference:** Page 1
> **Content:** Lower investment compared to futures.

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept Summary:** Options allow retail traders to participate in high-value stock movements with a fraction of the capital needed for futures.
- **Formula/Rule:** Standard guidelines apply.
- **Application Context:** No specific application context available.

### 🔍 Detailed View
**Explanation:** Trading 1 lot of stock futures requires maintaining heavy margins (20-25% of contract value). Buying an option only requires paying the premium, which is often 2% to 5% of the total contract value.
**Worked Example:** 1 lot of Maruti Futures (65 shares) at ₹12,000 requires a margin of ₹1,60,000. An At-the-Money Maruti Call option with a premium of ₹200 only costs ₹13,000 (₹200 * 65 shares) to buy, representing a 92% reduction in initial capital.
