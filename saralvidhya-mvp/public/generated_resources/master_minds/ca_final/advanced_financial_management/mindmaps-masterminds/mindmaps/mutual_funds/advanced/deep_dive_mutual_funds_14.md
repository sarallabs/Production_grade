## 🔬 Advanced Context & Institutional Insights

In deep dive mode, we explore the institutional application and regulatory background of this concept. Under SEBI rules, Indian derivatives are subject to standard margins (SPAN + Exposure) and are tightly regulated to ensure systemic safety.

### Quantitative Modeling & Mathematical Context

> **Model Parameters:** No specific quantitative model details.

The manager adjusts allocations based on the valuation spreads between capitalization tiers (e.g., shifting to large-caps when small-cap valuations are at a historical premium). This category is highly popular among institutional managers because it avoids the forced illiquidity of the multi-cap mandate. The performance is heavily dependent on the manager's tactical asset allocation skill.
