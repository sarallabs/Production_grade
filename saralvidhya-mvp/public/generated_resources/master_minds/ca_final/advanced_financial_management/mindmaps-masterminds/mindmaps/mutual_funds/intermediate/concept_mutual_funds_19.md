> **Sub-Topic 3.2.3:** Debt & Other Funds | **Chapter:** Mutual Funds | **Level:** Intermediate

> **Reference:** Page 4
> **Content:** Money Market: Invest in instruments with maturity < 1 year (T-bills, CP, CD). Low volatility.

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept Summary:** Debt funds investing in money market instruments with a maturity of up to one year.
- **Formula/Rule:** Standard guidelines apply.
- **Application Context:** No specific application context available.

### 🔍 Detailed View
Under SEBI rules, these funds invest in instruments like Commercial Paper (CP), Certificates of Deposit (CD), Treasury Bills (T-Bills), and Tri-party Repo (TREPS) with maturities up to 1 year. Since maturity is short, interest rate risk is minimal, making them suitable for placing short-term business surplus funds.
