### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept Summary:** The cash market involves trading tangible financial assets, while the derivatives market trades contracts derived from those underlying assets.
- **Formula/Rule:** Asset Type: Cash (Actual assets) vs. Derivatives (Derivatives of underlying).
- **Application Context:** Traded on Indian exchanges (NSE/BSE/MCX) with standard contract specifications (Nifty lot size of 25, Bank Nifty lot size of 15).

### 🔍 Detailed View
The cash market (also called the spot market) is where real assets are bought and sold for immediate delivery and payment. The derivatives market trades contracts. The pricing of these contracts is mathematically linked to the spot price of the underlying asset. For example, if Nifty spot is at 22,000, Nifty futures is a contract whose price is determined by adding the cost of carry to the spot price.
