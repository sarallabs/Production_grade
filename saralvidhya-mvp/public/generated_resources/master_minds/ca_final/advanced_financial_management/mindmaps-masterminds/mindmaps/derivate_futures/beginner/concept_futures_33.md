# Arbitrage Opportunities

> **Sub-Topic 3.1:** Hedging & Price Discovery | **Chapter:** Derivates Futures | **Level:** Beginner

--- 

### 📝 Source Institutional Content
> **Reference:** Page 4
> **Content:** Arbitrage: Earn riskless profit when derivatives trade away from theoretical prices.

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept:** Arbitrage is when you buy an asset in a cheap market and sell it in an expensive market at the same time, locking in a risk-free profit.
- **Analogy:** If tomato prices are ₹40/kg in your local wholesale market and ₹60/kg in the retail market down the street, you can buy at the wholesale market and sell at the retail market to lock in a ₹20 profit.
- **Key Takeaway:** Arbitrage exploits pricing mismatches to secure riskless profits.

### 🔍 Detailed View
If the theoretical price of a Nifty futures contract is ₹22,050, but it is currently trading in the market at ₹22,100, an arbitrageur will buy Nifty shares in the cash market for ₹22,000 and sell Nifty futures at ₹22,100. This locks in a risk-free profit of ₹50 per contract, which is realized at expiry.
