### 📝 Source Institutional Content
> **Reference:** Page 8
> **Content:** Strategy: MF (Directional) vs. HF (Directional, Arbitrage, Spread).

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept Summary:** Mutual funds are generally long-only directional strategies, whereas Hedge Funds employ long-short, arbitrage, macro, and leveraged derivative strategies.
- **Formula/Rule:** Standard guidelines apply.
- **Application Context:** No specific application context available.

### 🔍 Detailed View
**Mutual Funds:** Must remain fully invested (cash limits apply), cannot short sell stocks (except minor hedging), and cannot borrow money to invest (leverage is banned except for meeting temporary redemption liquidity).
**Hedge Funds:** Can short-sell stocks, buy derivatives, use high leverage (up to 2 times the fund size in India), and take highly concentrated bets on single companies or themes.
