### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept Summary:** Rho ($\rho$) measures the sensitivity of the option value to a 1% change in the risk-free interest rate.
- **Formula/Rule:** Standard guidelines apply.
- **Application Context:** No specific application context available.

### 🔍 Detailed View
**Explanation:** Rho ($\rho = \frac{\partial V}{\partial r}$) is the derivative of option price with respect to interest rate. It is generally ignored by short-term option traders because interest rates change slowly and have minimal impact on options expiring within a month.
**Worked Example:** If you hold a long-term Nifty option expiring in 1 year with a Rho of 12.5 and a premium of ₹300, a 1% increase in the RBI repo rate will increase the option's premium by ₹12.50 to ₹312.50.
