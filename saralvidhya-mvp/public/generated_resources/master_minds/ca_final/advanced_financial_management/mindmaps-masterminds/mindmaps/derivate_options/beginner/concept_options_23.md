# Gamma

> **Sub-Topic 2.4.1:** Sensitivity Measures | **Chapter:** Derivatives Options | **Level:** Beginner

--- 

### 📝 Source Institutional Content
> **Reference:** Page 4
> **Content:** Gamma: Rate of change of Delta.

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept:** Gamma measures how fast the Delta changes when the stock price moves.
- **Analogy:** Pressing the accelerator pedal in a car. It tells you how fast your speed (Delta) is increasing.
- **Key Takeaway:** Gamma is the acceleration of the option price.

### 🔍 Detailed View
**Explanation:** Gamma is highest for options where the strike price is close to the current stock price (At-the-Money). It tells you how much the Delta will increase or decrease. High Gamma means the option's sensitivity to stock price changes is changing rapidly.
**Worked Example:** Your option has a Delta of 0.50 and a Gamma of 0.05. If the stock price rises by ₹1, your Delta increases by 0.05 to 0.55. Now, the next ₹1 rise in the stock will benefit you even more because your speed (Delta) has increased.
