> **Content:** Custodian: Keeps underlying securities safe, collects/pays dividends.

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept Summary:** An independent financial institution responsible for securing the physical and electronic assets backing the ETF.
- **Formula/Rule:** Standard guidelines apply.
- **Application Context:** No specific application context available.

### 🔍 Detailed View
The custodian holds the shares/bonds delivered by the APs. They ensure that for every ETF unit trading on the exchange, there is a corresponding asset held in the custody account. They also handle corporate actions (like stock splits or mergers) and collect dividends, which are either reinvested or paid out to ETF holders.
