### Quantitative Modeling & Mathematical Context

> **Model Parameters:** No specific quantitative model details.

The manager targets stocks where current market sentiment is negative but fundamentals remain intact. This involves analyzing debt levels, asset backing, and cyclical recovery timelines. The primary risks are:
1. **Value Trap:** A stock that appears cheap but continues to deteriorate.
2. **Timing Risk:** The contrarian theme may take several years to play out, leading to prolonged underperformance against growth benchmarks.
