### 📋 Summary View
- **Strategic Context:** **Concept Summary:** An option contract that can only be exercised at the exact moment of expiration.
**Key Takeaway:** Simplified pricing dynamics with a fixed expiry date ($T$). The standard contract style on the NSE.
**Analogy:** A Treasury Bill that pays its principal amount only on its maturity date.
- **Quantitative Model:** No specific quantitative model details.
- **Risk/Opportunity:** No specific risk/opportunity details.

### 🔍 Detailed View
**Explanation:** On the NSE, all index options (Nifty, Bank Nifty, FinNifty) and stock options are European style. Since early exercise is not permitted, these options can be priced using closed-form analytical equations like the Black-Scholes formula, rather than complex numerical methods.
**Worked Example:** A trader holds a European-style Nifty CE expiring at 3:30 PM on the last Thursday of the month. If Nifty rises by 300 points on the Monday before expiry, the trader cannot exercise the option early to claim Nifty shares. Instead, they sell the option contract back to the market to lock in the premium gain.
