# Arbitrage Concept

> **Sub-Topic 3.2:** Arbitrage Strategies | **Chapter:** Derivates Futures | **Level:** Beginner

--- 

### 📝 Source Institutional Content
> **Reference:** Page 12
> **Content:** Zero-investment strategy to make riskless profit by exploiting mispricing.

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept:** Arbitrage is a trading strategy where you make a guaranteed, risk-free profit without using any of your own money, by exploiting a price difference between two markets.
- **Analogy:** If a shop is selling a book for ₹100, and another shop next door is buying that same book for ₹120, you borrow ₹100 from a friend, buy the book, sell it next door, return the ₹100, and keep ₹20. You used zero personal capital and made a risk-free profit.
- **Key Takeaway:** Arbitrage is a risk-free, zero-net-investment strategy that exploits pricing errors.

### 🔍 Detailed View
In financial markets, arbitrageurs look for cases where a futures contract is trading away from its fair theoretical price. They execute offsetting trades in the cash and futures markets simultaneously. By locking in the prices, they guarantee a profit regardless of whether the market goes up or down.
