# Higher Leverage with Restricted Loss

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept Summary:** Options provide asymmetric leverage, magnifying percentage gains while strictly capping potential losses to the initial premium paid.
- **Formula/Rule:** Standard guidelines apply.
- **Application Context:** No specific application context available.

### 🔍 Detailed View
**Explanation:** Leverage is calculated as Contract Value divided by Premium Paid. Because the denominator (premium) is small, the leverage ratio is extremely high. If the stock moves favorably, the percentage return on investment (ROI) is far higher than holding the stock or future.
**Worked Example:** Stock is at ₹500. Call Option strike ₹500 costs ₹10.
If the stock goes to ₹550 (+10% move): Option value becomes at least ₹50. Your ROI is $\frac{50 - 10}{10} = 400\%$.
If the stock falls to ₹450 (-10% move): Your loss is capped at 100% (₹10), whereas a future trader would lose the full ₹50.
