# Put Option

> **Sub-Topic 2.1.1:** Core Definitions | **Chapter:** Derivatives Options | **Level:** Beginner

--- 

### 📝 Source Institutional Content
> **Reference:** Page 2
> **Content:** Put Option: Right to sell underlying security.

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept:** A Put Option is a contract that gives you the right to *sell* a stock at a locked price. You buy a Put Option when you expect the stock price to fall.
- **Analogy:** Buying mobile phone insurance. If the phone breaks (stock crashes), the insurance company pays you its locked full value.
- **Key Takeaway:** Put = Right to Sell (expecting prices to go down).

### 🔍 Detailed View
**Explanation:** A Put Option is like a price guarantee for selling. It protects you from falling prices. If you own a stock and buy a Put Option, you have guaranteed that you can sell your stock at the strike price, even if the actual market price crashes to zero.
**Worked Example:** You buy a Put Option for SBI shares with a strike price of ₹800 by paying a ₹30 premium. If SBI shares crash to ₹700, you can still sell them at ₹800. Your option is worth ₹100, giving you a net profit of ₹70 (₹100 gain - ₹30 premium paid).
