# Flexi-Cap Funds

> **Sub-Topic 3.2.2:** Equity Funds | **Chapter:** Mutual Funds | **Level:** Beginner

--- 

### 📝 Source Institutional Content
> **Reference:** Page 4
> **Content:** Flexi-Cap: No fixed allocation, min 65% in equity.

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept:** Equity funds where the manager has full freedom to invest any percentage of money in large, mid, or small companies.
- **Analogy:** A buffet plate where you can choose to eat only desserts or only starters, depending on what looks best.
- **Key Takeaway:** Offers maximum flexibility to the fund manager to adapt to market conditions.

### 🔍 Detailed View
Unlike multi-cap funds, Flexi-Cap funds have no minimum limits for large, mid, or small companies. The only rule is that at least 65% of the total money must be in equities. If the manager feels the market is risky, they can put 90% in safe large companies. If they feel optimistic, they can move money into small-cap stocks.
