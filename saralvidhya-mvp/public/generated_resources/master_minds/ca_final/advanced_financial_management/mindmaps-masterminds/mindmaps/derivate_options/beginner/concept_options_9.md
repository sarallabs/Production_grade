# Impact

> **Sub-Topic 2.1.3:** Futures vs. Options | **Chapter:** Derivatives Options | **Level:** Beginner

--- 

### 📝 Source Institutional Content
> **Reference:** Page 1
> **Content:** Impact: Futures (Symmetrical) vs. Options (Asymmetrical).

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept:** Futures have symmetrical risk-return (profits and losses rise and fall equally). Options have asymmetrical profiles (limited loss for buyer, unlimited profit).
- **Analogy:** Futures is like riding a see-saw (one goes up, other goes down equally). Options buyer is like wearing a safety harness while bungee jumping.
- **Key Takeaway:** Futures = Equal upside and downside. Options = Protected downside for the buyer.

### 🔍 Detailed View
**Explanation:** Symmetrical means that every ₹1 move in the stock results in a ₹1 gain or loss. Asymmetrical means that the option buyer's loss stops at the premium paid, but their profits can grow as long as the stock price keeps moving in their favor.
**Worked Example:** If a stock goes from ₹100 to ₹150, both the Future buyer and Option buyer make profit. If the stock crashes from ₹100 to ₹30, the Future buyer loses ₹70, but the Option buyer (who paid ₹5 premium) only loses ₹5.
