### Quantitative Modeling & Mathematical Context

> **Model Parameters:** No specific quantitative model details.

**Explanation:** Delta represents the equivalent share exposure of the option. An ATM option has a Delta of approximately 0.50 (Calls) or -0.50 (Puts). Deep ITM options approach a Delta of 1.00 (moving 1:1 with the stock), while deep OTM options approach 0.00.
**Worked Example:** You hold 10 lots of Nifty Call Options (250 units) with a Delta of 0.40. Your position behaves like you own 100 shares of Nifty ($250 \times 0.40 = 100$ shares). If Nifty rises by ₹50, your portfolio value increases by ₹5,000 ($100 \times \text{₹}50$).
