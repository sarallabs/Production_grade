### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept Summary:** Spot assets can be held indefinitely, while derivatives have finite life spans and must be settled or rolled over at contract expiration.
- **Formula/Rule:** Holding Period: Cash (Indefinite) vs. Derivatives (Must settle at contract end).
- **Application Context:** Traded on Indian exchanges (NSE/BSE/MCX) with standard contract specifications (Nifty lot size of 25, Bank Nifty lot size of 15).

### 🔍 Detailed View
The difference in holding periods impacts investment strategy:
**Cash Market**: Investors can adopt a buy-and-hold strategy. They do not face time decay or mandatory liquidation unless the company is delisted.
**Derivatives Market**: All contracts have an **Expiry Date**. For Indian equity derivatives, contracts are available for three concurrent months (near-month, next-month, and far-month). At expiry, the contract must be settled. To maintain a position, a trader must execute a **Rollover**, which involves closing the expiring contract and opening a new contract in the subsequent month, incurring transaction costs.
