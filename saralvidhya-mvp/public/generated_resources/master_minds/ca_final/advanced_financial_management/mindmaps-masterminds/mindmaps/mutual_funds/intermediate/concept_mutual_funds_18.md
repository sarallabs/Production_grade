### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept Summary:** Debt funds investing a minimum of 80% of assets in sovereign government securities.
- **Formula/Rule:** Standard guidelines apply.
- **Application Context:** No specific application context available.

### 🔍 Detailed View
Gilt funds invest in Central and State Government securities. They have no credit risk. However, they hold long-maturity government bonds, which gives them a high **Macaulay Duration**. A high duration means their NAV is highly volatile when the RBI changes the repo rate.
