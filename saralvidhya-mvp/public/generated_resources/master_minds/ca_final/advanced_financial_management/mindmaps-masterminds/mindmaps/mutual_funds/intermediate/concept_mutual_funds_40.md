- **Application Context:** No specific application context available.

### 🔍 Detailed View
Tax advantage of SWP over IDCW or Debt FD interest:
When you withdraw ₹10,000 via SWP, it is not treated as pure income. It is treated as a redemption of units. The tax is calculated only on the capital gains portion of that ₹10,000.
For example: If you redeem 100 units at NAV ₹100 (Total ₹10,000) that you bought at NAV ₹90, the cost price was ₹9,000. The taxable capital gain is only ₹1,000. The remaining ₹9,000 is tax-free return of capital.
