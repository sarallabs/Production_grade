# Arbitrage Funds

> **Sub-Topic 3.2.4:** Specialized Strategies | **Chapter:** Mutual Funds | **Level:** Beginner

--- 

### 📝 Source Institutional Content
> **Reference:** Page 6
> **Content:** Arbitrage Funds: Exploit mispricing between cash and futures markets. Low risk, tax-efficient (equity taxation).

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept:** Funds that buy a stock in one market (cash market) and sell it immediately in another market (futures market) to lock in a tiny, risk-free price difference.
- **Analogy:** Buying onions in a village wholesale market for ₹20/kg and selling them instantly in the city market for ₹22/kg.
- **Key Takeaway:** Safe like a debt fund but taxed like an equity fund (lower tax).

### 🔍 Detailed View
Arbitrage funds exploit short-term price differences. Since buy and sell orders are placed simultaneously, there is zero market risk. Because they invest more than 65% in equity-related arbitrage, they enjoy low equity tax rates, making them attractive for short-term investing.
