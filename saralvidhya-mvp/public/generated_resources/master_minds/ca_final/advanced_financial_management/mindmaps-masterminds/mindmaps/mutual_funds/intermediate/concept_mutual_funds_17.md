### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept Summary:** Debt funds investing in corporate debentures, bonds, and commercial paper to earn interest yield (accrual) and capital gains (trading).
- **Formula/Rule:** Standard guidelines apply.
- **Application Context:** No specific application context available.

### 🔍 Detailed View
Bond funds manage two main risks:
1. **Credit Risk:** The risk of default by the issuer. Managed by tracking credit ratings (AAA down to D).
2. **Interest Rate Risk:** Bond prices move inversely to interest rates. When interest rates fall, bond prices rise, increasing the fund's NAV.
