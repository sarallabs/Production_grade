### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept Summary:** Options involve paying an upfront, non-refundable premium. Futures do not have premiums; they use refundable margin deposits.
- **Formula/Rule:** Standard guidelines apply.
- **Application Context:** No specific application context available.

### 🔍 Detailed View
**Explanation:** The premium is the market price of the option contract, paid from buyer to seller. The margin is a risk collateral held by the exchange clearing house (like NSCCL) to prevent default risk. Future traders pay margin; option writers also pay margin, but option buyers only pay the premium.
**Worked Example:** To write (sell) a Nifty option, you must block ~₹1,20,000 in margin. To buy the same option, you only need to pay the premium (e.g., ₹2,500), and no margin is blocked.
