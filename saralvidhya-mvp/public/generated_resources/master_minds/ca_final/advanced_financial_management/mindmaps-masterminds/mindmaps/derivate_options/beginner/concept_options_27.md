# No Dividends During Option Life

> **Sub-Topic 2.5.1:** Assumptions | **Chapter:** Derivatives Options | **Level:** Beginner

--- 

### 📝 Source Institutional Content
> **Reference:** Page 3
> **Content:** No dividends during option life.

--- 

### 📋 Summary View
- **Concept:** The model assumes that the company will not pay any cash dividends to its shareholders while the option contract is active.
- **Analogy:** Assuming a rental car's value won't drop because the owner took out some parts during your rental period.
- **Key Takeaway:** BSM assumes stock prices are not affected by dividend payouts.

### 🔍 Detailed View
**Explanation:** In reality, when a company pays a dividend, its stock price drops by the dividend amount on the ex-dividend date. Since the BSM formula assumes no dividends are paid, it can misprice options for companies that pay high dividends (like ITC or Coal India).
**Worked Example:** If ITC announces a ₹10 dividend, the stock price will drop by ₹10 on the dividend day. The BSM model doesn't account for this drop, so it would overvalue Call options on ITC.
